FROM REQUEST TO REPAIR

What happens after you book?

A useful repair visit starts with the right information. Here is what to expect as your enquiry moves from a request to an agreed job.

01

Send your request

Enter the name and mobile number you want us to use. Choose your machine type, or “No Idea” if you are unsure. Keep the phone available for the follow-up.

02

Confirm the details

Discuss your locality, the fault, machine access and an available visit. Confirm the applicable visit or inspection charge and the address before the technician travels.

03

Review the assessment

The technician checks the reported problem and explains the proposed work. Ask what needs attention, which parts are involved and the complete repair price.

04

Approve and check

Approve the agreed work before it begins. At handover, ask what was tested, retain the service record and confirm the warranty for any new parts supplied.

KNOW THE TERMS

Repair charges, clearly explained

There is no single repair price for every “washing machine not working” enquiry. A blocked drain, a damaged hose, a lock fault and an electronic problem can involve different work and parts. The assessment establishes what is needed.

  • Before the visit: confirm the visit or inspection amount for your location.
  • Before repair: ask for the complete price and the part or work it covers.
  • Before off-site work: agree any transport arrangements, charges and expected return.
  • After repair: keep the invoice or service record and covered-part details.

DESCRIBE WHAT YOU NOTICE

What problem does your washing machine have?

You do not need to diagnose the fault before booking. Explain what happens, when it started and whether it happens on every cycle. These details help the technician focus the assessment.

Not draining

Water remains inside after a wash, the cycle stops before spin, or clothes come out sitting in water. Tell us whether the machine is completely full or has only a small amount left.

Read the repair guide →

Not spinning

The washer may wash normally but fail to reach its spin stage. Mention whether it drains first, whether the drum moves at all and whether the issue affects every load.

Read the repair guide →

Water leaking

Describe where you first see water: at the door, behind the machine, under the cabinet or near a hose. A clear observation helps the assessment without assuming which part needs replacement.

Read the repair guide →

Machine not starting

Tell us whether the display is dark, the buttons respond or the door locks without a cycle starting. A no-power symptom and a machine that powers on but will not run need different checks.

Read the repair guide →

Noise or vibration

Mention the stage where the noise happens, whether the washer was recently moved and whether the cabinet itself shakes. An installation issue and an internal mechanical fault need different work.

Read the repair guide →

Door or lid problems

Describe whether the door will not close, will not lock or remains locked after a cycle. Do not force the latch. Share any error shown and whether water is still inside.

Read the repair guide →
If there is a burning smell, electric shock or water near electrical parts, stop using the machine. Avoid touching wet electrical equipment. Do not restart it just to demonstrate the fault.

MAKE THE VISIT USEFUL

A little preparation helps

Keep a note of the symptom, error message and when the problem occurs. A photo of the model label helps identify the correct version of your washer. Tell the team if the fault began after moving the appliance, a plumbing change or an earlier repair.

Arrange safe access to the machine and make sure the person approving the work can be reached. Do not dismantle covers or force a locked door before the visit. If you are booking for a tenant, family member or another property, provide the on-site contact during the follow-up.

Choose the service that matches the need

A repair request concerns a fault. General service, filter cleaning, descaling and installation are different jobs. Describe the result you need so the scope can be agreed.

BEFORE YOUR VISIT

Booking questions, answered

Practical answers about washing machine service in Bangalore, charges, machine details and arranging the visit.

Request a repair visit
How do I book washing machine repair near me in Bangalore?

Enter your name, phone number and machine type in the form. Keep your Bangalore locality, PIN code, model details and a description of the fault ready for the follow-up conversation. The team confirms the visit details and applicable charges with you.

What if I do not know whether my machine is front load or top load?

Choose “No Idea” in the form. A front-load machine has a door on its front, while a top-load machine opens from above. You can share a model-label photo or describe the machine when the team contacts you. There is no need to guess a failed part.

What is the visiting charge if I do not proceed with repair?

A ₹200 visiting charge applies if repair is not carried out. Inspection charges depend on location. Ask the team to confirm the applicable amount and how it relates to any repair quote before the visit.

Can I request a same-day washing machine repair visit?

Yes, ask about the next available slot for your Bangalore address. Same-day availability depends on the locality and technician schedule. Completing the repair also depends on the diagnosis, required parts and access to the machine.

Which washing machine brands can I book an enquiry for?

You can enquire about LG, Samsung, IFB, Bosch, Whirlpool, Haier, Godrej, Panasonic and other models. Provide the full model number so support and replacement-part availability can be checked. FixMyWasher is an independent service and does not claim manufacturer authorisation.

Does the one-year warranty cover the whole washing machine?

The one-year warranty applies to new parts supplied and replaced by FixMyWasher. It is not a whole-machine warranty. Keep the service record and confirm the covered part, start date and relevant follow-up terms before approving the work.

Should I book general service or a repair?

If a machine has a specific fault, such as not draining, not spinning, leaking or stopping with an error, describe that fault when booking. A routine cleaning or descaling request has a different scope and should not be assumed to resolve a failed component.

What details should I keep ready for the technician?

Have the brand, full model number, symptom and any error code ready. Note when the problem happens and whether the machine was recently moved or repaired. Share your exact locality and access information, including stairs, a stacked machine or a fitted cabinet.

Can repairs be completed at my home?

Many enquiries begin with an assessment at the customer’s address. The diagnosis and access determine which work can be completed there. If workshop repair or transport is proposed, agree the reason, charges, expected return and handling arrangements first.

How do I change an existing booking or ask about a previous repair?

Call or WhatsApp 91641 51568 and give the booking name, phone number and service details. For a previous repair, have the invoice or service record ready. That helps the team connect your question with the correct job.
