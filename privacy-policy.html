Privacy policy

How information from service enquiries and visits is used.

Updated 11 October 2026

On this pageInformationHow we use itServices we useWebsite analyticsRetention and requestsContact us

FixMyWasher handles the details you provide when you contact us about washing machine repair in Bangalore. This page explains the information involved in a service enquiry and how you can ask questions about it.

Information we receive

When you call, use WhatsApp, email or submit our contact form, you may give us your name, phone number, optional email address, Bangalore locality or service address, and a description of your appliance or problem. You may also send a model number, an error message or photos. If you arrange a visit, the service conversation and any repair record may contain the work discussed, a quotation, the parts supplied and payment or invoice details.

Please send only details needed for your enquiry. Avoid including passwords, card numbers or unrelated personal information in your message.

How we use it

We use the details to reply, check service availability, arrange a visit, assess the appliance, prepare a quotation, carry out approved work, keep service records and respond to questions about invoices or the applicable parts warranty. We may also use service feedback to understand and improve the experience.

Services involved in communication

Details needed for a visit may be shared with the technician assigned to the job. The contact form sends the information you enter through FormSubmit to our service inbox. If you choose WhatsApp, your message is handled on WhatsApp; email and telephone enquiries use their respective providers. Those services process information under their own terms and privacy practices.

We may disclose information where required by applicable law.

Website analytics and cookies

Our pages include Google Analytics to understand visits and use of the site. It may collect page and device information and use cookies or similar identifiers according to its settings. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. The site may still need some browser functions for navigation and forms.

We do not put your name, phone number or enquiry message into the page address for analytics. If you click an external service such as WhatsApp, that service may collect data under its own policy.

Keeping information and your requests

We keep enquiry and service records for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, handle warranty or billing questions, and meet applicable record requirements. To ask what information we hold about your enquiry, request a correction, or discuss deletion, contact us with enough detail to identify the relevant request. We will review it and respond subject to the information we need to retain.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page as our website or service practices change. The date above identifies this version.

Contact us

For a privacy question or request, email help@fixmywasher.in or call 91641 51568. For service enquiries, see the contact page.
