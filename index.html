Start with the symptom

A problem with your washer? Start here.

Choose the symptom you recognise. You do not need to know which part has failed—we can start with what you see, hear or notice.

Water not draining

What to tell us

Water left in the drum, a stopped cycle or clothes still sitting in water? Share when the machine stops and any error shown. A drainage fault needs assessment before choosing a pump.

Drainage problem guide →

Drum not spinning

What to tell us

Tell us whether the drum turns during washing and whether water drains first. A wet load can have several causes; it does not by itself establish a motor fault.

Spinning problem guide →

Water leaking

What to tell us

Note whether the leak appears during filling, washing or draining, and where you first see it. Stop using the machine if water is reaching a socket or electrical parts.

Leak assessment guide →

Machine not starting

What to tell us

Describe whether the display is blank, the controls respond or the door fails to lock. Repeated resets will not resolve an underlying electrical or component fault.

No-start problem guide →

Noise or strong vibration

What to tell us

A new knocking, scraping or grinding sound deserves a check. Tell us if the washer was recently moved, installed or overloaded, and stop the cycle if it moves violently.

Noise and vibration guide →

Door locked or error displayed

What to tell us

Send the exact error and full model number. Codes differ between models. Do not force the door or bypass a safety lock to remove clothes.

Locked-door guide →

Stop using the machine if there is smoke, a burning smell, electric shock, repeated tripping or water near electrics. Do not touch wet plugs, force a locked door or bypass a safety device.

Know what you are approving

Washing machine repair charges, explained

There is no reliable single repair price for every machine. The fault, exact model, required part and access all affect the work. Ask for the complete quotation after assessment, before repair begins.

  • Diagnosis: what was found and what needs repair, cleaning or replacement.
  • Price: parts, labour, visit or inspection charges, and any agreed transport or other costs.
  • Part choice: the compatible part, its availability and whether the proposed part is original or an alternative.
  • Completion checks: what will be tested and how the repaired fault will be demonstrated.

How do I book washing machine repair in Bangalore?

Use the booking page, call 8970794240 or send a WhatsApp enquiry with your locality, machine type and problem. Include the brand, model and error code if available. A visit is confirmed after the team replies with availability and the applicable visit or inspection charge.

What is the washing machine repair visiting charge?

A ₹200 visiting charge applies if repair is not carried out. Inspection charges depend on your location. Confirm the applicable visit or inspection amount and how it is included in the repair quotation before the visit. The final repair price depends on the diagnosis, parts, labour and access.

Do you provide same-day washing machine service?

Ask the team about the next available slot for your Bangalore address. Same-day visits depend on technician availability and location; repair completion also depends on the fault and parts. An enquiry does not guarantee a same-day visit or completed repair.

What warranty does FixMyWasher provide?

FixMyWasher provides a one-year warranty on new parts supplied and replaced by the service. This is not a whole-machine warranty. Keep the service record and confirm the covered part, start date, exclusions and any follow-up visit or labour terms when approving the work.

Which washing machine brands and types can I enquire about?

You can enquire about front-load, top-load and semi-automatic washing machines, including LG, Samsung, IFB, Bosch, Whirlpool, Haier, Godrej and Panasonic. Share the full model number to confirm support and part availability. Washer-dryers, imported models and specialist installations need a model-specific scope check.

Are you an authorised service centre for these brands?

FixMyWasher is an independent washing machine repair service. Brand names identify the machines customers enquire about; they do not imply manufacturer authorisation. If the machine is under a manufacturer warranty or service plan, check that provider’s process before approving independent work.

Can my washing machine be repaired at home?

The first assessment can be arranged at your Bangalore address after availability is confirmed. Whether the work can be completed there depends on the fault, machine access and required repair. If off-site work is proposed, agree the reason, transport arrangements, charges and expected return before the machine is moved.

Do you cover my locality in Bangalore?

Send your locality, PIN code and a nearby landmark or apartment name. Area guides are available for places including Whitefield, Marathahalli, HSR Layout, Electronic City, Jayanagar, Yelahanka and Rajajinagar. Confirm availability for your exact address before booking; a guide is not a promise of an immediate visit.

Should I repair or replace an older washing machine?

Compare the complete repair quote with the machine’s age, condition, previous repairs and the cost of a suitable replacement. Check part availability and warranty as well as the price. A large drum or electronic repair needs a different decision from a small hose or lock repair.

What should I do before the technician arrives?

Keep the model details, error message and a short description of the fault ready. Clear safe access around the washer without dismantling it. If there is smoke, a burning smell, electric shock, repeated tripping or water near electrics, stop using the appliance and avoid touching wet electrical equipment. Do not reconnect it simply to demonstrate the fault.
