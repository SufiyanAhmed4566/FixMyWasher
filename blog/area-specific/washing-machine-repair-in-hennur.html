Use the model suffix, not just the capacity

Send the complete model from a safely accessible record. Mention front-load, top-load or washer-dryer format and any imported version. If the label is inaccessible, use an existing invoice or photo rather than tilting the machine or removing panels to find it.

Models with similar names can use different components or revisions. Ask the intended provider to confirm the match before supply. A general brand-support statement does not establish that every board, motor or door assembly is available for the installed variant.

Describe the first failed stage before naming a component

Explain whether the washer fails to fill, stops during wash, retains water or does not spin. Include the exact message and earlier work. These details focus assessment without turning the customer’s guess into a confirmed diagnosis.

If another provider recommended a part, share the finding and written proposal rather than only its name. Ask what can be independently confirmed. A second recommendation should clarify the evidence and scope, not merely replace one unexplained component list with another.

Distinguish new supply from repair of an existing assembly

Ask whether the offer repairs the current part or supplies a replacement, and what condition that replacement is in. The word genuine should be supported by a clear item description and supply terms; it does not by itself explain the work.

Compare the complete installed amount and written coverage for the actual offer. New parts supplied by FixMyWasher have the stated one-year warranty under written terms. Existing-part repair does not automatically receive the same arrangement, and neither implies whole-machine coverage.

Keep sourcing promises specific and reviewable

Ask whether the required item has been confirmed or is still being checked. If supply depends on a later confirmation, keep the repair date and amount provisional where appropriate. A first visit cannot guarantee that every possible part is already available.

If the proposed component or price changes, request the reason and revised total before approval. Ask what happens if the item cannot be matched and what condition the appliance is currently in. An unfinished sourcing enquiry should not be labelled a completed repair.

Coordinate the part with safe access and the resident

Mention a fixed cabinet, stacked dryer or restricted utility space before ordering work. The correct component still needs safe fitting access. Agree any additional handling and the person responsible rather than discovering the restriction after supply is arranged.

For a return visit, confirm the accepted gate, resident availability, approval contact and any additional charge. Keep the earlier finding and quotation available to the attendee. Appointment confirmation should follow the actual supply and access status.

Retain the installed item and relevant outcome checks

At completion, ask which part was fitted and which checks addressed the original complaint. A supplied item on an invoice should match the actual work and written terms. A machine powering on is not a complete result for every drain, leak or spin repair.

Keep any remaining limitation or property dependency with the record. If a symptom later returns, the provider can compare the current sequence with the earlier finding and item. Clear documentation is more useful than an unsupported promise that all future faults are covered.

A short checklist before you confirm the visit

Prepare the actual locality and entrance, complete model, first failed stage and earlier work. Ask for component condition, compatibility, supply status and installed total. Add access restrictions and the contact approving any revised proposal.

Keep the machine in its normal position and leave access to the front clear. Do not dismantle it or disconnect fixed wiring for an appointment. Tell us in advance if it is stacked, inside cabinetry or difficult to access. Share an existing photograph of the display or model label only when it is safely accessible.

For an active leak near electricity, a burning smell, smoke, shock or damaged wiring, stop using the appliance and keep away from the hazard. Operate an isolation control only if it is safely accessible. Do not restart it just to record a video. A routine booking does not replace urgent assistance for an unsafe situation.

New parts, compatibility and your one-year warranty

New parts supplied by FixMyWasher carry a one-year warranty. The invoice should identify the covered component and written terms. This is not automatically a warranty on the entire washing machine, every future fault or the property installation.

Ask whether a proposed component is manufacturer-original, a compatible alternative or repair of the existing assembly. These are different offers. Confirm the full model match, condition, sourcing and installed price before approving it. A “universal” description alone does not establish suitability.

Labour, workmanship, repairs to existing components and revisit terms need separate confirmation. Keep the invoice and contact the provider with the original job details if a concern returns. Where manufacturer warranty or a service plan may still apply, check that route before authorising independent work.

Prepare access without opening or moving the machine

Keep the model from your invoice or an existing safe label photograph, the complete error message and any previous repair record ready. Clear ordinary access only where it is safe. Tell us about stairs, a stacked installation, a tight enclosure or a locked utility area before the appointment.

Do not dismantle panels, force a locked door, tilt a heavy washer or recreate a dangerous symptom for a video. If there is a shock, smoke, burning, severe movement or water near electrics, stop use and keep away; arrange the appropriate urgent help before a routine appliance visit. Use an isolation control only if it is safely accessible without approaching the hazard.

Safe inspection may require a qualified electrician, plumber or building team first. A washing-machine booking does not automatically include property wiring, earthing or plumbing correction.
