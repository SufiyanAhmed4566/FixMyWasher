The E-Commerce Installation Problem

Lloyd sells heavily through Flipkart, Amazon and other online channels, often at prices that undercut in-store competitors. The trade-off most buyers don't realise until later: online delivery frequently means the machine arrives at your door without a proper professional installation visit, unlike a showroom purchase where installation is typically bundled in. This single fact explains a huge share of the "faults" we get called out for on Lloyd machines under three months old.

Transit Bolts Never Removed

  • By far the most common issue on new online-purchased Lloyd machines
  • Causes violent vibration and banging noise during spin
  • Delivery personnel are couriers, not installation technicians, and often skip this step
  • A five-minute fix once identified, but frequently misdiagnosed as a motor fault

Unlevelled Placement

  • No professional levelling check on self-assembled or courier-delivered units
  • Causes excessive noise and accelerates motor mount wear over time
  • Particularly common in new-build Bangalore apartments with uneven tile flooring

DIY Plumbing Connections

  • Inlet and outlet hoses self-connected without proper sealing
  • Slow leaks that go unnoticed until they damage flooring or nearby fittings
  • Outlet hose height not meeting the minimum required for correct drainage

Genuine PCB & Component Faults

  • Relay-level PCB faults preventing program advancement
  • Water level sensor occasionally arriving out of factory calibration
  • Inlet valve solenoid faults, more often manufacturing variance than wear
  • Genuine faults do happen, but installation issues outnumber them in our data

Why Lloyd Parts Sourcing Works Differently

Because Lloyd operates under the larger Havells Group umbrella alongside the company's established electricals, fans and appliance businesses, its washing machine parts distribution leans on infrastructure built for a much broader product range rather than a dedicated washing machine service network the way IFB or LG have built over decades. In practice, this means core electrical components like PCB relays and wiring harnesses are often easier to source quickly than washing-machine-specific mechanical parts like drum bearings or gearbox assemblies, which are newer to Havells' supply chain. We maintain a working relationship with multiple supplier channels specifically to close this gap, since waiting on a single-source mechanical part is where Lloyd repairs most often stall for less-prepared technicians.

What Lloyd's Advertised Warranty Actually Covers

Lloyd listings on e-commerce platforms often headline aggressive warranty periods, sometimes years-long coverage on specific components like the motor. What that advertising rarely makes clear is the distinction between parts coverage and labour coverage, and the specific exclusions around installation-related faults, which as covered above make up a disproportionate share of early-life Lloyd issues. If your transit bolts were never removed and that caused vibration damage, that's generally treated as an installation issue, not a covered manufacturing defect, regardless of how the headline warranty number reads. Before assuming a fault is covered, check your actual purchase invoice and warranty card rather than the marketing copy on the listing page, and call us for an honest read on what's likely to be covered versus what will need a paid repair visit.

Hard Water as a Compounding Factor, Not the Primary Issue

Unlike some of the brands we service where hard water is the dominant local fault driver, Lloyd's machines are new enough to the Bangalore market that installation issues still outnumber water-quality-related faults in our data. That said, hard water does compound existing problems: a machine already vibrating from unremoved transit bolts wears its motor mounts and inlet valve seals faster under Bangalore's mineral-heavy borewell water than it would on softer municipal supply. We check both factors together on any Lloyd service call, since fixing the installation issue alone without addressing early scale buildup on the inlet valve just delays the next fault rather than preventing it.

What You Can Check Yourself Before Calling Us

If your Lloyd machine is new and vibrating badly, check the back and base of the machine for visible transit bolts or shipping brackets before booking a visit — this alone resolves a large share of new-machine complaints. If the machine won't start, confirm the door is fully latched and check the wall socket with another appliance. For any leak, trace it back to the inlet or outlet hose connection point first, since a loose fitting from self-installation is far more common than an internal fault.

What we don't recommend attempting yourself: opening the PCB housing, which risks static damage to a board that may already be difficult to source quickly, or disassembling the motor mount assembly, which needs correct torque and alignment to avoid creating a worse vibration problem than the one you started with.

Lloyd Installation Correction & Proper Setup

Given how many Lloyd machines arrive without a proper installation, this is genuinely one of our highest-value services for the brand. If you bought your Lloyd machine online and it was simply left at your door, booking a proper installation visit now is often cheaper than waiting for a preventable fault to develop and require a repair call later.

Getting the Most Out of a New Lloyd Purchase

If you've just bought a Lloyd machine online, the single best thing you can do is book a proper installation check within the first week rather than assuming self-setup was sufficient, even if the machine seems to be running fine. This catches transit bolts, levelling issues and hose sealing problems before they cause damage rather than after. Once installed correctly, avoid overloading the drum beyond its rated capacity, since Lloyd's simpler motor mounts have less tolerance for excess strain than the reinforced designs on some premium brands. If you're in a hard-water area like Whitefield or Electronic City, an occasional descaling rinse cycle protects the inlet valve and any heating element from early mineral buildup. Keep your purchase invoice and warranty card accessible, since Lloyd's coverage terms are specific about what's included and having documentation ready speeds up any warranty-related conversation.

This is almost always leftover transit bolts from an incomplete installation. Because many Lloyd machines are bought online and delivered without a professional installation visit, this is the single most common issue we see on machines under 3 months old.
