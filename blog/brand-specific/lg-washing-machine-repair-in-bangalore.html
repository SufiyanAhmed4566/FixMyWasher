Identify the LG appliance beyond capacity or a feature sticker

Copy the complete model from a safely accessible label, invoice or manual. Include the suffix and say whether it is a front loader, automatic top loader or a combined washer-dryer. Do not move or tilt a heavy appliance to photograph an inaccessible label.

Direct Drive, Smart Inverter and ThinQ describe particular technologies or capabilities; none is a complete part reference. LG’s India product pages show feature combinations that differ between models. A board or motor chosen only from “LG 7 kg” is not an adequately identified replacement.

Mention the purchase country if imported, approximate age and any previous part replacement. These details help establish the right documentation and support before sourcing begins.

An OE message directs attention to drainage, not automatically a new pump

LG’s Indian support material describes OE in connection with water not draining on applicable washers. Record the complete display and confirm its meaning for your exact model. Say whether water remains, whether a drain sound occurs and whether the outlet arrangement changed recently.

A sound does not prove that the pump works correctly, and silence alone does not prove it needs replacing. An assessment should distinguish a restriction, installation condition, operating interruption and component failure.

Use owner filter care only if the manual permits it and you can follow the isolation, cooling and retained-water precautions. Do not force a filter, open panels or use a household drain cleaner inside the washer.

A UE or repeated-spin complaint needs the load and installation history

LG provides Indian front-loader guidance for UE imbalance messages. The model-specific instructions matter; a code should not be converted directly into a suspension or motor quotation. Describe the items in the load and whether the same issue occurs with suitable everyday laundry.

Include a recent move, stand change or altered floor position. One large absorbent item is different evidence from repeated failure with permitted ordinary loads. Rearrange laundry only after normal safe access and according to the manual.

Stop for violent movement, banging or leakage. Do not hold the cabinet steady with your body or run another unsafe cycle to record a video. Assessment should address the conditions under which the fault actually occurs.

Ask what Direct Drive repair means on your quotation

If movement fails, first describe the sequence: does the washer accept Start, fill, attempt to turn and then stop, or does it never begin the wash? A broadly worded “Direct Drive problem” is not enough to select the work.

Ask whether the finding concerns the fitted motor, its control system, another connected component or a condition preventing movement. Do not accept a belt-related explanation for a model without that arrangement; the exact design must be confirmed.

A replacement quote should name the relevant component and any configuration or associated work. Repairing an existing electronic assembly, fitting a compatible replacement and supplying a manufacturer-original part are different offers and should be described accurately.

Keep a locked front door and retained water in the same enquiry

Say whether the washer finished normally, stopped during drainage or has a visibly damaged handle. A door that remains locked while the machine is not in a safe opening condition needs a different assessment from a broken physical fitting.

Do not force the handle, insert a tool around the opening or use a release method copied from another LG model. Follow the correct owner instructions only where access is clearly safe; hot water and a full tub need special care.

If a lock or pump is proposed, ask which finding supports it. The completion check should cover the relevant water-removal and release behaviour, rather than merely showing that the replacement clicks when operated.

Separate ThinQ connectivity from local washing operation

For a supported connected model, explain whether the washer completes a normal programme from its own controls. An app connection problem alongside normal local operation is a different starting point from a machine that cannot wash by either method.

Use LG’s applicable setup and support instructions for the feature. Do not share your Wi-Fi password, account password or one-time code with a repair enquiry. An app screenshot should exclude unrelated personal information.

Diagnostic information may help describe a concern, but it does not guarantee identification of the failed part. Before approving a board replacement, ask how a connection or setup issue was distinguished from a confirmed appliance control fault.

Review LG manufacturer coverage before independent work

LG’s India warranty information distinguishes product and component coverage and states conditions for extended component terms. Check the documents for your own purchase and model rather than treating a long motor promise as free coverage for every no-spin repair.

Ask the manufacturer which part, labour, visit and other charges apply to your case. FixMyWasher’s independent service is a separate route, and its one-year warranty applies to new parts it supplies under the stated terms.

If comparing quotations, compare the same scope. A motor-only part price and an installed repair total including another failed assembly cannot be judged as equivalent just because both are described as LG repair.

Prepare the enquiry and agree a useful handover

An illustrative message is: “LG automatic front load, model shown on the attached label, HSR Layout. Washing starts but it stops before spin with water inside and the attached message. It has not recently moved.” Replace the example with the actual sequence.

Mention a combined drying function only if fitted and say whether its failure is separate from washing. Include counter clearance, stacked installation or building access so the visit can be planned without moving the washer unsafely.

After work, ask which original condition was reproduced and what remains for normal observation. Keep the model, finding, supplied-part details and written terms so a recurrence can be compared with the same fault.

Safe checks before arranging a visit

  • Record the exact display text and the stage: filling, washing, draining, spinning or completion. Take a clear panel photo if safely accessible.
  • Check ordinary settings against the model manual: selected programme, pause, delayed start and control lock. Do not enter hidden service menus.
  • Confirm a known water-supply interruption and look for a visible hose kink without dragging the washer or disconnecting plumbing.
  • Check suitable laundry quantity and whether fabric is trapped at the opening. Rearrange items only through normal safe access after movement stops.
  • For accessible filter care, follow the exact manual, isolate power safely, let hot water cool and prepare for retained water. Stop if access is unclear or a fitting is stuck.

Stop using the machine if there is a shock, burning smell, sparking, repeated electrical tripping, violent movement or water near electrical fittings. Do not touch wet plugs or sockets; have the supply isolated safely and arrange appropriate help. Do not keep testing the appliance to record a better video.

Do not remove panels, test live wiring, bypass a door or lid interlock, or force a locked opening. A house-supply or socket problem may need a qualified electrician in addition to an appliance technician.

What a useful diagnosis should explain

A repair assessment should connect your reported symptom to a finding on the actual machine. Ask the technician to identify the affected system, explain what has been ruled out and describe any remaining uncertainty. The goal is a defined repair scope that you can approve.

A no-spin complaint, for example, should account for drainage, load and operating state before a motor is condemned. A dead panel should not automatically be called a failed main board. If the problem is intermittent, a fault may not appear during a brief inspection; agree what evidence and further observation are needed.

Ask before approving extra dismantling, workshop transport or another visit. Clarify what happens if the assessment finds that a compatible part is unavailable or the repair is not economical. No single visit, code photograph or phone conversation guarantees that every fault can be resolved immediately.

Read the inspection and diagnosis scope for the questions to settle before authorising work.

Choose a compatible part with a clear description

Ask for the proposed part to be matched to the full model and relevant revision. Similar appearance, kilogram capacity or connector shape does not establish interchangeability. An electronic board may also have configuration requirements that must be confirmed for the appliance.

A manufacturer-original part and a compatible third-party part are different descriptions. Request an accurate explanation of what is available, its condition, price and warranty before consenting. Parts availability and sourcing time must be confirmed; this page does not promise universal stock or a first-visit replacement.

If a repair to an existing component is proposed instead of replacement, ask what is being repaired and how the result will be checked. Keep the quotation and invoice so that a later enquiry can identify the actual work.

Understand original and compatible parts when reviewing the options.

Decide whether the proposed repair is worthwhile

Compare the written total with the machine’s condition, repair history, parts availability and a suitable replacement option. Age alone is not a complete decision rule. A machine with one contained fault presents a different choice from one with several major issues.

Ask whether the quotation addresses the reported cause and whether any additional defect remains. A cheaper repair that leaves a known leak or damaged drum unresolved may not meet your needs. Equally, a minor correctable issue does not automatically justify buying a new washer.

For a rented home, obtain the owner’s agreement before authorising work or replacement. If another person will approve payment, share the finding and full quote with them before the repair starts.
