Washing machine cycle taking too long? Find out where the delay starts

A long wash is not automatically a fault. The useful comparison is whether your washer now takes noticeably longer on the same programme with a similar load, or whether you have selected a programme designed to run longer.

Start with the programme and options, then identify whether the extra time occurs during filling, washing, rinsing, draining or spinning. This guide explains what those patterns mean and which checks are safe at home.

How long should a washing machine cycle take?

There is no single correct duration for every washer. Use the programme table for your exact model, including its load limits and selected options. A quick wash, an eco programme and a wash-and-dry programme perform different jobs.

Some eco settings deliberately extend washing time while reducing energy use. A long duration shown before you press Start may therefore be expected. Do not judge electricity consumption from elapsed time alone.

On many automatic machines, the displayed time is an estimate that changes with operating conditions. An adjusted estimate does not prove the timer is faulty. What needs attention is repeated failure to complete, a new unexplained delay or poor results alongside the extra time.

References: Bosch instructions describing Eco Perfect and Speed Perfect and Samsung explanation of estimated wash time. These describe particular features; check your own manual.

Compare like-for-like before deciding something has changed

A cotton cycle with extra rinses cannot be fairly compared with a short mixed-load wash. Before booking, note the following from a recent affected cycle:

  • Programme and options: temperature, prewash, soak, extra rinse, stain treatment and drying, where available.
  • Load: approximate amount, fabric type and any single bulky item.
  • Timing: the initial estimate, actual start time and when it finished or stopped progressing.
  • Results: whether clothes were clean, rinsed and spun as expected.
  • Changes: a different detergent, relocation, plumbing work or interrupted water supply.

If the same settings and suitable loads previously finished reliably, describe that change. You do not need to run repeated test washes to prove it.

Confirm what the display is counting. A Delay End or Ready-in setting may show time until a scheduled finish rather than active washing time. Check the symbols and instructions for your model.

Slow filling can add time more than once

A washer may need incoming water at several stages. If each fill is slow, the effect can accumulate across the wash and rinses.

Check whether your home or building had a water supply interruption. From a safe position, look for an obviously trapped hose and check a safely accessible supply tap. Avoid pulling a heavy washer out or disconnecting pressurised hoses.

Inlet restrictions and supply conditions need checking against the model requirements. Do not fit a booster pump or modify plumbing based only on a slow cycle.

Use the slow filling guide for this symptom. If water seems to leave as it enters, see water drains while filling; adding water manually will not correct that installation or operating problem.

Programme options and heating can extend the wash stage

Prewash, soak and certain stain-treatment settings can add stages or extend existing ones. Check which options are active, especially if someone else last used the machine.

On models that heat wash water internally, incoming temperature and the selected programme can affect the time needed. A longer warm wash alone does not establish that the heater is faulty.

If a familiar programme now stalls repeatedly, especially with an error message, report the pattern. Do not touch wiring, test the heater or bypass a sensor. Feeling the outside of the door is not a reliable heating diagnosis.

This check applies differently to machines without an internal heater. Use the exact model specification rather than assuming all front load or top load machines heat water in the same way.

Manufacturer context: LG guidance on cycle delays and LG explanation of water heating on applicable models.

Extra rinses and excess detergent can lengthen the programme

First check whether an extra-rinse option was selected. On some machines, excessive suds can also trigger additional rinsing.

Use detergent suitable for the washer and follow the product’s dosing guidance for the load, soil level and water conditions. More detergent does not automatically improve cleaning, and a concentrated product may require a different dose.

Do not add another dose when restarting a wash that already contains detergent. Avoid adding vinegar, dishwashing liquid or other chemicals as a shortcut for removing foam. Follow the washer manual’s recovery instructions.

If rinse delays continue with appropriate detergent use, record whether filling or draining is slow. The extra time may involve the water path rather than detergent alone.

Drainage and load balance affect the final stages

Water takes too long to leave

Incomplete drainage can interfere with normal progression into spin. Note whether water remains, drainage sounds continue or an error appears. A pump sound does not show whether water is moving properly.

Do not open a filter on a full or hot machine without the exact model’s safe draining procedure. See the not draining guide, or arrange help if access is unclear.

The washer repeatedly tries to balance the load

Some machines redistribute laundry before accelerating. A single heavy item or an unsuitable load can delay that process. Follow the manual and fabric care labels for the appropriate programme and load.

Only adjust laundry after motion has stopped and the machine permits safe opening. If suitable loads repeatedly fail to spin, arrange assessment. See the not spinning guide.

The machine moves or bangs severely

Stop the programme if safe rather than letting it keep trying. Do not hold the washer down. Describe any recent move or installation change and use the vibration guide.

A safe check sequence before calling

  1. Confirm the programme, options and display meaning in the manual.
  2. Record which stage takes longer and any error before resetting.
  3. Review the load and detergent used in the affected wash.
  4. Check known water interruptions and accessible hose routing visually.
  5. Follow only the model’s approved pause, drain or restart instructions.
  6. If the delay repeats, share these observations and arrange diagnosis.
Stop for electrical or water warning signs. Burning smells, smoke, sparks, a hot or damaged plug, repeated circuit trips or water near electrical connections require stopping use. Avoid wet switches and plugs; have power isolated safely from a dry location by someone competent. Do not repeatedly reset a tripped circuit.

Do not remove panels, bypass a door lock or keep running an unsafe machine to record a demonstration.

How to choose a shorter wash without compromising the load

Use a quick programme only for the load size, fabric and soil level specified in the manual. The machine’s advertised maximum capacity may not apply to its shortest programme.

Select only the options the laundry needs. Keep required rinsing and care-label temperatures, and do not interrupt the final stages simply because the estimate has changed.

For a washer-dryer, compare wash-only time with wash-only time. Drying adds another process and may have a different permitted load. A combined programme should not be compared with an ordinary wash.

On a semi-automatic machine, soaking, transferring clothes and separately selected wash or spin timers also affect total laundry time. That is different from an automatic countdown repeatedly extending.

When to arrange repair, and what the quote should cover

Arrange assessment for a persistent new delay on comparable loads and settings, repeated error messages, water remaining after the wash, failure to spin or a programme that never finishes.

A technician should establish which stage is delayed, review relevant fault information and check the supply, installation and affected systems as appropriate. A long cycle alone does not prove that a pump, heater or circuit board needs replacing.

  • Confirm the visit or inspection charge before attendance.
  • Ask what applies if you decline the recommended work.
  • Request parts, labour and other charges separately.
  • Clarify whether household plumbing or electrical work is included.
  • Ask how successful operation will be checked and review any repair warranty terms.

If manufacturer warranty may apply, check its conditions before outside work. Costs depend on the confirmed cause, model, access and parts required.

Questions about long wash cycles

Is a three-hour wash always a fault?

No. Some programmes are designed to run for several hours. Compare with your model’s programme table, selected options and whether the wash finishes properly.

Why does the displayed time increase after starting?

On many automatic washers it is an estimate that can be adjusted as the programme progresses. A changing estimate alone does not identify a defect.

Does a longer eco wash use more electricity?

Duration alone cannot tell you energy consumption. Some eco programmes run longer while reducing energy use; check the model’s programme data.

Can slow water supply affect the rinse too?

Yes. If the programme needs more incoming water for rinsing, slow filling can add time at that stage as well.

Should I always use quick wash?

Use it only for the load and cleaning needs specified in the manual. Its load limit and intended use can differ from the main wash programmes.

Does a long warm wash mean the heater is broken?

No. The programme and operating conditions need checking. Repeated unusual delays or errors deserve diagnosis, rather than an assumed heater replacement.

What if the last minute never finishes?

Note whether the machine is draining, attempting spin or silent. Use the one-minute guide and arrange help if the failure repeats.

Should I keep cancelling and restarting?

No. Record the symptoms and follow the approved model procedure. If the problem returns, arrange assessment instead of repeatedly restarting.
