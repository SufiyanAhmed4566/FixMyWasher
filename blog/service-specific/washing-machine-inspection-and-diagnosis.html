What the inspection is intended to cover

The visit starts with the reported problem, model information and relevant history. The technician reviews the operating conditions, checks the affected areas where safe and accessible, and investigates the likely system using an appropriate model-specific process.

  • Understand the symptom, cycle stage and whether the issue is consistent or intermittent.
  • Check relevant programme settings, accessible installation conditions and visible damage.
  • Assess the implicated water, drainage, door, drive or control system as required.
  • Explain confirmed findings and any uncertainty that needs further investigation.
  • Discuss the proposed repair, part availability and applicable charges before approval.

The scope follows the fault. An enquiry about a leaking door does not automatically require dismantling the motor, and an error code does not automatically justify a control-board replacement. Any additional dismantling or off-site assessment should be discussed first.

What to do before requesting an inspection

Find the exact model from the receipt or a safely accessible label. Write down the full error message and the programme selected. Explain whether the problem started after moving the appliance, cleaning a filter, changing detergent or an earlier repair.

Keep an existing photo or video if one is available. Do not restart an unsafe washer to make a recording, clear all messages with repeated resets or remove parts before the visit.

If you have already completed a manufacturer-approved check, tell the technician what you did and what changed. That helps avoid treating an earlier maintenance action as an unexplained new fault.

When the machine should remain stopped

Stop use for smoke, sparks, a burning smell, repeated circuit trips, severe mechanical contact or water near electrical connections. Do not touch wet plugs or switches. Arrange safe power isolation from a dry location by someone competent when necessary.

If water keeps entering, close accessible supply taps safely. Keep a front-loader door closed while water could spill or may be hot, and never bypass a door or lid safety mechanism.

Inspection does not require you to reach behind a running machine, lift a heavy appliance or perform electrical tests. Let the technician know about trapped water or difficult access when booking so the assessment can be planned.

What a useful diagnosis should explain

Ask what was observed and how it relates to the reported symptom. A useful explanation might distinguish an obstructed drain path from a defective pump, or a door alignment problem from a failed interlock.

A code, sound or discoloured part can guide the checks without proving the complete cause. Where a part is recommended, ask why cleaning, an installation correction or repair of a related component would not resolve the issue.

You should also understand what remains uncertain. If a fault does not appear during the visit, the technician should describe the limits of the checks rather than claim that every possible cause has been ruled out.

Manufacturer reference: Samsung: drainage checks before requesting service. Procedures and parts vary by model; use the manual for your machine.

How to handle a fault that does not happen every time

Keep a simple record of the programme, load type, approximate point in the cycle and message shown. Report whether the machine was recently used, whether water supply changed or whether the issue followed another event you actually observed.

Do not recreate an electrical warning or leak for the technician. Existing observations are enough to describe the risk. Safe testing can then be decided during the assessment.

For a fault that cannot be reproduced, agree on the next step and any further charge before another visit or off-site work. A short successful test cannot promise that an intermittent problem will never return.

Inspection is a decision-making step. It does not commit you to accepting a repair, and it should not be described as a full refurbishment of the washer.

What is separate from a normal fault enquiry

Household electrical repairs, building drain clearance, cabinet alterations and unrelated appliance faults may require separate specialists or separately agreed work. Deep cleaning and replacement of unrelated worn parts are also different from diagnosing the reported symptom.

If the machine is built in, stacked, in a cramped utility area or difficult to move, describe that before the visit. Do not dismantle furniture or lift a stacked appliance to prepare it yourself.

If manufacturer warranty may still apply, review its conditions before commissioning outside repair work. This inspection page does not imply manufacturer-authorised status.

How diagnosis changes the repair decision

Illustrative situations: These examples explain repair decisions. They are not records of actual FixMyWasher customer jobs.

Water remains after washing

A washer may retain water because the accessible drain path is restricted. If the restriction is resolved and drainage is verified, replacing the pump would not follow from the original symptom alone. If drainage remains faulty, further assessment is needed.

A closed door does not start the programme

A machine may show a door message because clothing is trapped at the opening or because the locking system has a fault. The inspection should distinguish those situations before a replacement lock is quoted.

The problem does not occur during the visit

A washer may complete a safe assessment sequence even though the customer has seen a recurring fault. The appropriate outcome is to record the checks and remaining uncertainty, then agree on the next diagnostic step.

This is a warranty on the new part supplied, not a statement that every component of the washing machine has been replaced or covered. Confirm any labour, revisit and claim arrangements when approving the work; these should not be assumed from the parts-warranty period.

Frequently asked questions

What is the visiting charge if I do not proceed with repair?

The visiting charge is ₹200 if no repair is carried out. Inspection charges depend on location; confirm all applicable charges together before the visit.

Does ₹200 include replacement parts?

No. It is the visiting charge when no repair is carried out, not a complete repair price. Any parts and repair labour require a separate quote.

Can I request diagnosis without knowing the faulty part?

Yes. Describe the symptoms, cycle stage and any display message. Identifying the cause is the purpose of the assessment.

Does an error code prove that a specific part needs replacing?

No. The code helps direct model-specific checks. Related settings, installation or other components may need assessment.

What if the fault does not happen during the visit?

Discuss the checks completed, what remains uncertain and the next step. Further investigation or another visit should be agreed with its charges.

Do I have to approve the repair after inspection?

You can review the findings and quote before deciding. Confirm the applicable visiting and inspection charges beforehand.

Is the entire machine covered for one year?

The one-year warranty applies to new parts supplied by FixMyWasher. Confirm the identified part and any labour or revisit arrangements with the repair paperwork.

Should I run the washer before the technician arrives?

Do not operate it to reproduce a leak, electrical warning or severe noise. Share existing observations and keep an unsafe machine stopped.
