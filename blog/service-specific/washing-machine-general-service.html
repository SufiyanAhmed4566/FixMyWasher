You can book without identifying a part. A description such as “it drains slowly and sometimes stops before spin” is more useful than requesting a general service while leaving that symptom unmentioned.

What a general-service proposal should include

The agreed visit can cover a review of the model and history, accessible condition checks, relevant installation observations, specified maintenance and an appropriate operating check. The actual tasks depend on the machine and the reason for booking.

Ask for those tasks to be described before the visit. An external inspection, a removable-filter clean and a full internal strip-down are different amounts of work; the same label should not make them appear equivalent.

Replacement parts, major dismantling, extensive cleaning and household plumbing or electrical work are not automatically included. If findings support any of those, the technician should explain the separate scope and charges before proceeding.

This is a scope discussion, not a promise that every component is dismantled or measured at every visit. Ask which checks are relevant to your washer and what the technician could not assess.

An existing fault should lead the visit

A general service is not a substitute for diagnosing a known failure. If there is retained water, leakage, overheating, severe movement or an error code, tell us before booking so the visit can focus on the affected system.

The technician should distinguish a maintenance finding from the cause of the complaint. A dirty drawer or filter can deserve cleaning without being the reason the washer will not start.

Use the inspection and diagnosis guide for fault-led work. The outcome should identify the finding, proposed remedy and any uncertainty, rather than list unrelated maintenance as proof the original fault is fixed.

Cleaning tasks should be named individually

A service may include selected accessible cleaning if that is agreed, but the description should identify the drawer, seal, filter or maintenance cycle involved. “Machine cleaned” does not tell you which areas were accessed.

If deposits or odour require a broader process, discuss deep cleaning as a defined scope. If mineral treatment is appropriate, the descaling service has a different purpose and product-selection requirement.

The general-service quote should not imply that every treatment is included. It should also not recommend all treatments automatically simply because the washer is due for a visit. The actual model, condition and complaint should guide the work.

Use the appliance manual to guide maintenance

Maintenance access, cleaning methods and cycle instructions vary. Some washers provide a user-serviceable drain filter or removable lint filter; others have different arrangements. The technician should identify the actual model rather than apply a routine from another appliance.

LG’s maintenance guidance illustrates several distinct tasks, including tub and dispenser care. The useful principle is to match the procedure to the particular washer and the part being serviced.

If a task is already being done correctly at home and the machine operates normally, say so. The appointment can then focus on the agreed needs instead of repeating work without explaining its purpose.

Why installation belongs in the discussion

A washer can appear faulty when its support, hose routing or connection arrangement is unsuitable. This is particularly relevant after relocation, cabinet changes or plumbing work. A service should note those conditions where they are accessible and relevant.

Tell us about a stand, raised platform, narrow enclosure or a machine that moves across the floor. Do not assume that an accessory pad or a higher water setting is the right remedy without assessment.

The installation guide explains the separate commissioning scope. Where the site needs plumbing, electrical or structural work, agree that boundary rather than expecting a general service to correct the property itself.

Separate observations from repair recommendations

A useful service report tells you what was found and why it matters. A visible stain, an operating noise and a confirmed defective component are different kinds of findings. They should not all become immediate replacement recommendations.

Ask whether an issue affects present operation, needs further diagnosis or is simply something to monitor. The technician should explain the evidence for any new part and the expected result of fitting it.

If a concern cannot be verified during the visit, it should remain recorded as uncertain. A promise that everything is perfect after a short general check can be as unhelpful as recommending a long list of parts without a confirmed fault.

The customer should leave the discussion knowing what is complete, what is optional and what prevents normal use. Those distinctions should follow the findings, not an automatic upsell package.

When a general service leads to a replacement quote

If a part is defective, ask for its identity, reason for replacement, compatibility and complete fitting cost. Confirm what the proposed assembly includes and whether a further visit or configuration is required.

A new component should be recorded as new. If a used or refurbished alternative is discussed, it must have an accurate description and separately stated terms. A generic part name should not hide its condition.

You can review the proposal before deciding. Agree any further investigation or dismantling charge in advance, especially if it is needed to establish whether a major repair is worthwhile.

How often should a general service be booked?

There is no single interval that suits every model, household and service scope. Use the manufacturer’s maintenance guidance, actual usage and condition, along with any recurring symptom. A routine reminder is different from evidence that a component needs replacing.

Keep a simple record of cleaning, repairs and changes in behaviour. This helps avoid duplicate treatments and makes a later fault enquiry more useful. Note the task completed rather than just writing “service done.”

Do not wait for a routine appointment if a new safety warning appears. Conversely, a normally operating appliance should not be told it needs major parts merely because a calendar interval has elapsed.

Information that makes the visit more useful

Have the complete model, locality or PIN code and main reason for booking ready. Mention recent moving, changes in water supply, earlier repairs and any cleaner already used. Share an existing error photo or recording only if available safely.

Describe access constraints such as stacking, built-in cabinetry or narrow stairs. Clear ordinary space around the appliance, but do not lift it, remove panels or detach hoses to prepare.

If water or laundry is trapped inside, report that. Do not force the door or open a filter to make the machine empty; safe access may need to be planned as part of the assessment.

Warnings that change a maintenance visit into fault assessment

Keep the washer stopped for smoke, a burning smell, repeated trips, shock or tingling, severe movement or a significant leak. Do not run a demonstration cycle to help the technician see those symptoms again.

Avoid wet electrical connections and arrange safe isolation from a dry location where necessary. Close a safely accessible water supply during uncontrolled filling or an active supply leak.

Do not bypass a safety control, restrain a violently moving cabinet or keep restarting an appliance that trips. A general service should not be used to justify operating through an unresolved dangerous condition.

What the operating check can and cannot establish

The appropriate check depends on the agreed scope and original concern. It may review relevant filling, movement, drainage, controls or leakage under the model’s procedure. Ask which stages were actually observed.

A short successful run does not prove every component is fault-free or reproduce a problem that appears only with a particular normal load or after a long period. Those limits should be stated clearly.

If maintenance was completed but the original complaint remains, the job record should say so and explain the next proposed step. Cleaning a component is not the same as verifying that an interrupted programme now completes.

Leave with a useful service record

Ask for the completed tasks, findings, supplied parts and operating checks to be summarised. The record should distinguish work already done from recommendations awaiting approval.

Confirm any remaining fault, any condition that prevents use and how to contact FixMyWasher if the original symptom returns. Include the relevant warranty and separately agreed labour or revisit terms.

A clear record is more valuable than an unsupported “fully serviced” label. It gives you a practical maintenance history and prevents the next enquiry from starting with uncertainty about which parts were actually changed.

General-service charges and approval

If no repair is carried out, the visiting charge is ₹200. Inspection charges depend on your location. Share your Bangalore locality or PIN code when booking so FixMyWasher can confirm the applicable charges before the visit.

The ₹200 visiting charge is not a complete repair price. Ask for the inspection, labour and any proposed parts to be explained separately, including how the visiting charge is treated if you approve a repair. The total should be agreed before repair work begins.

A planned maintenance service requires its own quote. Ask which checks, cleaning tasks, operating test and visit arrangements are included. Parts and additional repairs should be itemised separately.

Confirm how the ₹200 no-repair visiting policy applies to a maintenance-only booking and whether any location-based inspection amount is included in the agreed price. It should not be mistaken for the full general-service charge.

If a repair is proposed during the visit, review the finding and total before approval. Any additional dismantling, workshop assessment or further visit needs its own agreed arrangement.

What the one-year new-parts warranty covers

New parts supplied by FixMyWasher carry a one-year warranty. A general-service visit without new components does not automatically place the whole appliance under a one-year warranty.

Where an approved repair includes a new part, have that item identified in the job details. Confirm the terms for service work, labour and revisits separately; they should not be assumed from the new-parts period.

Keep the record and report a returning symptom promptly. The follow-up should assess the actual issue and supplied part rather than assume every later problem is related to the maintenance visit.

Frequently asked questions

Does general service include every repair?

No. The maintenance and inspection scope should be agreed first. Confirmed repairs and replacement parts require a separate explained proposal.

Is deep cleaning automatically included?

No. Ask which areas and procedures are included. Extensive cleaning or dismantling should be stated explicitly.

Should I book general service for a machine that will not drain?

Describe the drainage fault when booking. Diagnosis should guide the visit rather than assuming routine cleaning is enough.

How often does every washer need servicing?

There is no universal interval. Follow the model’s maintenance guidance and consider usage, condition and recurring symptoms.

Will a short test prove the whole machine is healthy?

No. Ask which functions were checked and what limitations remain, especially for intermittent faults.

Can the technician add parts without discussing them?

The finding, component and complete charge should be explained and agreed before additional repair work.

Is ₹200 the full general-service price?

No. Confirm a separate maintenance quote and how the no-repair visiting policy and location-dependent inspection charges apply.

Does general service give a one-year whole-machine warranty?

No. The confirmed one-year warranty applies to new parts supplied by FixMyWasher. Service-work, labour and revisit terms need separate confirmation.

Should I run the washer before the visit to demonstrate a fault?

Do not reproduce leakage, electrical warnings or severe movement. Share observations already available and keep an unsafe machine stopped.

What should I receive at handover?

A clear account of completed tasks, findings, supplied parts, operating checks, remaining issues and the applicable terms.
