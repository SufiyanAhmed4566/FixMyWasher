Washing machine installation service in Bangalore

A washing machine installation should finish with a suitable position, correct connections and a meaningful operating check. Placing the appliance near a tap and switching it on is only part of the job. FixMyWasher installation enquiries cover the model, the intended location and whether the machine is new, relocated or being reconnected.

This guide explains what to prepare, which site conditions need attention and what to confirm in the installation quote. Share photos of the accessible space and connection points if available, without moving a heavy appliance or opening electrical fittings.

What the installation service should cover

The agreed scope can include checking the intended position, following the model’s unpacking and transport-restraint instructions, connecting suitable inlet and drain arrangements, levelling the appliance and performing the appropriate commissioning check. The exact tasks depend on the model and the condition of the site.

New plumbing points, electrical repairs, furniture alterations, stacking work and transport are not automatically included. If one of those is needed, it should be identified and separately agreed before the installation proceeds.

Tell us whether you need a first installation, a reconnection after moving house or correction of an existing setup problem. An older washer that has stopped working may need diagnosis as well as reconnection; installation alone cannot establish that every internal component is sound.

Start with the exact appliance and its instructions

Provide the full model number, not just the brand or load capacity. Front-load, top-load, semi-automatic and washer-dryer designs have different connection and operating requirements. An inlet arrangement or drain measurement from another machine may not apply.

If you have the manual, supplied hoses, covers and packaging components, keep them available. Mention any missing items before the appointment. Do not order an adapter or extended hose based only on a photo without confirming suitability.

For a new appliance, check the seller’s and manufacturer’s installation arrangements before commissioning third-party work. This service page does not claim manufacturer-authorised status or promise how an outside installation affects a particular manufacturer warranty.

Position, clearance and floor support

The washer needs the clearances, surroundings and support specified for its model. Allow for opening the door or lid, accessing normal maintenance points and keeping connections from being crushed when the appliance is placed in its final position.

Describe sloped tiles, a narrow balcony, a raised platform or a cabinet enclosure before booking. A machine that physically fits into a space may still lack required clearance or practical access. Do not assume that packing material or loose blocks make an unsuitable surface acceptable.

If a pedestal or stand is proposed, confirm that it is suitable for the appliance and installed according to its requirements. The installer should not rely on improvised supports to hide a floor problem. Any change to the supporting structure needs its own suitable assessment.

Packing and transport restraints must be handled correctly

Some washers use transport bolts or other restraints to protect components during moving. The model instructions determine which items need removal before operation. Do not guess from the appearance of a rear-panel fastener; structural screws and transport restraints are not interchangeable.

The installer should check the relevant unpacking steps before the first operating test. Where the manual calls for retaining transport items for a future move, keep them with the appliance documents and explain their location at handover.

For a relocated machine, tell us how it was transported and whether its transport protection was used. If there are signs of transit damage or an uncertain mechanical condition, the next step may be assessment before commissioning, rather than trying a high-speed spin immediately.

Inlet hose and water-supply connections

The water connection should match the appliance’s requirements, including the intended inlet arrangement and suitable hose and fittings. A single-inlet machine and a model with more than one supply connection should not be treated as identical.

Check that the intended tap is accessible and report visible damage, corrosion or an existing leak. The connection work should include checking the relevant fittings for leakage once water is admitted. An old hose should not be assumed sound merely because it has the right thread.

If the water supply does not meet the model requirements, discuss the finding before adding accessories or changing plumbing. Do not fit a booster or adjust household pressure based solely on a slow-fill symptom. A supply problem and an appliance valve fault require different assessment.

Drain routing affects how the washer operates

The drain arrangement must follow the model instructions for height, routing, insertion and securing. These details affect more than tidiness: unsuitable installation can contribute to unwanted water loss, poor draining or water returning into the appliance.

A hose should not be crushed behind the cabinet, left insecure or extended using an improvised connection. If the existing drain point cannot support the required arrangement, identify that before completing the installation. Building drain blockage or plumbing alteration may need separate work.

Ask the installer to explain the final route and leave normal access practical. Do not use a generic drain-height number from another model. If water repeatedly drains while the washer is filling or returns later, report that behaviour instead of assuming the drain pump must be replaced.

Electrical suitability and safe access

The appliance must use the power supply and connection arrangement specified by its manufacturer. Report a damaged socket, loose plug fit, visible heat damage, previous trips or uncertainty about the supply before installation. Appliance connection does not substitute for checking defective household wiring.

Do not improvise an extension lead, multi-plug arrangement, earthing bypass or adapter to make a distant socket convenient. Where the supply needs work, have a qualified electrician provide the appropriate solution before the washer is commissioned.

Keep the plug and socket accessible as required and away from leakage risk. The cable must not be pinched or stretched when the washer is positioned. If electrical warning signs appear during the test, stop the test and have the cause assessed rather than repeatedly resetting the supply.

Levelling and stability in the final position

Levelling should follow the appliance instructions, with the machine supported correctly and its adjustable feet secured where the design requires. The relevant check is in the final operating position, not only before it is pushed back into the space.

A cabinet that appears straight by eye is not sufficient evidence of a stable installation. Uneven support, floor movement or a trapped hose can still affect operation. Ask the installer to explain any remaining site limitation rather than treating an accessory as a universal cure for vibration.

If excessive movement remains during an appropriate test, stop and investigate installation, loading conditions and possible appliance faults. A relocated washer can have both a setup issue and an existing mechanical problem; correcting one does not automatically rule out the other.

What the commissioning check should establish

The handover should include the appropriate model-specific initial test or setup procedure. Relevant observations include filling, leakage at connections, drainage and stable operation. Some models require an additional calibration or setup routine; this should be based on their instructions, not a hidden sequence borrowed from another machine.

LG provides an installation and setup reference for its washers. Use it as a manufacturer example, then follow the exact instructions for the appliance being fitted. Requirements shown for one region or model should not override the manual supplied for yours.

Ask what was tested and whether anything remains outstanding. A display lighting up does not demonstrate successful installation. If the site is not ready for a full check, record that limitation and agree what must happen before normal use.

Reinstallation after moving house

A moved machine needs more than reconnection of its old hoses. Review the new supply points, drain route, supporting floor and clearance. A setup that worked at the previous property does not prove the new location meets the same requirements.

Tell us about damage noticed during moving, missing parts and any problem that existed beforehand. This helps distinguish installation work from an unrelated repair and keeps the quote clear. Do not run a visibly damaged machine simply to establish whether the move caused a fault.

Transport, stair carrying, unstacking and cabinet removal need advance discussion. Do not attempt to move a heavy machine alone or pull it by hoses, the door or the dispenser. Arrange the appropriate handling support separately if it is outside the agreed visit scope.

What you should know before the installer leaves

Ask to be shown the normal controls needed for first use, the detergent arrangement and the maintenance points the manual allows the user to access. Keep the manual and any retained transport pieces in a place you can find before a future move.

Confirm the final connection checks, any site condition that remains unresolved and whether the machine is ready for ordinary use. If a repair is needed, ask for it to be recorded as a separate finding with its quote rather than being folded into an unexplained installation charge.

During the first normal wash, remain aware of unusual leakage or movement and follow the load guidance. Report problems promptly. Do not continue operating a washer that moves violently, leaks significantly or shows electrical warning signs.

Installation charges and additional work

If no repair is carried out, the visiting charge is ₹200. Inspection charges depend on your location. Share your Bangalore locality or PIN code when booking so FixMyWasher can confirm the applicable charges before the visit.

The ₹200 visiting charge is not a complete repair price. Ask for the inspection, labour and any proposed parts to be explained separately, including how the visiting charge is treated if you approve a repair. The total should be agreed before repair work begins.

Installation and reinstallation require a separate service quote. Confirm what labour, connections, test and handover are included, and whether any supplied hose or fitting is extra. The ₹200 no-repair visiting charge is not advertised as the installation price.

For a planned installation where no repair is involved, confirm how the visiting policy applies and whether a site inspection is charged separately or included. Ask what happens if the property is not ready and a later visit is needed.

Electrical work, plumbing changes, transport, lifting, stacking and furniture alterations must not be assumed included. Agree any extra work and its price before it begins.

Installation work and the new-parts warranty

New parts supplied by FixMyWasher carry a one-year warranty. If the installation includes a new supplied hose or other component, ask for that part to be identified in the job details. This statement does not automatically cover an old hose, customer-supplied part or the whole appliance.

Confirm installation-workmanship and revisit arrangements separately, including how to report a connection issue after handover. The new-parts warranty should not be used as a substitute for clearly stating those service terms.

Keep the installation record alongside the appliance documents. Stop use and report leakage, serious vibration or an electrical warning rather than continuing while waiting for a follow-up.

Frequently asked questions

Is installation priced at ₹200?

No. ₹200 is the visiting charge if no repair is carried out. Installation has a separate agreed quote; confirm how visiting and location-based inspection charges apply.

Do you need the full model before installation?

Yes. Connections, clearances, transport restraints and setup procedures can vary between models.

Are new plumbing and electrical points included?

Do not assume so. These require suitable assessment and separately agreed work where needed.

Can the washer be connected using an extension lead?

Do not improvise an extension or multi-plug setup. Follow the model’s electrical requirements and arrange a suitable supply with a qualified electrician if needed.

Is the drain height the same for every washer?

No universal measurement is given here. Follow the exact model’s drain installation requirements.

Should I remove every bolt at the back?

No. Only the transport restraints identified in the model instructions should be handled as part of unpacking. Do not mistake structural fasteners for shipping bolts.

Can you reconnect a washer after moving house?

Request reinstallation and share the model and site details. Transport, access work and any existing fault should be discussed separately.

Does every washer need calibration?

No. Any calibration or initial setup should follow the specific model instructions.

What is covered by the one-year warranty?

The confirmed warranty covers new parts supplied by FixMyWasher. Installation workmanship, labour and revisit arrangements need separate confirmation.

What if the site is not ready when the technician arrives?

Discuss the missing requirements and the charges for the visit and any return appointment. The machine should not be treated as commissioned until the required work and checks are completed.
