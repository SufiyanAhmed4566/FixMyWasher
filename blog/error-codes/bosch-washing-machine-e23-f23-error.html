Bosch washing machine E23 or F23: stop use and arrange assessment

Bosch identifies washing-machine E23/F23 as AquaStop activation and directs customers to arrange service. Leave the washer unused. Close its water supply if the tap is safely accessible, and isolate power only from a safe, dry position. Do not touch wet electrical connections or restart the machine to see whether another wash will work.

This is not a code to clear by tilting the washer or bypassing a protective device. The practical aim is to establish why the protection activated, correct the cause and verify the affected operation. The display alone does not identify a replacement hose, seal, sensor or control board.

You do not need a video of an active leak to request help. An existing photograph or a plain description is sufficient. If a model label is inaccessible, start with the purchase record instead of moving the appliance.

A protection alert is not a complete parts diagnosis

The manufacturer’s basic interpretation is clear, but the source of activation still needs assessment on the actual appliance. A quotation should explain the relevant findings rather than convert the words “AquaStop activated” directly into “replace AquaStop hose.”

Use the full E-Nr when matching documentation and spares. Bosch washers can have different inlet and protection arrangements; a component pictured for another model does not establish what is fitted to yours. Include the FD number if it is available on an accessible record.

This guide concerns a Bosch washing machine. Do not import a dishwasher error explanation, drainage sequence or base-access procedure merely because that appliance uses a similar display. The product type and exact identification come before any internal diagnosis.

Why no visible puddle does not justify another wash

A customer can report only the areas that are safely visible. Water may no longer be spreading, the floor may have been wiped, or the triggering condition may not be obvious from the front. None of those observations proves that the machine is ready for normal use.

Do not remove a side panel or tip the cabinet to look for hidden water. Leave internal inspection to the person handling the service visit. A useful description is “E23 appeared; I have not seen water on the floor,” rather than “there cannot be a leak.”

If the alert appears and disappears, preserve that history. An intermittent display changes the information available for diagnosis; it does not automatically make the original event harmless or remove the need to understand what happened.

Separate stopping the programme from stopping the water supply

Pressing a front-panel button is not the same action as closing the plumbing supply. If water is entering or escaping unexpectedly, identify a safely accessible washer tap and close it normally. Do not force a seized valve or disconnect a hose under pressure.

If the tap is behind a heavy appliance, above wet electrical fittings or cannot stop the flow, obtain suitable assistance. Tell building maintenance or the attending professional what you can and cannot isolate. Do not risk injury trying to protect the laundry room.

Keep the machine unused while awaiting the agreed assessment. Do not reopen the water for a trial cycle just before the appointment unless the professional responsible for the check has established that it is safe to do so.

Why tilting, drying and bypass tricks are poor next steps

Tilting a washer can release water into the room or shift it toward areas you cannot see. It also changes the evidence available when the technician arrives. Even if a message disappears afterwards, that outcome does not demonstrate that the original water path is sound.

Do not use a hairdryer inside the cabinet, apply heat to electrical parts, bridge a protective switch or disconnect a safety device. These are not owner recovery procedures offered by this guide. A temporarily quiet display is not the same as a completed repair.

If an online instruction suggests a long button sequence, compare it with the actual manufacturer guidance before acting. Bosch’s E23/F23 support direction is service. This page therefore provides no universal reset combination or instruction to keep cycling power.

Recovering laundry is a separate, controlled task

Tell the provider whether laundry is trapped and whether water is visible through the door. Mention if a hot programme was interrupted. Those details help plan recovery without assuming that the first step should be to force the door open.

Do not unscrew the pump filter as a quick way to empty the machine during an unexplained protection alert. Retained water can be substantial or hot, and the discharge needs controlled containment. A stuck cap or inaccessible drain arrangement is a reason to request assistance.

After a safe recovery, ask whether the machine must remain isolated until further parts or work are available. Being able to remove the clothes does not establish that normal washing can resume. Agree that distinction clearly before the technician leaves.

Distinguish something you saw from something you suspect. “Water was under the left front corner” is useful evidence; “the pump is definitely broken” may send an enquiry toward the wrong part before the machine has been examined.

What professional assessment should establish

Ask for the cause of the activation, the affected area and the proposed correction to be explained separately. The answer may depend on the E-Nr-specific arrangement, evidence of escaping water and the behaviour of the relevant system. It should not be a standard bundle of every possible leak-related part.

If a hose or connection is implicated, the provider should identify the item and why correction or replacement is appropriate. If a door seal is proposed, ask what damage or failure was found. If a sensor or electronic component is named, ask what supports that conclusion beyond the displayed code.

The customer does not need to supervise live electrical tests or request access to the cabinet. A concise written finding is enough to make the decision reviewable: what failed, how the proposed work addresses it, and what remains unverified.

If the cause cannot yet be reproduced or access is restricted, that uncertainty belongs in the record. Ask whether further diagnosis, a return appointment or another professional’s plumbing work is needed before approving a large replacement.

Questions to settle before work begins

Ask for the complete payable total and the part description, including whether the proposal supplies a new item or repairs an existing one. “Leak service” is too broad if the invoice will later include an expensive assembly that was never discussed.

If the repair requires a second visit, clarify part availability, any amount due now and what approval is needed before ordering. Do not assume that a displayed error guarantees a particular spare is stocked or that the work will finish during the first attendance.

Where the appliance may still have manufacturer coverage, check that route before approving independent work. FixMyWasher’s own supplied-part warranty is separate from any Bosch warranty or extended plan. The booking should not imply manufacturer-authorised status.

Two situations that need different explanations

Illustrative situations: These examples explain repair decisions. They are not records of actual FixMyWasher customer jobs.

E23 appeared after recent hose work, with water seen at an accessible connection

Share the installation history and leave the supplies safely isolated. The connection is relevant evidence, but the attending professional should confirm the source and affected areas before declaring the whole inlet assembly faulty.

F23 appeared with no water visible outside the appliance

Arrange assessment instead of tilting the washer to make the code disappear. The absence of an external puddle cannot establish the cause, and a sensor replacement should still require supporting findings.

What a completed repair should demonstrate

The completion record should name the cause found, work performed and any new parts supplied. Ask what relevant operation was checked under safe conditions and whether disturbed water connections were inspected. A photograph of a blank display alone is not a useful completion test.

The scope of testing should match the original complaint as far as practical. If a long programme, particular loading condition or intermittent event was not reproduced, record that limitation. Avoid a blanket “fully tested” statement when only a brief powered check was possible.

Keep the original quotation and final record together. If the alert returns, stop use and share the new event with the earlier job details. A recurrence needs review of the current cause; it does not automatically prove that every previous part was unnecessary or that the same item failed again.

Frequently asked questions

What does Bosch E23 or F23 mean?

Bosch identifies the washer message as AquaStop activation and directs customers to service. The exact cause still needs assessment on the identified model.

Can I keep washing if the floor is dry?

Leave the machine unused and arrange assessment. A dry area in front of the washer does not establish why the alert appeared or prove the appliance is safe to restart.

Does E23 automatically mean a new AquaStop hose?

No. The alert alone does not identify the failed item. Ask for the model-specific findings before approving any assembly.

Should I tilt the machine to clear F23?

No. Tilting can release or move water and does not correct the underlying cause. Report any tilting already performed so the technician understands the history.

Is switching off at the front panel enough?

It does not replace safe water isolation when water is entering or escaping. Close the accessible supply and isolate power only when safe; avoid wet electrical areas.

Can I force the door open to recover clothes?

No. Mention trapped laundry and retained or potentially hot water when booking. Recovery should be controlled, without forcing the latch or opening a filter unexpectedly.

Is E23 the same as E18?

No. Use the exact washer code and model. Bosch E18 has separate drainage guidance; do not choose a pump repair from an E23/F23 alert.

How much does E23 repair cost?

There is no single code-based price. The cause, model, compatible part and agreed scope determine the quote; the page gives only conditional guide budgets.

What if it started after an earlier repair?

Provide the earlier invoice and describe the timing, water location and new alert. Ask for a review of the current cause before approving additional parts.

What warranty applies to a new part?

New parts supplied by FixMyWasher carry a one-year warranty on those parts. Confirm other repair, labour, workmanship and revisit terms separately.
