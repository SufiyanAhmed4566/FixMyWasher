How to clean a top-load washing machine lint filter—and check whether yours has one

First confirm that your top-load washing machine has an owner-removable lint filter. Once the washer is stopped, the basket is still and power is safely disconnected, release the collector as your manual shows, remove lint, clean the permitted surfaces and refit the intact assembly securely. Do not copy another model’s clip or twisting direction.

Some top loaders do not have this type of removable collector. A missing filter in the place shown by a video is not a reason to remove the agitator, pulsator or tub. This guide covers the accessible lint-collection task and explains what to do if clothes still have lint afterwards.

Cleaning an owner-removable lint collector

  1. Stop, empty and isolate

    Allow the programme to finish or stop normally, wait for permitted lid access and for the basket to stop. Remove laundry, switch off and unplug from a safe, dry position. Never bypass the lid lock or reach into a moving basket.

  2. Release the filter as illustrated for your model

    Support the case and use the documented tab, latch or movement. Do not assume all collectors twist or all tabs press in the same direction. Stop if the case resists or the plastic starts to bend abnormally.

  3. Open only the owner-removable cover

    Keep the pieces together and lift out loose lint into a waste container. Do not scrape mesh with a sharp tool or pry apart a sealed assembly. If a cover or bag is not designed to separate, follow the applicable cleaning method without forcing it.

  4. Clean the permitted surfaces

    Use the manual-approved water and soft-brush method for the case or mesh. Remove accessible deposits gently and clean the permitted mounting surface. Avoid making holes larger or pushing a tool into hidden tub passages.

  5. Check condition and refit

    Inspect the mesh and fasteners for visible damage. Complete any required drying, close the cover and seat the case as instructed. The referenced Samsung design uses a positive click; do not assume every model has the same confirmation.

  6. Review before the next suitable load

    Confirm the assembly is secure and no cloth or loose part remains inside. Follow any specified maintenance check, then assess the next ordinary appropriate wash. If a filter detaches, rubs or will not secure normally, stop and request help.

Lint collection is different from pump-filter cleaning

The name “filter” is used for several parts of a washer. In this guide it means the model’s accessible lint collector inside the top-load washing area. It does not mean every component that screens water somewhere inside the machine.

If water will not drain, use the drainage symptom guide. Cleaning an accessible lint case does not prove the pump path, hose or household outlet is clear.

If incoming water is slow, use the inlet-filter guide. That task concerns a supply connection and different isolation requirements.

Keeping the names separate helps a booking too. Say “the removable case inside the tub” if that is what you cleaned, rather than telling a technician the pump filter was serviced when it was not.

Prepare for a small owner task, not a dismantling job

A soft brush, clean water, a cloth and a container for loose lint are a practical starting point when permitted by the manual. Follow any different material or drying instruction for a fabric bag or specialised collector.

Avoid metal picks, wire brushes, abrasive pads and improvised solvents. If residue cannot be removed with the approved method, seek product-specific advice rather than increasing force.

No generic tool kit is needed to remove a user-release filter. A procedure that requires tub, pulsator or agitator removal is outside this guide. Do not interpret a stubborn latch as permission to unscrew unrelated parts.

Do not wash out lumps of collected lint into the washer or deliberately push them into a household drain. Remove loose material into a suitable waste container before completing the permitted surface cleaning.

What to do with damaged mesh or a broken clip

Look for a visible tear, loose edge, cracked hinge or latch that no longer retains the assembly. Record the model before buying a replacement: similar outer dimensions do not necessarily mean a part fits or locks correctly.

Do not enlarge holes to improve flow, attach a homemade mesh with wire or glue a broken clip inside the washing area. A loose repair can create a new problem during operation. The intended assembly should be restored with a suitable component.

Ask whether the required item is the mesh, a removable insert or the complete case. Those may be different part scopes depending on the model. A quote should name what is supplied and why.

If the filter is intact but will not seat, check the normal orientation and owner instructions without force. Request help if the fit remains wrong. An apparent click from one side is not enough to ignore an obviously loose assembly.

Review the load before buying a new filter

If the complaint is limited to a particular fabric combination, note that pattern and use the care labels and model loading guidance. Avoid treating one high-shedding item as proof that the entire filtration system is faulty.

Check pockets and remove loose items as part of normal preparation. Follow the programme’s capacity, which may differ from the maximum figure advertised for the machine. Overpacking should not be used as a way to reduce the number of washes.

Do not add a random floating filter, net or accessory on the assumption that it is compatible with every top loader. If you want an accessory, establish suitability for the actual appliance and intended load first.

If the machine previously handled similar suitable loads and the complaint is now persistent, describe when it changed. Cleaning history, product changes and any recent work help the assessment without predetermining the cause.

How often should the lint filter be cleaned?

Use the owner manual’s schedule and any applicable reminder. The referenced LG example recommends care after laundry, while other designs may have different instructions. This guide does not replace your model’s requirement with a universal weekly or monthly rule.

Make the permitted check part of an ordinary routine you can sustain. Keep track of recurring unusual deposits only when there is a reason; a normal household does not need a detailed maintenance diary for every clean.

A filter that fills repeatedly should prompt a review of what is being collected and the loads involved, not an automatic expensive repair. A torn filter or unreliable clip is a different finding and should not be ignored because cleaning is frequent.

Cleaning cannot guarantee completely lint-free results for every textile. Avoid buying a service on an absolute promise that no future load will ever shed fibres.

A tub-clean programme does not identify this component for you

Follow the actual tub-clean instructions separately if that maintenance is due. Do not assume a cycle removes everything captured inside a removable collector, and do not run a cleaning programme with an unsecured or missing filter.

The instructions determine whether a product is used and whether the drum must be empty. Do not add ordinary detergent or household chemicals because the washer is being cleaned rather than used for clothes.

If there is an active leak, abnormal noise or another unresolved fault, settle that condition before starting an additional maintenance cycle. A cleaning programme is not a substitute for a damaged-part assessment.

When an assessment is more useful than repeated cleaning

Request help when the release is stuck, the collector is damaged, the correct component cannot be identified or a repeated symptom remains after appropriate care. Share the full model and what was actually cleaned.

If a repair is proposed, ask how it relates to the remaining complaint. A new motor, pump or board is not justified merely by lint on clothes. Any internal fault needs supporting findings.

For a replacement collector, clarify the compatible part and whether fitting is needed. For broader cleaning, define the included areas. Washing an accessible lint case is not automatically a complete internal tub-cleaning service.

After work, ask what was corrected and checked. If a particular wash-quality problem could not be reproduced during the visit, record that limitation instead of accepting an unsupported guarantee.

Bangalore visiting and inspection charges

The visiting charge is ₹200 if no repair is carried out. Inspection charges depend on location. Share your Bangalore locality or PIN code and confirm all applicable charges together before booking. ₹200 is not an advertised complete repair or parts-replacement price.

Ask how the visiting and inspection charges are treated if a repair proceeds. No automatic waiver, deduction or additional charge should be assumed. Your approved quote should make clear whether these amounts are included, credited or payable separately, and state any applicable tax and the final payable total.

Warranty when a new part is supplied

New parts supplied by FixMyWasher carry a one-year warranty. Ask for the supplied part to be identified on your job record or invoice. This applies to that new part; it does not turn the job into a one-year warranty on the entire washing machine.

Repairing an existing component is different from supplying a new one. Confirm the terms for repaired boards, retained parts, workmanship, labour, revisits and customer-supplied items separately. Do not infer those terms from the new-parts warranty.

Keep the quote, approved changes and completion record together. If the symptom returns, report what happened and when rather than assuming that the same part has failed. Stop using the washer if it leaks significantly, trips the circuit or develops a burning smell.

Routine cleaning without a new supplied part does not create a one-year warranty on the whole appliance. Confirm the agreed cleaning scope and any service or revisit terms separately.

Frequently asked questions

Does every top-load washing machine have a lint filter?

No. Check the exact manual. Do not dismantle the tub to search for a removable collector that the model does not provide.

Is a Magic Filter the same shape on every machine?

No universal shape or release applies. Use the diagram and instructions for the full model.

Can I remove the filter while the basket slows down?

Wait until operation has stopped, access is permitted and the basket is still. Never bypass the lid safety mechanism.

Can I scrub mesh with a metal brush?

Do not use an abrasive tool that can damage the collector. Use only the model-approved cleaning method.

What if the mesh is torn?

Identify the compatible replacement scope. Do not enlarge holes, fit improvised mesh or rely on adhesive repairs inside the washing area.

Why is lint still on clothes after cleaning?

Review the actual load and whether the deposit is fibre or product residue. Persistent symptoms need assessment; a clean filter alone does not identify the cause.

Will cleaning this filter fix a drain error?

It is not a complete drain diagnosis. Report retained water, the exact error and the actual component cleaned.

Should I buy a floating lint-catching accessory?

Do not assume universal suitability. Check compatibility with the model and intended use before adding an accessory.

How often should I clean it?

Follow the exact model schedule. The examples differ, so this guide does not impose one interval for every design.

Can I wash without the filter while waiting for a replacement?

Do not assume that operation without the intended assembly is permitted. Follow the manufacturer’s guidance for the exact model or seek advice before use.
