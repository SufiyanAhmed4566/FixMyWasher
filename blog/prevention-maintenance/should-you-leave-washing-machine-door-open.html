Should you leave the washing machine door open after a wash?

Follow your model’s after-use guidance: airing an empty washer can help accessible moisture dry, but only when the open door or lid does not create unsafe access or an obstruction. Children, pets and the room layout matter. There is no universal rule to leave every washer wide open all day or for a fixed number of minutes.

This page is about the arrangement after a completed wash. It is not an instruction to force a locked door, bypass an interlock or leave an auxiliary door open during operation. Airing is one part of care; it does not clean residue, mend a seal or resolve retained water.

An empty drum is still a space a child or animal could enter. Disconnecting power does not remove that physical access risk.

Six decisions for safe after-wash airing

  1. Wait for normal access

    Allow the cycle to finish and the door or lid to release under the model instructions. Keep hands clear of moving parts. If access remains restricted, identify the reason rather than pulling harder.

  2. Remove the laundry and loose accessible items

    Empty the drum when practical and check that nothing remains caught at the opening. Do not reach behind the gasket or remove a retaining ring as part of unloading.

  3. Complete the permitted accessible drying

    Use the model’s gentle wiping and drying method on the appropriate accessible areas. Keep cloths out of the machine and avoid unapproved sprays or chemicals. Airing does not replace this task where it is specified.

  4. Check who can access the opening

    Consider children, pets, visitors and the person using the room next. Arrange effective access control or supervision as appropriate. Do not treat a control-panel setting as a physical barrier to an open drum.

  5. Use only the model-appropriate door arrangement

    Follow any instructions for leaving the main door or lid ajar. Avoid improvised wedges, magnets or devices that strain the hinge or interfere with the lock. Keep required passage and access clear.

  6. Review before the next use

    Check the drum and opening before closing and loading, and make sure no object or animal has entered. If the door no longer moves or closes normally, stop forcing it and arrange assessment.

What leaving the door ajar is intended to achieve

Samsung’s after-use guidance for covered products recommends airing the main door and dispenser. The practical purpose is to avoid leaving accessible moisture enclosed after washing. It is not a claim that an open door sterilises the machine.

The arrangement should be considered alongside permitted wiping, prompt unloading and scheduled care. If detergent residue or trapped material remains, airflow alone does not remove it.

Do not judge success only by whether the drum smells strongly of a cleaning product. A neutral or improved smell is an observation, not proof that every hidden surface is clean.

Airing also does not resolve a leak, drainage error or door-fit problem. Those need their own assessment even if the machine is left open between loads.

Children and pets change the decision

LG’s WD-8015C manual explicitly warns that an open door can attract children to climb inside or hang on it. This is why a general maintenance tip must be considered with the model’s safety instructions and the actual household.

Do not assume an empty or unplugged machine is harmless to enter. Prevent children and pets from playing in or on the appliance and follow the manufacturer’s supervision requirements.

A child-lock feature can have model-specific control functions. Do not assume it prevents entry into an already open drum or makes an unattended open-door arrangement safe.

If effective safe access cannot be maintained, do not prioritise airflow over that concern. Seek a manufacturer-appropriate care arrangement, dry accessible areas as permitted and manage the laundry space safely.

How long should you leave it open?

Use any time or condition specified by your own manual. If it gives no fixed period, do not borrow a universal 15-minute, one-hour or overnight rule from another model’s support page.

The relevant question is whether you can follow the permitted drying routine safely in the room. A longer opening period is not automatically a better outcome if it creates access or an obstruction.

You do not need to keep a stopwatch to maintain a normally operating washer unless a specific product or procedure requires timed steps. Do not transfer a chemical contact time into a door-airing instruction.

For long absences, use the model’s non-use procedure and a separate household access plan. The arrangement after an ordinary load is not automatically the right one for an unattended home.

Should the detergent drawer also stay open?

Only use the airing position permitted for the actual dispenser system. A conventional drawer and an automatic-dosing reservoir can differ in what is exposed and how it should be maintained.

Do not pull a drawer out beyond its normal position and leave removable inserts where a child or pet can access them. Keep detergent and cleaning products stored appropriately.

If residue is present, use the drawer-cleaning guide. Leaving a dirty compartment open is not the same as cleaning and restoring it.

If the drawer leaks or will not seat properly, treat that as a separate condition. Do not wedge it in an airing position to avoid dealing with the fit.

Do you need a door prop or airing gadget?

Not automatically. First check whether the manufacturer provides a suitable feature or specific advice for your model. A generic accessory should not be assumed compatible because it attaches magnetically or is advertised for all front loaders.

Do not place an object where it strains the hinge, distorts the seal or interferes with normal latching. Do not alter the lock or bypass a protective function to keep the door open.

If the door swings because of an installation or hinge concern, ask for that condition to be assessed. A prop may conceal the symptom without addressing the reason.

An accessory also cannot make unrestricted child or pet access safe. Assess the room arrangement before purchasing a device intended only to hold an opening.

A door that will not open is a different problem

If water remains or the door is still locked after a cycle, use the exact model’s recovery guidance. Do not force it for the sake of airing, insert tools into the latch or bypass the lock.

Record the display, whether water is visible and the stage at which the machine stopped. Do not assume a failed lock or control board from the fact that the door is closed.

Use the door-not-opening guide for the symptom route. The safe access decision comes before cleaning or ventilation.

If the door opens but closes only with unusual force, report that separately. A damaged hinge, trapped fabric, latch or gasket-fit issue requires a relevant finding rather than a guessed seal replacement.

If the machine still smells when aired

Review the actual routine: laundry removal, compatible detergent dosing, accessible drawer and seal care, and the model’s maintenance programme. Note whether the smell is in the machine, on clothes or around the waste outlet.

A persistent smell should not be masked with perfume, strong disinfectant or repeated mixed chemicals. Use the prevention guide and the existing odour symptom route where needed.

If visible mould-like material remains, follow the component-specific approved method rather than identifying its health significance from colour. A stain and a torn seal are different findings.

An electrical burning smell, repeated trip or water near electricity is not a ventilation problem. Stop use and obtain appropriate help instead of airing the room and continuing the wash.

The visiting charge is ₹200 if no repair is carried out. Inspection charges depend on location. Share your Bangalore locality or PIN code and confirm all applicable charges together before booking. ₹200 is not an advertised complete repair or parts-replacement price.

Ask how the visiting and inspection charges are treated if a repair proceeds. No automatic waiver, deduction or additional charge should be assumed. Your approved quote should make clear whether these amounts are included, credited or payable separately, and state any applicable tax and the final payable total.

Warranty when a new part is supplied

New parts supplied by FixMyWasher carry a one-year warranty. Ask for the supplied part to be identified on your job record or invoice. This applies to that new part; it does not turn the job into a one-year warranty on the entire washing machine.

Repairing an existing component is different from supplying a new one. Confirm the terms for repaired boards, retained parts, workmanship, labour, revisits and customer-supplied items separately. Do not infer those terms from the new-parts warranty.

Keep the quote, approved changes and completion record together. If the symptom returns, report what happened and when rather than assuming that the same part has failed. Stop using the washer if it leaks significantly, trips the circuit or develops a burning smell.

Routine cleaning without a new supplied part does not create a one-year warranty on the whole appliance. Confirm the agreed cleaning scope and any service or revisit terms separately.

Frequently asked questions

Should every washer door stay wide open all day?

No. Follow the model’s guidance and maintain safe access and passage. There is no universal wide-open rule.

Is a fixed one-hour airing period required?

Only use a fixed period if your exact instructions specify it. Do not transfer another model’s timing.

Does unplugging make an open drum safe for children?

No. Physical access and entrapment risks remain; prevent children and pets from entering or playing on the appliance.

Can child lock protect an already open drum?

Do not assume that. Control features vary and are not a substitute for physical access protection.

Should I force a locked door open to dry it?

No. Resolve the locked-door or retained-water condition through the proper model-specific route.

Can I use a generic magnetic door prop?

Do not assume compatibility. Avoid accessories that strain parts, interfere with latching or create unsafe access.

Does opening the door replace drum cleaning?

No. Airing, surface care and scheduled maintenance have different purposes.

Can the detergent drawer stay open too?

Use only the permitted arrangement for the actual dispenser, with safe access and no obstruction.

Why does the machine still smell when left open?

The remaining complaint may need another maintenance area or assessment. Record the routine and symptom instead of simply extending the opening time.

Is the holiday door arrangement the same as after a wash?

Not automatically. Use the non-use instructions and account for who can access the unattended room.
