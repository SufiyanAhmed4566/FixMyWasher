Repair or replace · Bangalore

Should you repair or replace your washing machine?

Repair is worth considering when the fault is confirmed, the proposed work is suitable and the rest of the machine still meets your needs. Replacement becomes more attractive when major damage, several necessary repairs, unavailable parts or an unsuitable machine make the repair a poor use of your budget. If the diagnosis is uncertain, the next decision may be to investigate further.

This guide helps Bangalore households compare those options before approving a repair or buying another washer. It does not use a fixed age limit or a single repair-cost percentage to make the decision for every machine. A small repair on an older washer and a major assembly replacement on a younger one deserve different conversations.

A decision table is a starting point, not a remote diagnosis. The useful evidence is a finding tied to your machine: what failed, what the proposed work will restore and what limitations will remain. “Old machine” and “expensive brand” are not sufficiently detailed explanations.

A symptom is not yet a repair plan

A washer that does not spin may be waiting for another condition to be satisfied. A machine that will not start can also have different causes. Before choosing between repair and replacement, establish whether the quotation follows an assessment or is only a preliminary allowance based on your description.

Request a short explanation in ordinary language: what was checked, what was found, and why the named component or corrective work follows from that finding. You do not need internal electrical readings or a demonstration with exposed parts to receive a useful explanation.

If substantial dismantling is needed, separate that permission from permission to repair. Agree the investigation cost, what it is expected to establish, the stopping point and the condition in which the machine will be left if you decline further work. An initial inspection should not silently become approval for a complete assembly replacement.

Judge the washer you will have after the repair

Think about its condition before the current fault. Did it complete the programmes you use, hold an appropriate load and fit the available space? Had there been a separate leak, serious corrosion, repeated interruption or an unresolved problem? Record what is actually known rather than treating every old component as a future failure.

Age provides context for ownership and repair history; it does not prove that a specific repair will succeed or fail. A relatively new washer can have extensive damage, while an older machine can have one isolated, serviceable fault. No inspection can promise a precise number of remaining years.

Distinguish cosmetic wear from a functional or structural concern. A scratched panel is a different issue from damage affecting mounting or safe operation. Have the technician explain whether each observation is cosmetic, requires monitoring, needs correction now or prevents the proposed repair from being suitable.

Use written totals where possible. A headline repair subtotal should not be compared with an all-inclusive purchase total, or the reverse. Do not add ordinary fitting twice when it is already included, and do not count an uncertain exchange value as money you have definitely received.

For replacement, compare a machine you would actually buy. The cheapest advertised model may not meet your capacity, dimensions or programme needs. Equally, comparing a modest repair only with a premium upgrade can make repair look artificially attractive. Keep optional upgrades separate from the minimum suitable replacement.

Use cost ranges to prepare, then decide from your quote

As indicative FixMyWasher budgeting estimates, a minor correction without major parts is ₹500–₹1,500, an existing PCB repair is ₹1,800–₹4,500, and bearing/seal work on a separately serviceable design is ₹3,500–₹8,000. A specified drum or tub assembly with normal labour is ₹7,000–₹18,000, with some designs higher.

These are proposed guide ranges, not fixed tariffs or surveyed market averages. Brand, exact model, part, confirmed damage, scope, access and location affect the quote. Normal labour is included in these named scopes; separately applicable visit, inspection, transport, access and tax treatment need confirmation.

A repair being inside a range does not establish value on its own. Its suitability, the machine’s remaining known condition and your alternative matter. Use the full repair cost guide for component budgets and the quotation guide for a line-by-line review.

Why a fixed percentage or age rule is incomplete

A repair-to-replacement percentage can summarise two prices, but it cannot explain the quality of the diagnosis, part availability or whether the washer still suits you. There is no universal percentage at which every repair becomes sensible or every replacement becomes necessary.

Avoid calculations that divide a repair price by an invented number of extra years. If someone promises that a new board gives the whole machine five more years, ask what evidence supports that promise. Replacing one component does not renew every other component.

Also separate money already spent from money you can still choose to spend. A previous repair is part of the history, but you cannot recover it by automatically approving another. Ask whether the next expenditure is worthwhile from today onward. A recent repeat of the same symptom also deserves review against the earlier job and warranty before another paid replacement.

Three realistic ways the decision can differ

Illustrative situations: These examples explain repair decisions. They are not records of actual FixMyWasher customer jobs.

An older washer with one modest correction

Suppose assessment identifies one correction at ₹900, with no major new part, and no other essential fault has been found. If the washer still meets the household’s needs, that may favour repair. Age alone does not require replacement. Confirm the payable total and the scope; the example does not promise how long the appliance will last.

A bearing option turns into a larger assembly decision

Suppose a preliminary ₹5,500 bearing repair depends on reusable surrounding parts. Assessment instead finds damage requiring an ₹11,000 specified assembly. Those are alternative scopes, not a ₹16,500 package. Pause at the changed finding, obtain the revised total and reconsider it against the rest of the machine and a suitable replacement.

An affordable repair on an unsuitable machine

Suppose the fault is repairable but the machine has already become too small for the household. Repair may restore operation without solving the capacity problem. Compare the repair as a way to keep the existing appliance with the full cost and practical benefit of changing it. Do not label a capacity upgrade an essential fault repair.

Availability and compatibility can decide the outcome

A low estimate is of limited use if it depends on a part that has not been identified. Supply the full model from an accessible rating label, plus a serial or production reference if requested. A brand name, capacity and photograph may not distinguish similar-looking versions.

Ask whether a new replacement, repaired original or another clearly described option is being quoted. If a part needs ordering, confirm the current availability estimate and any deposit or cancellation terms before committing. Do not assume a first-visit completion or a guaranteed delivery date.

Compatibility also matters for a future replacement machine. Check the measured installation space and route of access before ordering; include room required by the manufacturer for connections and operation. A nominally similar capacity does not establish that a new washer will fit the same recess.

Bosch India’s spare-parts lookup uses the appliance model number (E-Nr.) to identify available parts. This is one manufacturer example of why the full model is more useful than the brand alone; it is not a promise of stock for your particular repair.

Include inconvenience without inventing savings

If the washer is essential to your daily routine, compare credible repair and delivery timelines. Temporary laundry costs can be included when you genuinely expect to incur them. Use a reasonable stated allowance rather than making a repair look poor by assigning it an exaggerated delay.

A newer machine may have different energy and water performance, but do not assume a guaranteed saving just from its age. A financial comparison needs the selected model’s information, realistic usage and your actual tariffs. Avoid claiming that an unmeasured saving will definitely repay the purchase.

The decision can also involve accessibility, familiar controls and programmes you rely on. Those are legitimate benefits to weigh alongside price. Write down your two or three priorities so a discount or a single attractive feature does not replace the whole comparison.

Ask how the visiting and inspection charges are treated if a repair proceeds. No automatic waiver, deduction or additional charge should be assumed. Your approved quote should make clear whether these amounts are included, credited or payable separately, and state any applicable tax and the final payable total.

Warranty: the supplied new part and the rest of the washer

New parts supplied by FixMyWasher carry a one-year warranty. Ask for the supplied part to be identified on your job record or invoice. This applies to that new part; it does not turn the job into a one-year warranty on the entire washing machine.

Repairing an existing component is different from supplying a new one. Confirm the terms for repaired boards, retained parts, workmanship, labour, revisits and customer-supplied items separately. Do not infer those terms from the new-parts warranty.

Keep the quote, approved changes and completion record together. If the symptom returns, report what happened and when rather than assuming that the same part has failed. Stop using the washer if it leaks significantly, trips the circuit or develops a burning smell.

For this decision, have the component or assembly clearly identified as new, repaired or retained. The difference affects what you are buying and which warranty statement applies.

Frequently asked questions

Is it usually better to repair or replace a washing machine?

It depends on the confirmed fault, complete repair cost, remaining known condition, part availability and household needs. A defined repair can be sensible; major combined damage or an unsuitable machine can favour replacement.

Should I automatically replace a ten-year-old washer?

No. Age is context, not a diagnosis. Review the actual fault, repair history, condition and available suitable parts without assuming a fixed remaining lifespan.

Should repair cost stay below half the price of a new machine?

A percentage can help describe prices, but it is not a universal decision rule. Compare complete totals and the quality and usefulness of each option.

Does a major repair make the whole washing machine new?

No. Only the agreed components and work are changed. Retained parts keep their existing condition and are not automatically covered by the supplied-part warranty.

Should I include my earlier repair bill in today’s choice?

Use it as evidence of repair history and possible recurring faults. Money already spent is not a reason by itself to approve another repair. Review any relevant earlier warranty first.

What if the diagnosis is uncertain?

Agree a limited investigation, its cost and stopping point before authorising a major part. Do not treat a symptom or an unexplained error message as proof of one component failure.

Can I compare repair with any new washer price?

Use an actual suitable alternative with the capacity, dimensions and functions you need. Include confirmed delivery, installation and site costs, and keep optional upgrades separate.

Will I pay ₹200 if I decline the repair?

The visiting charge is ₹200 if no repair is carried out. Inspection charges depend on location. Confirm the complete no-repair total before booking.

What warranty applies if I choose repair?

New parts supplied by FixMyWasher carry a one-year warranty. Confirm separate terms for repaired originals, labour, workmanship and revisits; the whole washer is not automatically covered.

Can you guarantee how many more years a repair will provide?

No precise remaining lifespan can be promised for the whole appliance from a component repair. The decision should use the confirmed scope, condition and actual warranty.
