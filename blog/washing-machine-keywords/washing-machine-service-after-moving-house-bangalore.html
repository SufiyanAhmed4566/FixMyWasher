State which stage of the move the machine has reached

A delivered but unconnected washer needs a different enquiry from one that has already completed several washes and developed a concern. Say whether it is still packed, positioned, connected, awaiting installation or already in use at the new property.

Identify who handled preparation, transport and setup, and share any records available. If you do not know whether model-specific transport items were dealt with correctly, state that uncertainty. Do not guess from the fact that the appliance switched on.

The service contact can then discuss the actual task: installation-related assessment, investigation of a new symptom or follow-up of a known earlier fault. This helps avoid an appointment framed as routine cleaning when the real question is whether the new setup or machine needs attention.

Do not treat delivery as completed installation

Check what work was actually agreed and completed by the delivery or moving provider. Placing a machine in a utility space does not necessarily mean it has been prepared and installed for use according to the exact model instructions.

Ask the appropriate installer to confirm the relevant preparation and setup tasks. Do not run the machine simply to see whether the move was successful if installation status is uncertain or visible damage is present. Keep the model manual and transport accessories available.

Manufacturer guidance varies by appliance. Use the official support route for the actual model rather than copying a sequence from a similar-looking machine. For an LG appliance, the official India support hub can help locate the relevant product assistance; it does not imply a relationship between LG and FixMyWasher.

Explain the transport history without assigning blame

Share what is known about the move: whether the machine was stored, who prepared it and whether any visible incident or damage was reported. Keep statements factual. If a mover said something was checked but no record exists, identify it as that statement rather than as your own verified observation.

A problem appearing after transport can have several possible explanations. The assessment should consider the machine’s current condition and installation instead of concluding from timing alone that a particular person caused damage.

Retain relevant condition photographs and handover records for your own discussions with providers. The appliance assessment should describe findings and limitations accurately; it should not be stretched into a guarantee about the cause of every concealed condition or a decision on a dispute.

Tell the provider how the new utility space differs

Mention a different floor position, tighter cabinet, stacked installation or balcony placement. Say whether access to the connections is restricted and whether the washer was moved again after initial setup. A safe photograph from the room entrance can help explain space constraints.

Do not pull the washer out, tilt it or remove surrounding fittings to investigate on your own. Safe access may need to be planned with an appropriate installer or another professional. The service scope should identify any separate cabinet or property work.

The new space must be considered against the actual appliance instructions. A repair does not automatically make an unsuitable location appropriate, and the absence of visible exterior damage does not establish that all installation conditions are correct.

Keep water-supply and drainage observations separate

Describe the actual behaviour: no filling, slow filling, retained water or water appearing outside the machine. Mention known changes in taps, hoses or drainage arrangements at the new property without deciding which component is responsible.

If another fixture or common drain is affected, report that observation too. The provider can explain which checks relate to the washer and whether the property needs a qualified plumbing or building assessment. A new drain arrangement should not automatically lead to a pump replacement.

Do not alter shared plumbing, extend connections with unsuitable fittings or use a generic drain-height instruction for an unidentified model. The appropriate installer should follow the correct instructions and assess the actual situation before the household resumes normal use.

Report electrical concerns before a routine service visit

Tell the provider about damaged cables, tripping, shock, burning or water near electrical equipment. Stop use and keep away from the hazard. Use an isolation point only if it is safely accessible without approaching the unsafe condition, and arrange appropriate urgent help where needed.

Do not improvise an extension or adaptor arrangement to make the machine reach a socket, and do not ask for protective devices to be bypassed. Property wiring and protection questions belong with the appropriate qualified professional.

An appliance appointment may need to wait until a property hazard is resolved. That is a scope and access issue to clarify, not evidence that the machine itself has been repaired. Keep the property finding and appliance assessment connected but separately documented.

Record the first observed behaviour without repeated testing

If the machine was already used safely before the concern appeared, note the programme, load and stage. Keep the complete displayed code and say whether the issue occurred once or repeatedly. Mention whether the same symptom existed at the previous home.

There is no need to recreate a dangerous leak or severe movement for the technician. Existing safe observations can guide the assessment. If no first-use check has occurred, say that rather than describing the machine as working or faulty based only on a lit display.

Ask what the professional can establish during the agreed visit and what remains uncertain. A brief check may not reproduce an intermittent concern; the record should reflect the actual checks rather than implying every operating condition has been tested.

Distinguish setup correction from an appliance repair

After assessment, ask whether the proposed work concerns installation, a property condition, an established appliance fault or more than one task. The quotation should describe those scopes clearly enough to understand why each is needed.

If a replacement component is proposed, ask how the finding supports it and what establishes the full-model match. If setup-related work is proposed, ask which condition it addresses and what relevant checks will follow. Avoid approving a bundle simply because everything happened after moving.

The total depends on the actual work, brand, model, parts, labour and access. The stated ₹200 visiting amount if no repair is carried out is not an all-inclusive relocation or reinstallation price. Confirm inspection and any installation-related charges before the appointment.

Coordinate unfinished tasks between different providers

The mover, installer, building team and repair provider may each have a different responsibility. Keep the actual finding and next step available to whichever person needs to act. A vague message that the washer needs service may not explain what prevents completion.

If another provider must correct a property issue or supply a required accessory, ask what must be resolved before the next appliance visit. Confirm the later appointment rather than assuming it remains reserved indefinitely.

Do not approve overlapping changes without keeping their sequence clear. If someone alters the installation after assessment, tell the next provider. Accurate records help explain why the machine’s present condition may differ from what the earlier technician observed.

Keep a clear starting record for the new installation

At handover, ask what work was completed and which relevant functions were checked. Record any limitation, unresolved property condition or pending component. A successful check of one function should not be presented as a general certification of the entire machine and property.

Save the model, location, invoice and written coverage together with the transport and setup history. New parts supplied by FixMyWasher carry a one-year warranty under written terms; other work and revisits require separate clarification.

If a concern returns, refer to this record and describe the current behaviour. A clear starting history at the new home helps the next enquiry distinguish the original move-related concern from a later, possibly different issue.

Visit charges and the price of the actual work

The visiting charge is ₹200 if no repair is carried out. Inspection charges depend on location. Confirm the applicable booking and inspection amounts, and how they are treated if you approve work, before scheduling. The ₹200 visiting amount is not the price of a complete repair or a promise of a free inspection.

The repair total depends on the brand, full model, diagnosed fault, required part, labour and access. Ask for the complete payable amount before approval, including any separately agreed transport, additional visit or tax. A symptom described on the phone can guide the enquiry but may not support a fixed final price.

Keep the diagnosis and quotation together. If the scope changes after assessment, ask for the revised explanation and total before additional work begins. Any property plumbing or electrical work must be identified and priced separately where required.

New parts, compatibility and your one-year warranty

New parts supplied by FixMyWasher carry a one-year warranty. The invoice should identify the covered component and written terms. This is not automatically a warranty on the entire washing machine, every future fault or the property installation.

Ask whether a proposed component is manufacturer-original, a compatible alternative or repair of the existing assembly. These are different offers. Confirm the full model match, condition, sourcing and installed price before approving it. A “universal” description alone does not establish suitability.

Labour, workmanship, repairs to existing components and revisit terms need separate confirmation. Keep the invoice and contact the provider with the original job details if a concern returns. Where manufacturer warranty or a service plan may still apply, check that route before authorising independent work.

Prepare access without opening or moving the machine

Keep the model from your invoice or an existing safe label photograph, the complete error message and any previous repair record ready. Clear ordinary access only where it is safe. Tell us about stairs, a stacked installation, a tight enclosure or a locked utility area before the appointment.

Do not dismantle panels, force a locked door, tilt a heavy washer or recreate a dangerous symptom for a video. If there is a shock, smoke, burning, severe movement or water near electrics, stop use and keep away; arrange the appropriate urgent help before a routine appliance visit. Use an isolation control only if it is safely accessible without approaching the hazard.

Safe inspection may require a qualified electrician, plumber or building team first. A washing-machine booking does not automatically include property wiring, earthing or plumbing correction.

Questions customers ask

Can I request service after moving my washer within Bangalore?

Yes. Share the full model, new locality, setup status and current concern so support and scope can be confirmed.

Does a problem after moving prove transport damage?

No. Timing is relevant but the actual cause requires assessment.

Should I run the washer immediately after delivery?

Do not treat delivery as completed installation. Confirm model-specific preparation and setup first, especially if anything is uncertain or damaged.

What transport information is useful?

Known preparation, storage, handling events, accessories and condition records.

Can I use a setup guide for a similar-looking model?

Use the instructions for the actual appliance through the manufacturer’s official support route.

What if the new installation has tight access?

Describe the space before booking and agree safe access; do not move heavy equipment yourself.

Does a drainage problem always mean a failed pump?

No. The appliance and relevant installation conditions need assessment.

What if the machine trips or causes a shock?

Stop use, keep away from the hazard and arrange appropriate help; do not repeatedly reset or bypass protection.

Is reinstallation included in the visiting amount?

Not automatically. Confirm the specific task and applicable inspection, installation or repair charges.

What if a different provider changes the setup later?

Keep the change recorded and tell the next assessor so the sequence remains clear.

What should be checked after the work?

The relevant function and original concern under appropriate conditions, with any limitation explained.

What record should I keep at the new home?

Model, transport and setup history, finding, completed work, invoice and written terms.
