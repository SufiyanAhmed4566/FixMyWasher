A first message that gives the booking team what it needs

Send the locality or PIN code where the washer is installed, the machine type and complete model if available. Describe what happened in ordinary language: it stopped while filling, left water after a wash or developed a new noise. Include the complete displayed code rather than an interpretation of it.

Say whether there has been a recent move, previous repair or change in installation. If the machine is under possible manufacturer coverage, mention that before arranging independent work. These details help clarify the appropriate route and whether the requested assessment is supported.

An example message is: “I need an assessment for a front-load washer in HSR Layout. It stops before spinning and leaves water. I can share the full model from the invoice. Please confirm support, available visit windows and the applicable charges.” This describes a request without guessing a failed part or claiming an appointment already exists.

Agree a window that works for the household

Provide a preferred time range and any firm access restrictions. The provider needs to confirm an available window; a preference entered in a message is not a reserved slot. Ask whether any information is still needed before the appointment can be treated as confirmed.

Plan for the assessment to require appropriate access to the appliance and its installation. The exact duration cannot always be known from the symptom alone. Avoid assuming that the visit must finish within a short gap between other commitments unless that limitation has been discussed.

If a deadline matters, explain why: a move, a property handover or a period when the home is occupied. Ask what can realistically be confirmed. Availability, diagnosis and suitable parts all affect the plan, so an attendance window should not be confused with a guaranteed repair-completion time.

Clarify charges before you commit time and access

FixMyWasher’s stated visiting charge is ₹200 if no repair is carried out. Inspection charges depend on location. Ask which amounts apply to your booking, what the initial assessment includes and how those amounts are treated if a repair is approved.

Do not assume an automatic deduction, free inspection or complete repair at the visiting amount. The repair quotation depends on the actual finding, brand, model, part, labour and access. A phone description may help plan an assessment without supporting a fixed final repair price.

If the arrangement involves an additional trip, transport or another separately priced task, ask for those terms before proceeding. Keep the agreed first-visit terms in the confirmation so the person at home and the person paying understand the same arrangement.

Identify who opens the door and who approves the work

The access contact and the approval contact may be different people. Say who will be at the property and who can authorise a quotation. If you are arranging service for a parent, tenant or another household, use the machine’s actual location rather than your own current location.

Make sure the person providing access knows the original concern and where the appliance is installed. They should not have to infer whether additional work is authorised. The approval contact should be reachable at the stage when the findings and quotation are available.

Where approval cannot be given during the visit, discuss an assessment-only arrangement in advance. This can prevent an avoidable misunderstanding about completion. Do not share more personal information than is needed to arrange the work; the full service address can be exchanged privately after the basic details are agreed.

Organise entry without assuming building procedures

Tell the provider about security registration, permitted work hours, visitor parking, lift restrictions or access through a locked utility area. Follow the actual building’s procedure; there is no single entry rule that applies to every Bangalore apartment.

Ask what visitor details are available for registration and when they can be provided. Use the building’s established process and keep the access contact informed of changes. If the building requires an owner or resident to approve entry, arrange that before the visit window.

Mention stairs, narrow access, a stacked machine or a washer enclosed in cabinetry. These conditions can affect safe access and the work plan. Do not move heavy equipment or dismantle a cabinet yourself to make the appointment appear ready. Discuss the constraint so the appropriate next step can be arranged.

Prepare information and ordinary access, not a DIY diagnosis

Keep the invoice or model record, complete displayed code and relevant previous repair details ready. Clear ordinary household items from the approach only where safe. Tell the provider if laundry or water remains inside a locked machine.

You do not need to run a fresh cycle before the visit, force a door open or remove a filter to qualify for an appointment. Procedures depend on the model and current condition. If a safe photograph already shows the complaint, share it without recreating the problem.

Report water near electrical equipment, shock, burning, smoke or repeated tripping before the appointment. Stop use and seek appropriate urgent help where required. An ordinary booking should not delay action on an immediate hazard, and the household should not touch wet electrical fittings or attempt live testing.

If your availability or the service arrangement changes

Update the established booking contact as soon as you know access will be unavailable, the address has changed or the machine’s condition is different. Give the original appointment details and the new constraint so the next arrangement can be understood clearly.

Ask for a replacement window to be confirmed. Do not assume that replying with a different preferred time automatically reschedules the visit. If the provider reports a delay, clarify the revised plan with the person who will be at home.

Any cancellation, rescheduling or unsuccessful-access terms should be confirmed with the provider; this page does not invent a fixed fee or universal notice period. Keeping the change in the same conversation helps avoid two people acting on different versions of the appointment.

Keep assessment and repair approval as separate decisions

At the visit, explain the original symptom and any changes since booking. After assessment, ask for the finding, proposed work and complete amount. A confirmed assessment appointment should not be treated as unrestricted approval for component replacement.

If a new part is proposed, ask what item will be supplied, whether it matches the full model and which written terms apply. If the existing component is to be repaired, clarify that method and its coverage separately. Ask whether the proposed work can be completed at home or needs another specifically agreed arrangement.

Where the scope changes, request the revised explanation and total before approving further work. If you choose not to proceed, keep the findings and settle the applicable agreed assessment or visit terms. The useful outcome is a clear decision rather than uncertainty over what was authorised.

When the repair needs a part or another appointment

A return visit should have a stated purpose: fitting an identified component, completing agreed work or carrying out a further assessment. Ask what must happen before it can be scheduled and which part of the timing is still uncertain.

If sourcing is required, clarify availability, any advance and the next update. Do not treat a possible delivery estimate as a confirmed return appointment. Once the relevant dependency is resolved, confirm the window, access arrangements and applicable charges again.

Keep the first visit’s finding and approved scope attached to the follow-up. If a different person attends, the household should still have a record of the same model, complaint and authorised work. Any newly proposed task needs its own explanation rather than being assumed part of an open-ended booking.

Finish with a record of the result and next contact

Before the visit closes, ask what was completed, what checks were performed and whether anything remains pending. Keep the invoice, component description and written terms. If no repair was performed, retain the assessment outcome rather than assuming a service has been completed.

For an unresolved concern, agree the exact next step and who will provide the update. A clear question such as “Has the compatible part been confirmed?” is easier to follow than restarting the entire enquiry each time.

If the original symptom returns, refer to the job details and describe the current behaviour. The relevant coverage and revisit terms determine the follow-up arrangement. A completed booking record helps connect the new report to the actual work without promising that every future issue is included.

Visit charges and the price of the actual work

The visiting charge is ₹200 if no repair is carried out. Inspection charges depend on location. Confirm the applicable booking and inspection amounts, and how they are treated if you approve work, before scheduling. The ₹200 visiting amount is not the price of a complete repair or a promise of a free inspection.

The repair total depends on the brand, full model, diagnosed fault, required part, labour and access. Ask for the complete payable amount before approval, including any separately agreed transport, additional visit or tax. A symptom described on the phone can guide the enquiry but may not support a fixed final price.

Keep the diagnosis and quotation together. If the scope changes after assessment, ask for the revised explanation and total before additional work begins. Any property plumbing or electrical work must be identified and priced separately where required.

New parts, compatibility and your one-year warranty

New parts supplied by FixMyWasher carry a one-year warranty. The invoice should identify the covered component and written terms. This is not automatically a warranty on the entire washing machine, every future fault or the property installation.

Ask whether a proposed component is manufacturer-original, a compatible alternative or repair of the existing assembly. These are different offers. Confirm the full model match, condition, sourcing and installed price before approving it. A “universal” description alone does not establish suitability.

Labour, workmanship, repairs to existing components and revisit terms need separate confirmation. Keep the invoice and contact the provider with the original job details if a concern returns. Where manufacturer warranty or a service plan may still apply, check that route before authorising independent work.

Prepare access without opening or moving the machine

Keep the model from your invoice or an existing safe label photograph, the complete error message and any previous repair record ready. Clear ordinary access only where it is safe. Tell us about stairs, a stacked installation, a tight enclosure or a locked utility area before the appointment.

Do not dismantle panels, force a locked door, tilt a heavy washer or recreate a dangerous symptom for a video. If there is a shock, smoke, burning, severe movement or water near electrics, stop use and keep away; arrange the appropriate urgent help before a routine appliance visit. Use an isolation control only if it is safely accessible without approaching the hazard.

Safe inspection may require a qualified electrician, plumber or building team first. A washing-machine booking does not automatically include property wiring, earthing or plumbing correction.

Questions customers ask

How do I request a washing-machine repair appointment?

Call or send your Bangalore locality, machine type, model and concern through WhatsApp, then confirm scope, charges and an available visit window.

Does submitting the form book a technician automatically?

No. It prepares a WhatsApp draft for you to review and send; a reply is needed to confirm an appointment.

Can I choose a preferred time?

You can state a preference. The provider must confirm availability before it becomes an agreed window.

What if I do not know the full model?

Explain that and share available invoice or identification details safely. Exact-model support may still need confirmation.

Do I need to provide my full address immediately?

Start with locality or PIN code; exchange the full service address privately as the appointment is agreed.

Can I arrange the visit for someone else?

Yes. Provide the actual machine location and identify who gives access and who approves the work.

Is the ₹200 visiting charge the total repair price?

No. It applies if no repair is carried out. Inspection charges depend on location, and the diagnosed repair has its own quotation.

Do I have to empty a locked washer before the visit?

Do not force it open or follow an unsuitable procedure. Tell the provider that water or laundry remains inside.

What should I do if I cannot provide access?

Contact the established booking contact promptly and confirm a revised arrangement and any applicable terms.

Will the repair finish at the first appointment?

That depends on the finding, suitable parts, access and scope. No universal first-visit completion promise is made.

How is a return visit arranged?

Confirm its purpose, dependencies, window, access contact and applicable charges.

What should I keep after the appointment?

The assessment or repair outcome, invoice, approved scope, component details and written coverage.
