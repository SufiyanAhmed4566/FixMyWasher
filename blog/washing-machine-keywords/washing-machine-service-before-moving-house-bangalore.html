Decide what you need before asking for a moving service

If the washer works normally, your enquiry may concern model-specific preparation rather than repair. If it already leaks, stops or vibrates unusually, describe that as a separate fault assessment. A general request to service it before moving can conceal two different jobs.

Say when the machine last worked normally and whether it will be stored before installation elsewhere. Mention an upcoming property handover or limited access window. The provider can discuss an appropriate appointment, but the moving deadline does not guarantee parts availability or completion of an unknown repair.

Ask what the accepted service includes. Preparing an appliance does not by itself include packing materials, a vehicle or safe carrying through stairs. A useful plan names the person responsible for each task and keeps the appliance’s condition record with the move.

Record known condition without staging a test

Keep the full model, available invoice and earlier service records. Note any current complaint and the last known normal operation. Safe photographs of the exterior and existing connections can help document what was visible before movement, without proving the condition of hidden components.

Do not run a fresh cycle simply to create evidence if the machine has a suspected unsafe fault. A description of the last observed behaviour is enough to begin an assessment enquiry. If the washer has been unused for a long period, say so rather than describing it as tested.

Share the same factual history with the assessor and the person coordinating the move. This makes later discussion more accurate if a problem appears at the new property. Timing alone should not be used to decide whether a fault existed before transport or arose afterward.

Use the transport instructions for the actual appliance

Transport restraints and preparation procedures are model-specific. Some front-load models use transit bolts that must be handled according to their instructions. Do not assume every washer has the same arrangement, bolt count or preparation sequence.

Ask the appropriate professional to identify the required preparation and accessories before the moving day. If original transport items are missing, explain that early and confirm the correct model-specific solution. Do not improvise with ordinary bolts, wedges or packing placed inside the appliance.

Use the manufacturer’s official support route for the correct manual. For LG appliances, the official India support hub is one source for product assistance. This is a reference route, not an affiliation with FixMyWasher, and it does not replace the instructions for the particular model being moved.

Plan around retained water, a locked door or unfinished laundry

Tell the service provider if water or laundry remains inside or if the door is locked. Those conditions should be addressed through the appropriate model-specific process before transport is planned, rather than discovered when movers arrive.

Do not force the door, tip the machine to empty it or remove a filter without considering the model and current condition. Water may create additional risks around electrical equipment or the moving route. Ask for appropriate professional assistance where needed.

If the washer has an active leak or another hazardous condition, stop use and arrange suitable help. Moving-day pressure does not make an unsafe shortcut appropriate. Keep the assessor informed of the current state so the agreed task reflects what actually needs to happen.

Clarify disconnection and property responsibilities

Ask who will handle safe disconnection of the appliance and what happens to the property’s connection points afterward. The accepted appliance task may not include plumbing alteration, electrical correction or making changes required by a landlord or building team.

Mention inaccessible taps, fixed cabinetry, damaged fittings or an unusual installation before the visit. Do not pull a washer away from the wall to find out what is behind it if that cannot be done safely. A photograph from a normal accessible position may be enough to describe the constraint.

If separate property work is needed, agree that with the appropriate qualified professional and responsible person. Keep its charges and completion separate from the machine assessment. A clear division prevents the final property handover from relying on an assumption about work that nobody accepted.

Discuss stairs, tight access and stacked appliances early

Tell the moving provider about stairs, lift restrictions, narrow doorways and any equipment stacked above the washer. The appliance service contact also needs to know if these conditions affect safe preparation or access.

Do not remove a stacked dryer, lift the machine with improvised straps or attempt to shift it alone to speed up the appointment. Handling needs a suitable plan and people with the appropriate capability. This guide does not provide a universal lifting or transport-position instruction.

Confirm whether the mover will accept the appliance only after preparation is complete and how that handover will be recorded. If the service visit is delayed or a fault changes the plan, update the mover instead of assuming preparation can be skipped because a vehicle is waiting.

Keep the machine’s accessories and documents identifiable

Prepare a list of items that travel with the appliance: available transport accessories, manual, service records and any detachable items identified through the proper preparation process. Label the storage or packet so the destination installer can find them.

Do not place loose parts inside the machine as a makeshift storage method unless the actual manufacturer instructions permit it. Keep model-specific items distinct from unrelated household fittings; a similar-looking connector is not proof of compatibility.

Record any item that is missing and the agreed next step. The destination team should not discover an unresolved preparation issue only when the washer is about to be used. A short handover note can preserve these details even if different family members coordinate the two ends of the move.

If an existing fault is found, compare the timing options

Ask what the assessment establishes and whether the proposed repair can realistically be completed before the move. If a part is required, confirm actual compatibility and availability rather than relying on a hopeful estimate.

You may need to decide between completing supported work before movement and arranging assessment or repair at the destination. The decision depends on condition, access, timing and the agreed providers; this page does not prescribe one route for every machine.

Keep the finding and any unfinished work clearly documented. Do not describe a machine awaiting a part as repaired just because an assessment charge was paid. If moving with an unresolved condition is being considered, obtain appropriate advice about preparation and handling before proceeding.

Leave room for dependencies in the schedule

Request a preparation or assessment window with the actual moving date and any property access deadline. Confirm the appointment separately from the mover’s schedule. A preferred date in an enquiry is not a reserved visit.

Ask what must be ready beforehand: model information, required accessories, access contact and approval for the accepted task. If a dependency remains unresolved, clarify whether the visit can still achieve its purpose or needs a different arrangement.

Inspection charges depend on location, and the stated visiting charge is ₹200 if no repair is carried out. A preparation-only or installation-related task needs its own clearly agreed scope and price; do not treat the no-repair visiting amount as an all-inclusive relocation fee.

Send a useful handover note to the new home

The destination record should identify the machine, known condition, preparation completed, accessories supplied and any unresolved work. Include the appropriate installer or support contact and the planned next step.

Reinstallation is a separate stage. Do not assume the washer is ready to operate merely because it has arrived without obvious exterior damage. The exact model instructions and new installation conditions still need to be addressed by the appropriate person.

If the new home is outside Bangalore, confirm destination support separately; this enquiry is for Bangalore service arrangements. A clear final note lets the next provider work from an accurate history rather than guessing what happened before and during the move.

Visit charges and the price of the actual work

The visiting charge is ₹200 if no repair is carried out. Inspection charges depend on location. Confirm the applicable booking and inspection amounts, and how they are treated if you approve work, before scheduling. The ₹200 visiting amount is not the price of a complete repair or a promise of a free inspection.

The repair total depends on the brand, full model, diagnosed fault, required part, labour and access. Ask for the complete payable amount before approval, including any separately agreed transport, additional visit or tax. A symptom described on the phone can guide the enquiry but may not support a fixed final price.

Keep the diagnosis and quotation together. If the scope changes after assessment, ask for the revised explanation and total before additional work begins. Any property plumbing or electrical work must be identified and priced separately where required.

New parts, compatibility and your one-year warranty

New parts supplied by FixMyWasher carry a one-year warranty. The invoice should identify the covered component and written terms. This is not automatically a warranty on the entire washing machine, every future fault or the property installation.

Ask whether a proposed component is manufacturer-original, a compatible alternative or repair of the existing assembly. These are different offers. Confirm the full model match, condition, sourcing and installed price before approving it. A “universal” description alone does not establish suitability.

Labour, workmanship, repairs to existing components and revisit terms need separate confirmation. Keep the invoice and contact the provider with the original job details if a concern returns. Where manufacturer warranty or a service plan may still apply, check that route before authorising independent work.

Prepare access without opening or moving the machine

Keep the model from your invoice or an existing safe label photograph, the complete error message and any previous repair record ready. Clear ordinary access only where it is safe. Tell us about stairs, a stacked installation, a tight enclosure or a locked utility area before the appointment.

Do not dismantle panels, force a locked door, tilt a heavy washer or recreate a dangerous symptom for a video. If there is a shock, smoke, burning, severe movement or water near electrics, stop use and keep away; arrange the appropriate urgent help before a routine appliance visit. Use an isolation control only if it is safely accessible without approaching the hazard.

Safe inspection may require a qualified electrician, plumber or building team first. A washing-machine booking does not automatically include property wiring, earthing or plumbing correction.

Questions customers ask

Do I need a repair service before every move?

Not necessarily. Clarify whether you need fault assessment, transport preparation or both.

Does this booking include packing and transport?

Not automatically. Those tasks and any carrying or storage arrangement must be specifically confirmed.

What information should I provide first?

Full model, current condition, Bangalore locality, moving date and access constraints.

Are transit bolts the same for every washer?

No. Preparation and restraints depend on the exact model and its instructions.

What if original transport accessories are missing?

Explain that early and confirm the correct model-specific solution with the appropriate provider.

Should I run a cycle to prove the machine works before moving?

Do not recreate a fault or run a machine with a suspected unsafe condition. Share the last known operation honestly.

What if water or laundry is still inside?

Report it before the move and arrange the appropriate safe procedure; do not force or tip the washer.

Can movers assume the service provider handles all disconnection?

No. Agree the accepted appliance and property tasks explicitly.

Does ₹200 cover the whole relocation service?

No. It is the stated visiting charge if no repair is carried out; preparation and other work need their own confirmed terms.

Should an existing fault be recorded before transport?

Yes. Keep the observed symptom and any actual assessment finding with the machine’s history.

Is destination installation automatically included?

No. Confirm a separate installation arrangement and appropriate checks.

What if the destination is outside Bangalore?

Arrange destination support separately; this page concerns Bangalore service enquiries.
