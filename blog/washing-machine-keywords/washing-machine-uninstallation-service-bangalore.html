Describe the task instead of only saying removal

Removal can mean disconnecting the washer, moving it a short distance, carrying it down stairs, transporting it or taking it away permanently. Those are different responsibilities. List the specific tasks you need so a quote reflects the actual job.

For a property handover, confirm what must remain at the location and who owns the appliance. In a rental home, agree the owner’s instructions before arranging work on a supplied washer or changing fixed connections.

For an exchange, check whether the collection team expects the machine to be ready for pickup. A collection date alone does not confirm who disconnects it. Resolving this early avoids two providers arriving with different assumptions.

Report its present state accurately

Tell the provider when it was last used, whether the cycle completed and whether laundry or water remains inside. Add any error message and explain whether the door opens normally. You do not need to make the machine run to establish these details.

A washer stopped mid-cycle can require a different preparation plan from an empty working one. The provider should confirm whether the accepted visit can address that state safely or whether diagnosis is needed first.

If there is an electrical concern, severe leak or unstable installation, describe it without approaching the hazard. Do not treat the scheduled uninstallation time as a reason to keep using a machine that may be unsafe.

Make concealed connections visible in the booking information

Explain whether the tap, socket and drain point are accessible or hidden behind cabinetry, another appliance or a fitted panel. A photograph taken from an ordinary safe position is useful; pulling the washer out to obtain one is not required.

Where a connection cannot be reached without moving equipment or altering furniture, that access task needs planning. Ask who will provide it, what owner permission is needed and whether it changes the appointment or price.

A seized tap, damaged fitting or questionable electrical connection may require the appropriate property professional. An appliance technician should not be assumed to include repair of the building’s services within a simple disconnection quote.

Separate transport preparation from transport

If the washer is moving, provide the full model and say whether its original transport fittings and manual are available. Appropriate transport preparation depends on the appliance. Do not improvise restraints, use random bolts or assume every model needs the same arrangement.

Agree who prepares the appliance and who takes responsibility for handling it afterward. The person disconnecting it may not be the same person packing, lifting or transporting it. Each stage should have an understood handover.

If a required item is missing, raise this before moving day. A rushed schedule should not substitute for suitable preparation. Ask the responsible provider how the missing item affects the plan rather than treating disconnection alone as proof that the washer is ready for any journey.

Agree how the vacated space will be left

Ask the provider to describe the safe final state of the connections and any remaining property work. The handover should make clear whether the appliance is simply disconnected or whether additional agreed work at the vacated point has been completed.

Do not leave responsibilities implied. For example, if a plumber is required before the water supply arrangement can be left as intended, identify who will arrange that visit. If a fixed electrical connection is involved, confirm the appropriate qualified professional.

Record visible pre-existing damage or leakage before work where it can be observed safely. A basic condition record helps distinguish the original state from the result of the accepted job without turning the visit into a formal property survey.

Plan stairs, lifts and waiting space

Give the floor number, lift availability and restrictions on moving heavy items. Mention narrow turns, steps into the utility area and any upper appliance or counter that affects access. These details can determine whether the requested task can be accepted.

Confirm whether the machine will remain near its original location or be handed to movers immediately. The waiting space should be agreed with the household or building team, rather than selected after disconnection blocks an exit or shared passage.

Do not ask an unprepared household member to assist with lifting. Handling requires a suitable team and method. A single-person service appointment should not be treated as an unlimited carrying service.

Clarify the price and what happens if work cannot proceed

Ask for the amount for the confirmed uninstallation scope and identify separately priced handling, access, transport preparation or extra visits. A quote based on an empty accessible machine may need revision when a different condition is disclosed.

The stated ₹200 visiting charge applies if no repair is carried out; it does not establish a universal uninstallation price. Confirm the applicable terms for a disconnection-only enquiry, including any location-dependent inspection charge.

If the work stops because a connection is unsafe or access is unavailable, ask what has been completed and what remains. Get approval for any revised scope before proceeding. The next provider should receive a clear status, not an assumption that everything is ready.

Keep a short uninstallation record

Record the model, date, agreed work, observed condition and person receiving the appliance. List the accessories handed over and where transport fittings are kept, when relevant. If the machine is left on site, identify the agreed location.

For an exchange or sale, keep collection paperwork separate from the uninstallation invoice. Disconnection does not determine value, prove working condition or settle any buyer’s complaint about a later fault.

For storage or renovation, retain a note of why the washer was disconnected and whether any unresolved fault existed. That history is useful when arranging reconnection and prevents a pre-existing problem from being forgotten during the gap.

Three uninstallation decisions

These situations illustrate decisions, not customer testimonials or claims about completed jobs.

Exchange pickup is tomorrow but the washer is still connected

Confirm collection requirements and arrange the actual disconnection task. Do not rely on a pickup team to accept extra work on arrival.

A renovation team wants the washer moved behind another appliance

Agree a suitable waiting location and handling responsibility before disconnection. Keep the reconnection task as a later confirmed step.

The machine is empty but the tap cannot be safely accessed

Explain the obstruction. Arrange appropriate access or property work instead of treating the quote as permission to force a connection.

Visit charges and the price of the actual work

The visiting charge is ₹200 if no repair is carried out. Inspection charges depend on location. Confirm the applicable booking and inspection amounts, and how they are treated if you approve work, before scheduling. The ₹200 visiting amount is not the price of a complete repair or a promise of a free inspection.

The repair total depends on the brand, full model, diagnosed fault, required part, labour and access. Ask for the complete payable amount before approval, including any separately agreed transport, additional visit or tax. A symptom described on the phone can guide the enquiry but may not support a fixed final price.

Keep the diagnosis and quotation together. If the scope changes after assessment, ask for the revised explanation and total before additional work begins. Any property plumbing or electrical work must be identified and priced separately where required.

New parts, compatibility and your one-year warranty

New parts supplied by FixMyWasher carry a one-year warranty. The invoice should identify the covered component and written terms. This is not automatically a warranty on the entire washing machine, every future fault or the property installation.

Ask whether a proposed component is manufacturer-original, a compatible alternative or repair of the existing assembly. These are different offers. Confirm the full model match, condition, sourcing and installed price before approving it. A “universal” description alone does not establish suitability.

Labour, workmanship, repairs to existing components and revisit terms need separate confirmation. Keep the invoice and contact the provider with the original job details if a concern returns. Where manufacturer warranty or a service plan may still apply, check that route before authorising independent work.

Prepare access without opening or moving the machine

Keep the model from your invoice or an existing safe label photograph, the complete error message and any previous repair record ready. Clear ordinary access only where it is safe. Tell us about stairs, a stacked installation, a tight enclosure or a locked utility area before the appointment.

Do not dismantle panels, force a locked door, tilt a heavy washer or recreate a dangerous symptom for a video. If there is a shock, smoke, burning, severe movement or water near electrics, stop use and keep away; arrange the appropriate urgent help before a routine appliance visit. Use an isolation control only if it is safely accessible without approaching the hazard.

Safe inspection may require a qualified electrician, plumber or building team first. A washing-machine booking does not automatically include property wiring, earthing or plumbing correction.

Questions customers ask

Is uninstallation the same as moving the washer?

No. Disconnection, carrying, packing, transport and reinstallation are separate tasks unless expressly combined.

Can I request uninstallation without a repair?

Yes, as an enquiry. Confirm acceptance and the applicable disconnection-only charges before booking.

What information is needed first?

Full model, Bangalore locality, present condition, connection access and the machine’s next destination.

What if water or laundry remains inside?

Tell the provider in advance. Safe preparation may require additional assessment before disconnection.

Should I disconnect it myself before the visit?

Do not undertake unsafe or unfamiliar work. Agree what the professional will handle.

Are transport bolts needed for every machine?

Requirements vary; use the actual model’s instructions rather than a universal assumption.

Will the provider carry the washer downstairs?

Only if handling has been specifically accepted with the necessary team and access information.

Is disposal included?

No automatic disposal service is promised. Agree collection and ownership separately.

Who fixes a damaged property tap?

Confirm a suitable plumbing professional and a separate scope where required.

Does disconnection certify the washer as working?

No. A condition assessment is a different agreed task.

What should be recorded at handover?

Work completed, remaining tasks, appliance condition, accessories and who receives the machine.

Can reconnection be arranged later?

Enquire with the model, destination and installation requirements; confirm availability and charges separately.
