A first message that is easy to act on

Write a short message with the service locality, machine type, full model if known and current concern. Include the stage at which the problem appears and the complete displayed code. You do not need to name a failed part.

A useful example is: “Hello, I need a washing-machine assessment in BTM Layout. It is a fully automatic front loader. Water remains after the programme stops. I can share the full model from the invoice. Please confirm support, visit availability and applicable charges.” This is an illustrative enquiry, not a report of a completed job.

If you are arranging the visit for someone else, say that and identify the access and approval arrangements privately. The goal is enough information to discuss the next step, not a long message containing every personal detail or speculative repair suggestion.

Choose photos that answer a specific question

An existing image of the complete displayed code or a safely visible model label can help identify the enquiry. A normal view of a tight utility space can explain an access constraint. Ask what image would be useful if the provider needs more context.

Do not photograph private household documents in full when only the model or relevant invoice detail is needed. Share the minimum information necessary for the service discussion. Avoid including other people’s personal belongings or faces where they are unrelated to the appliance concern.

Photos cannot establish every internal condition or replace a relevant assessment. If a part is suggested from a picture, clarify whether it is a possibility or a confirmed finding. The actual repair quotation may depend on an inspection of the machine.

Use a video only when it can be obtained safely

A video already recorded during ordinary use may help describe an intermittent sound or programme behaviour. Explain when it was captured and whether the machine’s condition has changed since then. A short relevant clip can be more useful than a long recording with no context.

Do not recreate a leak, severe movement, shock, smoke, burning or repeated tripping to obtain evidence. Stop use and arrange appropriate help for unsafe conditions. Do not remove covers, bypass a lock or place a phone near moving parts.

If no safe video exists, describe the observation in words. A service enquiry should not require the household to provoke a dangerous fault. The professional can choose the appropriate checks when the assessment is arranged.

Treat remote suggestions according to their certainty

A conversation can help establish model support, urgency and the likely assessment needed. It may also identify information still missing. It cannot always establish the failed component or final repair total from a short description.

Ask whether a suggested cause is provisional and what needs to be checked before replacement is recommended. A familiar error message can guide investigation without proving that one part is faulty in every model.

Keep the distinction in the chat record. If a later inspection changes the proposed scope, ask for the finding and revised quote. The useful purpose of WhatsApp is to preserve a clear conversation, not to turn every early suggestion into a promise about diagnosis, price or completion.

What a reply must clarify before you plan around the visit

Confirm the accepted model and job, actual service locality, visit window and applicable assessment terms. Identify the person providing access and any building entry requirement. If a detail remains unresolved, ask whether the appointment is confirmed or still pending.

A read receipt or a brief acknowledgement does not necessarily reserve attendance. Likewise, sending your preferred evening or Sunday does not establish that a technician is available then. Keep the agreed window explicit.

If timing is urgent, explain the practical constraint and ask for the earliest actual option. Do not rely on an unanswered message for an immediate hazard. The chat should accurately distinguish a service enquiry from confirmed attendance and from eventual repair completion.

Keep the visit terms and repair quote easy to find

Ask for the applicable visit and inspection terms before booking. The stated visiting charge is ₹200 if no repair is carried out; inspection charges depend on location. Clarify how those amounts apply if repair is approved and whether other tasks have separate charges.

After assessment, keep the finding, proposed work and complete amount together. Ask for a clearly described component and its condition where replacement is proposed. A single unexplained number in a chat can be difficult to compare later.

If the provider offers options, identify which one you approve. Do not leave a conversation in which several totals are discussed but nobody can tell which scope was selected. Any later change needs a revised explanation and approval.

Correct a detail without starting a conflicting second request

Reply in the existing conversation when the model, address, symptom or preferred time changes. State the correction plainly, such as “The service address is in the new locality, not the earlier one.” Include the original appointment reference or date if available.

Ask for confirmation if the change affects attendance or charges. Sending a correction does not guarantee that the earlier visit plan has been replaced. Keep the household access contact informed of the latest agreed arrangement.

Avoid having several family members send different instructions without a coordinator. If another person takes over the conversation, preserve the model, finding and selected scope so the provider is not forced to reconstruct the job from contradictory messages.

Follow up with the exact question still outstanding

If an enquiry is awaiting a response, a concise follow-up can restate the machine, locality and unresolved question. For example, ask whether a visit window is available or whether the exact model is supported. Repeatedly sending the same long message can obscure new information.

If a confirmed appointment or part order is pending, refer to that specific stage. “Has the compatible part been confirmed?” is different from “Can someone visit?” Keeping the stage clear helps the conversation move toward a concrete next action.

Use the direct call option when you need to clarify an arrangement and have not established it through chat. Neither a message nor a call attempt is a guaranteed emergency response; immediate hazards require appropriate urgent assistance.

Visit charges and the price of the actual work

The visiting charge is ₹200 if no repair is carried out. Inspection charges depend on location. Confirm the applicable booking and inspection amounts, and how they are treated if you approve work, before scheduling. The ₹200 visiting amount is not the price of a complete repair or a promise of a free inspection.

The repair total depends on the brand, full model, diagnosed fault, required part, labour and access. Ask for the complete payable amount before approval, including any separately agreed transport, additional visit or tax. A symptom described on the phone can guide the enquiry but may not support a fixed final price.

Keep the diagnosis and quotation together. If the scope changes after assessment, ask for the revised explanation and total before additional work begins. Any property plumbing or electrical work must be identified and priced separately where required.

New parts, compatibility and your one-year warranty

New parts supplied by FixMyWasher carry a one-year warranty. The invoice should identify the covered component and written terms. This is not automatically a warranty on the entire washing machine, every future fault or the property installation.

Ask whether a proposed component is manufacturer-original, a compatible alternative or repair of the existing assembly. These are different offers. Confirm the full model match, condition, sourcing and installed price before approving it. A “universal” description alone does not establish suitability.

Labour, workmanship, repairs to existing components and revisit terms need separate confirmation. Keep the invoice and contact the provider with the original job details if a concern returns. Where manufacturer warranty or a service plan may still apply, check that route before authorising independent work.

Prepare access without opening or moving the machine

Keep the model from your invoice or an existing safe label photograph, the complete error message and any previous repair record ready. Clear ordinary access only where it is safe. Tell us about stairs, a stacked installation, a tight enclosure or a locked utility area before the appointment.

Do not dismantle panels, force a locked door, tilt a heavy washer or recreate a dangerous symptom for a video. If there is a shock, smoke, burning, severe movement or water near electrics, stop use and keep away; arrange the appropriate urgent help before a routine appliance visit. Use an isolation control only if it is safely accessible without approaching the hazard.

Safe inspection may require a qualified electrician, plumber or building team first. A washing-machine booking does not automatically include property wiring, earthing or plumbing correction.
