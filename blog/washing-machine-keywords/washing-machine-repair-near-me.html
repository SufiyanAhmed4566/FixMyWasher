Start with the service location, not just “near me”

A nearby-search phrase does not tell the provider which building needs service. Send the Bangalore locality or PIN code where the washer is installed. If the location is unfamiliar or on an area boundary, add a useful landmark during the private conversation.

If you are booking for a parent, tenant or another property, say that clearly. Your phone’s current location may have nothing to do with the service address. Agree who will be at the property and which person can approve the repair.

Do not publish your full address or personal contact details in a public comment to obtain a booking. The page’s optional message builder needs only a locality; the complete address can be shared privately once the appropriate service route and appointment are being arranged.

Choose the contact route that suits the information

A call is useful when you want to explain the concern and discuss availability. WhatsApp can keep the model, complete display message, location and agreed terms together. Existing safe photographs or a previous quotation can be added in the private chat if needed.

The optional form on this page prepares a WhatsApp draft for you to review and send. It does not take payment, store the details on this page or reserve a slot automatically. You remain responsible for sending the message and checking the reply.

Avoid sending several conflicting descriptions of suspected parts. A short factual sequence is more useful: what the machine did, where it stopped and what the display showed. Mention any safety concern at the start so ordinary booking steps do not delay the appropriate help.

What a confirmed appointment should establish

Before arranging your day, confirm the service locality, visit window, exact-model support and applicable visiting or inspection terms. Ask what the first visit includes and which person should be available to discuss the findings.

An acknowledgement or a message marked as read does not by itself confirm attendance. Keep the actual agreement in the conversation and ask how any timing change will be communicated.

The initial appointment is for the agreed assessment or work. It does not automatically guarantee that a particular part is available or the repair will be finished during that visit. If completion timing matters, explain the requirement and ask what can realistically be confirmed at that stage.

Settle visit terms before a technician is on the way

The stated visiting charge is ₹200 if no repair is carried out. Inspection charges depend on location. Ask which amounts apply to your enquiry and how they are treated if you choose to proceed with the quoted repair.

If another visit, transport or a special arrangement is proposed, settle its terms explicitly. Do not assume that the word “nearby” makes every assessment free or that a low visiting figure includes all labour and parts.

The final repair amount depends on the diagnosis, brand, model, component and access. You should understand the complete payable repair quotation before approval. A booking enquiry should not require you to authorise an unknown component replacement in advance.

Plan apartment entry and ordinary access

If your building uses visitor approval, arrange the appropriate entry process for the confirmed appointment. Tell the provider about restricted service hours, stairs, lift access or a utility area that requires a key.

Keep the route to the machine clear where it is safe, but leave heavy movement and technical disconnection to the appropriate people. Mention if the appliance is stacked, built into a tight cabinet or installed where reaching the supply is difficult.

If water or damaged electrical equipment makes the area unsafe, report that before arrival. Making an appointment does not establish safe access, and the right first step may involve the building team, a qualified electrician or a plumber.

Booking for a landlord, tenant or family member

Agree who owns the appliance, who will be present and who can approve the quotation. Those can be three different people. Provide a clear contact arrangement so the findings do not have to pass through several incomplete messages during the visit.

Explain whether the owner has a spending limit or wants a written estimate before deciding. Keep this requirement separate from the visiting and inspection terms, which should already be understood.

If the machine might need a component ordered or further work away from the property, obtain the appropriate approval before movement or sourcing. A person who can open the door is not automatically authorised to approve every repair or hand over the appliance.

What to ask during the assessment

Ask the technician to explain the finding and connect it to the problem you reported. If a part is proposed, ask for its identity, compatibility, source and installed total. If more investigation is needed, ask what remains uncertain and what the next step costs.

You do not need to perform electrical measurements or dismantle the machine to participate in the decision. Your role is to understand the scope and approve the work you choose.

If a property issue is identified, clarify who will resolve it. A repaired washer should not be returned to an unsuitable supply or an unresolved plumbing arrangement simply because the appliance portion of the quote has been completed.

Give approval for a defined repair

Keep the complete quotation together: finding, proposed correction, part if required, labour, additional visit or movement and the written terms. Ask about any difference between the initial estimate and the assessed scope.

If you need time to decide, clarify what charges apply for the completed assessment and what happens next. If the required component must be sourced, settle identification, availability, any advance and the follow-up appointment before ordering.

A clear approval avoids two common misunderstandings: expecting a complete repair for the visiting charge, or assuming that booking a visit authorises every further task. The agreed work should be specific enough that both sides know what completion means.

Before the technician leaves

Ask which relevant functional checks addressed the original concern and whether anything is still outstanding. A brief demonstration should be appropriate to the complaint; basic power alone does not explain a completed drainage or spin repair.

Keep the invoice, actual part description and one-year new-part warranty terms where applicable. Clarify labour, workmanship and revisit conditions separately. Ask how to report a recurrence with the original job details.

If there is a remaining restriction or another professional task, make it explicit before normal use. The useful handover is an understandable record of what was done and what you should do next.

Make follow-up easier with the original record

Save the model, invoice, reported concern and agreed terms together. If the machine behaves unexpectedly after the repair, describe the new event and whether it matches the original symptom. Include the job reference if one was provided.

Do not assume that every later interruption is the same fault, but do not lose the previous history either. A clear account helps the provider evaluate the connection and the applicable coverage.

If a shock, burning, severe movement or unsafe leak occurs, stop use and arrange the appropriate response. Avoid collecting a new video by exposing yourself or another person to the hazard.

Visit charges and the price of the actual work

The visiting charge is ₹200 if no repair is carried out. Inspection charges depend on location. Confirm the applicable booking and inspection amounts, and how they are treated if you approve work, before scheduling. The ₹200 visiting amount is not the price of a complete repair or a promise of a free inspection.

The repair total depends on the brand, full model, diagnosed fault, required part, labour and access. Ask for the complete payable amount before approval, including any separately agreed transport, additional visit or tax. A symptom described on the phone can guide the enquiry but may not support a fixed final price.

Keep the diagnosis and quotation together. If the scope changes after assessment, ask for the revised explanation and total before additional work begins. Any property plumbing or electrical work must be identified and priced separately where required.

New parts, compatibility and your one-year warranty

New parts supplied by FixMyWasher carry a one-year warranty. The invoice should identify the covered component and written terms. This is not automatically a warranty on the entire washing machine, every future fault or the property installation.

Ask whether a proposed component is manufacturer-original, a compatible alternative or repair of the existing assembly. These are different offers. Confirm the full model match, condition, sourcing and installed price before approving it. A “universal” description alone does not establish suitability.

Labour, workmanship, repairs to existing components and revisit terms need separate confirmation. Keep the invoice and contact the provider with the original job details if a concern returns. Where manufacturer warranty or a service plan may still apply, check that route before authorising independent work.

Prepare access without opening or moving the machine

Keep the model from your invoice or an existing safe label photograph, the complete error message and any previous repair record ready. Clear ordinary access only where it is safe. Tell us about stairs, a stacked installation, a tight enclosure or a locked utility area before the appointment.

Do not dismantle panels, force a locked door, tilt a heavy washer or recreate a dangerous symptom for a video. If there is a shock, smoke, burning, severe movement or water near electrics, stop use and keep away; arrange the appropriate urgent help before a routine appliance visit. Use an isolation control only if it is safely accessible without approaching the hazard.

Safe inspection may require a qualified electrician, plumber or building team first. A washing-machine booking does not automatically include property wiring, earthing or plumbing correction.
