Start with the complete regional identity

Use the invoice, manual cover or an existing safe label photograph to provide the model exactly as written, including suffixes. Add the country of purchase and approximate purchase date if known. Do not shorten the number to a familiar local model name.

If the washer came with a furnished home or was bought second-hand, state what you know and what is uncertain. A seller’s description such as European model is not a substitute for identification.

If the label is hidden, do not pull or dismantle the washer to find it. Explain the limitation. The provider can decide whether the available information is enough for an initial discussion or what must be established during an appropriate assessment.

Treat supply compatibility as a separate question

Do not assume that a plug fitting into an outlet, a similar voltage marking or past operation establishes overall suitability. The appliance’s documented requirements and installation need an appropriate professional review when compatibility is uncertain.

LG’s overseas-use guidance warns that appliances designed for one market may have operating or service limitations elsewhere. That does not determine the outcome for every imported washer; it explains why the exact variant and relevant manufacturer information matter.

Do not fit a plug adapter, improvised converter or changed wiring as a trial repair. If the installation or supply needs assessment, identify the responsible qualified professional before attempting operation.

Check the manufacturer route before independent work

Ask the manufacturer or original seller what support, if any, is available for the exact imported model in India. Keep any response with the model and region it refers to. Local sales of the same brand do not by themselves establish local coverage for an overseas purchase.

If you want independent assessment, provide that information rather than describing the washer simply as out of warranty. The provider needs to know whether an existing support route or restriction affects the work you are considering.

FixMyWasher is an independent service enquiry route. No manufacturer authorisation, international warranty acceptance or universal support for imported models is claimed here.

Confirm a part against the actual variant

A local model may look similar while using a different component or configuration. Ask the provider to establish the exact compatible item from the diagnosis and relevant identification, rather than ordering from appearance alone.

Clarify whether a proposed part is new, repaired, used or another condition. Confirm its source description, fitting acceptance and any model-specific setup that may be necessary. An item being available online does not establish that it is appropriate for your machine.

If an alternative is offered, ask what evidence supports suitability and what limitations remain. Do not authorise improvised connectors, safety bypasses or unsupported conversion simply because the original item is difficult to obtain.

Separate a sourcing enquiry from a confirmed delivery

Ask whether the part has been identified, whether availability is confirmed and what the quoted timeline depends on. These are different stages. A provider may be able to assess the appliance before knowing whether a repair item can be supplied.

If an order or advance is proposed, get the item description, total, next update and cancellation or return terms in writing. Any overseas sourcing arrangements need their own confirmed terms; this page does not promise import handling or a fixed delivery period.

Decide how long the household can reasonably wait before approving an open-ended process. Keep a date for reviewing the decision rather than allowing an uncertain parts enquiry to become an indefinite repair commitment.

Ask what can be established during assessment

Describe whether the machine ever worked normally in its current location and what changed before the fault. Include earlier repairs, installation changes and any conversion or adapter already present without attempting to alter it yourself.

Ask the provider what the accepted assessment can determine and what would need further documentation, a specialist or another professional. A diagnostic visit can have a useful outcome even if it identifies that local repair support is not feasible.

The result should distinguish a confirmed fault from an unresolved compatibility question. That distinction helps you decide whether to pursue repair, contact another support route or reconsider the appliance’s use.

Compare repair feasibility with the complete alternative

Use the assessed fault, full installed repair cost, sourcing uncertainty and expected completion plan to make a decision. Avoid comparing an unconfirmed overseas part listing with the complete cost of a locally installed replacement.

For replacement, consider delivery, installation, removal of the old appliance and whether the new model suits the available space. For repair, consider what remains uncertain after assessment and whether the provider can complete relevant checks.

Do not use a fixed percentage of original purchase price as the only rule. An imported appliance’s original price may bear little relation to current local repair support or the household’s practical needs.

Keep regional documentation with the completed job

Retain the original-model information, finding, approved work, component description and relevant completion outcome. If a limitation remains, have it recorded plainly.

The stated one-year warranty applies to new parts supplied by FixMyWasher under written terms. It should not be interpreted as an international manufacturer warranty or coverage for a customer-imported component. Ask separately about labour and revisit arrangements.

If you later sell or relocate the appliance, pass on the actual documentation and known limitations. A repair does not change the washer’s original market identity or establish universal compatibility with another location.

Three imported-model decisions

These situations illustrate decisions, not customer testimonials or claims about completed jobs.

The brand is common in India but the model suffix is unfamiliar

Keep the complete suffix and country of purchase. Ask about the specific variant instead of assuming support from the brand name.

An online seller offers a similar-looking replacement board

Have diagnosis and compatibility established before purchase. Appearance is not enough to approve a regional electronics substitution.

The washer stopped after a change to its supply arrangement

Report the change and arrange the appropriate compatibility assessment. Do not keep experimenting with adapters to see what works.

Visit charges and the price of the actual work

The visiting charge is ₹200 if no repair is carried out. Inspection charges depend on location. Confirm the applicable booking and inspection amounts, and how they are treated if you approve work, before scheduling. The ₹200 visiting amount is not the price of a complete repair or a promise of a free inspection.

The repair total depends on the brand, full model, diagnosed fault, required part, labour and access. Ask for the complete payable amount before approval, including any separately agreed transport, additional visit or tax. A symptom described on the phone can guide the enquiry but may not support a fixed final price.

Keep the diagnosis and quotation together. If the scope changes after assessment, ask for the revised explanation and total before additional work begins. Any property plumbing or electrical work must be identified and priced separately where required.

New parts, compatibility and your one-year warranty

New parts supplied by FixMyWasher carry a one-year warranty. The invoice should identify the covered component and written terms. This is not automatically a warranty on the entire washing machine, every future fault or the property installation.

Ask whether a proposed component is manufacturer-original, a compatible alternative or repair of the existing assembly. These are different offers. Confirm the full model match, condition, sourcing and installed price before approving it. A “universal” description alone does not establish suitability.

Labour, workmanship, repairs to existing components and revisit terms need separate confirmation. Keep the invoice and contact the provider with the original job details if a concern returns. Where manufacturer warranty or a service plan may still apply, check that route before authorising independent work.

Prepare access without opening or moving the machine

Keep the model from your invoice or an existing safe label photograph, the complete error message and any previous repair record ready. Clear ordinary access only where it is safe. Tell us about stairs, a stacked installation, a tight enclosure or a locked utility area before the appointment.

Do not dismantle panels, force a locked door, tilt a heavy washer or recreate a dangerous symptom for a video. If there is a shock, smoke, burning, severe movement or water near electrics, stop use and keep away; arrange the appropriate urgent help before a routine appliance visit. Use an isolation control only if it is safely accessible without approaching the hazard.

Safe inspection may require a qualified electrician, plumber or building team first. A washing-machine booking does not automatically include property wiring, earthing or plumbing correction.

Why is the model suffix important?

No. Give the history, but have any current compatibility or safety concern assessed.

Only after compatibility is established for the exact component and appliance.

No. Identification, confirmed availability and delivery are separate questions.

Does independent repair honour an overseas warranty?

Does a repaired machine become suitable for any supply?

No. Repair does not create universal electrical or installation compatibility.
