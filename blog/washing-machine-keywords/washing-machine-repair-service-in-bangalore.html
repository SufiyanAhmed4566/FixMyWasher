Choose the service task that matches the problem

A repair enquiry concerns a machine that is not operating as expected: it may fail to start, stop at a particular stage, leak or produce an abnormal sound. Cleaning, installation, relocation assistance and a pre-purchase check are different tasks, even when the same appliance is involved. Tell us which outcome you need.

If there are several concerns, list them separately. A washer can have a drainage fault and an unrelated damaged lid. The accepted quote should make clear whether both are included. Do not assume a general service charge covers every component, all cleaning, property connections and future visits.

Support is checked for the exact model and requested work. Mention a washer-dryer, imported model, commercial use or modified appliance before scheduling. The provider can then confirm the appropriate scope instead of discovering a material difference after arriving.

What happens between an enquiry and a confirmed visit

Send your locality or PIN code, complete model, machine type and concern. An existing invoice or safe model photograph is useful. Add your preferred time and any building-entry restriction. You do not need to send the full residential address publicly or include account passwords.

The enquiry is used to confirm supported assessment, visit availability and the applicable charges. Opening WhatsApp or completing this page’s message form does not by itself create a confirmed appointment. Wait for the agreed date or window and the required access arrangements.

If you are booking for someone else, name the person who can provide access and the person who can approve work. A resident’s availability and an owner’s approval are separate requirements. Establish both before the appointment to reduce an avoidable second visit.

Bangalore scheduling depends on the actual address and access

Tell us the locality accurately—for example, HSR Layout, Bellandur, Jayanagar, Rajajinagar or Whitefield—and provide the PIN code if the name could refer to more than one place. These examples help explain the booking details; they do not promise a nearby branch or an immediate arrival.

Mention apartment security procedures, parking or entry restrictions, stairs and utility-area access. If the appliance is stacked, enclosed or on a difficult platform, send an existing photograph if available and ask what handling can be supported.

Availability and inspection charges depend on the confirmed location and arrangements. Do not assume a general Bangalore enquiry guarantees the same arrival window or inspection amount at every address. The accepted appointment should reflect your actual site.

Expect a finding before a parts recommendation

An assessment should connect the reported symptom with the machine’s condition. Ask what has been confirmed, what is still uncertain and whether further checking is needed. A displayed code or a phone recording can guide the visit, but it is not a complete internal diagnosis.

If the fault does not occur during attendance, keep that limitation explicit. The provider can explain what functions were checked and what history would be useful next. An intermittent complaint should not be described as permanently resolved merely because a brief demonstration passed.

Where the cause involves a property drain, water supply or electrical installation, identify that dependency separately. Appliance work should not be used as a substitute for the appropriate building, plumbing or electrical correction.

Review the whole installed repair, not just the part price

Ask for the finding, proposed work, component description and complete payable amount. Check whether assessment, fitting, any agreed transport, additional visit and applicable tax are included. Compare quotations only when they cover the same scope and component condition.

Repairing an existing assembly, supplying a new manufacturer-original item and supplying an agreed compatible alternative are different offers. Ask which is proposed and why it is suitable for the full model. The word genuine should be supported by a clear description of what will actually be supplied.

If further assessment changes the diagnosis or scope, request a revised explanation and total before extra work proceeds. Keep optional care tasks visible as separate recommendations. A repair approval should not silently become permission for unrelated replacements.

Decide using the washer’s condition and your needs

A small, well-defined fault and a larger multi-component repair deserve different decisions. Consider the confirmed damage, other known problems, availability of suitable parts and the complete repair amount. The machine’s age is relevant context, but age alone does not prove that repair or replacement is the better choice.

Ask what result the accepted work is intended to achieve and which uncertainties remain. No responsible quotation needs to promise that the washer will never develop another fault. If a larger repair leaves unresolved concerns, keep those visible when comparing it with replacement.

If you seek a second opinion, provide the earlier findings and proposal. The useful comparison is whether each recommendation is supported and covers the same outcome, rather than choosing whichever names the cheapest single part.

Finish with an understandable job record

Ask what was found, what work was completed and which relevant checks were performed. The checks should relate to the original complaint: drainage after a drain repair, appropriate leak checks after water-path work, or the affected operating stage after a cycle interruption.

Record any condition that could not be reproduced and any task still assigned to a plumber, electrician, building team or another provider. Clarify whether the appliance is ready for ordinary use. A display that turns on does not establish every wash function, and a short visit does not guarantee future reliability.

Keep the invoice, component description, agreed terms and job contact together. If a concern returns, send that record with the new symptom and timing. This gives the provider a useful basis for discussing the next step and any applicable coverage.

Three ways a repair enquiry becomes clearer

These situations illustrate decisions, not customer testimonials or claims about completed jobs.

The washer is described as not drying

Clarify whether it is a washer-only model that leaves clothes wet after spin or a washer-dryer with a failed drying programme. The requested assessment changes with that distinction.

A repair is booked, but the machine is behind fixed cabinetry

Explain the enclosure beforehand. Access or cabinet handling should be agreed before the visit rather than assumed within a normal repair amount.

A low quote lists only a component

Ask for the installed total, compatibility, condition and checks. Compare that completed offer with other quotations before approval.

Visit charges and the price of the actual work

The visiting charge is ₹200 if no repair is carried out. Inspection charges depend on location. Confirm the applicable booking and inspection amounts, and how they are treated if you approve work, before scheduling. The ₹200 visiting amount is not the price of a complete repair or a promise of a free inspection.

The repair total depends on the brand, full model, diagnosed fault, required part, labour and access. Ask for the complete payable amount before approval, including any separately agreed transport, additional visit or tax. A symptom described on the phone can guide the enquiry but may not support a fixed final price.

Keep the diagnosis and quotation together. If the scope changes after assessment, ask for the revised explanation and total before additional work begins. Any property plumbing or electrical work must be identified and priced separately where required.

New parts, compatibility and your one-year warranty

New parts supplied by FixMyWasher carry a one-year warranty. The invoice should identify the covered component and written terms. This is not automatically a warranty on the entire washing machine, every future fault or the property installation.

Ask whether a proposed component is manufacturer-original, a compatible alternative or repair of the existing assembly. These are different offers. Confirm the full model match, condition, sourcing and installed price before approving it. A “universal” description alone does not establish suitability.

Labour, workmanship, repairs to existing components and revisit terms need separate confirmation. Keep the invoice and contact the provider with the original job details if a concern returns. Where manufacturer warranty or a service plan may still apply, check that route before authorising independent work.

Prepare access without opening or moving the machine

Keep the model from your invoice or an existing safe label photograph, the complete error message and any previous repair record ready. Clear ordinary access only where it is safe. Tell us about stairs, a stacked installation, a tight enclosure or a locked utility area before the appointment.

Do not dismantle panels, force a locked door, tilt a heavy washer or recreate a dangerous symptom for a video. If there is a shock, smoke, burning, severe movement or water near electrics, stop use and keep away; arrange the appropriate urgent help before a routine appliance visit. Use an isolation control only if it is safely accessible without approaching the hazard.

Safe inspection may require a qualified electrician, plumber or building team first. A washing-machine booking does not automatically include property wiring, earthing or plumbing correction.

Questions customers ask

What details should I send first?

Send your Bangalore locality or PIN code, full model, machine type, symptom and the stage when it occurs. Mention access restrictions and previous work.

Do you repair every washing-machine model?

Support is confirmed for the exact model and requested task. Identify washer-dryers, imported models and commercial-use appliances before booking.

Can I get a final repair price from a phone description?

A description may guide the enquiry, but a final amount can require assessment. Ask for the full quote before approving work.

Is the visiting charge the complete repair price?

No. The visiting charge is ₹200 if no repair occurs, while inspection charges depend on location. Confirm the applicable terms and repair amount separately.

Does a displayed code identify the part to buy?

Not by itself. It needs model-specific interpretation and findings before a component is selected.

Is cleaning included in every repair?

Do not assume so. Ask which care tasks are recommended, why they are appropriate and whether they are included or optional.

Can a tenant book a landlord-owned washer?

The enquiry can be coordinated by the tenant, but access, inspection permission and repair approval should be clear before work.

Do you guarantee same-day completion?

No completion guarantee is made here. Confirm availability, diagnosis, suitable parts and any return-visit arrangement for the actual job.

Are you a manufacturer-authorised service centre?

FixMyWasher is an independent provider. Check the manufacturer’s route first if warranty or a service plan may apply.

What should the invoice identify?

The appliance or job reference, accepted work, supplied component, amount, relevant terms and any agreed remaining tasks.

What if the problem does not occur during the visit?

Record the history, checks and limitation. Agree how a recurrence should be reported and the terms for any further assessment.

Does the enquiry form book the appointment?

No. It prepares a WhatsApp message for you to review and send. The appointment is confirmed after the service details are agreed.
