What happens when you use the enquiry form

Enter your Bangalore locality or PIN code, machine type and a short description of the concern. Add the full model if known. The form prepares those details as a WhatsApp message addressed to FixMyWasher’s published number, 9164151568.

You can review the draft in WhatsApp before sending. Opening a draft is not the same as sending a message, and sending an enquiry is not the same as booking an appointment. The provider must reply and confirm the accepted scope, applicable terms and availability.

The form itself does not take a payment or store your entries. Information you choose to send through WhatsApp becomes part of that conversation. Share only what is needed for the enquiry and provide the full address privately once the service details are being agreed.

Use the service location, not your current phone location

Enter the locality where the washer is installed. If you are arranging service for another home, your own address or current location may be irrelevant. A locality or PIN code is enough to start checking the enquiry; the full address can follow privately.

State whether the property is an apartment, independent home or another setting with special access conditions. If shared or commercial usage is involved, mention it so the exact support scope can be confirmed rather than assumed from the machine type selection.

The service enquiry is for Bangalore. Do not interpret an online form being accessible elsewhere as a promise of attendance outside the city. If the locality or access cannot be supported, clarify that before arranging time off or expecting a visit.

What to enter when the model is known or missing

Use the complete model from the invoice, manual or an existing safely obtained label image. Capacity and brand are useful starting details but may not identify the exact variant. Do not move a heavy washer or open panels to find information for the form.

If the full model is unavailable, leave the optional field blank and explain what you know in the conversation. The draft indicates that model details can be shared later. The provider may still need that information before confirming support or parts.

Choose the closest accurate machine type without hiding uncertainty. A washer-dryer or unfamiliar equipment should be described explicitly. An online selection is an enquiry detail, not confirmation that every model under that category is accepted.

Write a useful description of the concern

Describe the observed behaviour and programme stage: water remains after the cycle, the machine stops while filling or a new sound occurs during spin. Include the complete displayed code if one was already observed safely.

Add a recent move, previous repair or change in installation where relevant. Do not fill the field with a list of possible failed parts collected online. The assessment can investigate the cause; your observations help define the initial question.

If there is shock, burning, smoke, repeated tripping or water near electricity, stop use and seek appropriate help. State the condition clearly, but do not rely on an unanswered online message as a response to an immediate hazard or recreate the symptom to improve the description.

State a preference without assuming a reserved slot

You can mention a preferred date or time window in the concern field or subsequent conversation. Include any firm access limit, such as building work hours or when the household contact will be present.

This page does not provide a live appointment calendar. Availability must be confirmed through a reply. A preferred Sunday or evening should therefore remain a request until the provider agrees an actual window and the job details.

If timing is important, say whether your main need is the earliest assessment or a specific access window. Repair completion may depend on diagnosis and suitable parts, so a confirmed visit should not be presented to the household as guaranteed completion of an unknown fault.

Confirm terms in the conversation before booking

The stated visiting charge is ₹200 if no repair is carried out. Inspection charges depend on location. Ask how the applicable amounts are treated for the actual booking and whether any additional task or visit would have separate charges.

The final repair total depends on the finding, brand, model, component, labour and access. The form cannot diagnose the appliance or calculate a binding installed repair price from a short description. Keep any early price indication separate from the quotation after assessment.

No payment is taken by this page. If a later arrangement involves an advance for an identified task or component, confirm the purpose and terms through the established contact. The online enquiry itself should not be mistaken for acceptance of an unspecified charge.

If someone else will provide access or approve work

Tell the provider when you are arranging service for another household. Identify who will be at the property and who can approve the repair quotation. You do not need to put multiple people’s personal details in the public page form; arrange the necessary contact information privately.

Make sure the access person knows the original concern and the confirmed window. The approval person should receive the assessment finding and complete proposed scope before additional work is authorised.

If nobody can approve repair during the visit, discuss an assessment-only arrangement in advance. A successful online enquiry should lead to a practical visit plan, not a technician arriving while everyone assumes somebody else is responsible for the decision.

If WhatsApp does not open or you prefer to call

Use the visible Call button or dial 9164151568. You can also open the direct WhatsApp link and type the same locality, model and concern yourself. The form is optional and there is no need to submit repeated drafts to make an enquiry count.

If WhatsApp opens on another device or asks you to continue in your usual account, review the destination and message before sending. The published contact is the same across the direct links and prepared draft.

If you close the page before sending, do not assume the provider received the form contents. This page does not create a separate stored ticket from the fields. Check the actual conversation or call when you need to establish whether an enquiry has been received.

Keep corrections and changes connected to the original request

If the model, address or concern changes after you send the message, update the same conversation with a short correction. Identify the earlier appointment if one has already been confirmed. This is clearer than sending a second unrelated request with conflicting details.

If your preferred time changes, ask for a revised window to be confirmed. Editing a local draft or typing a new preference does not automatically reschedule an agreed appointment. Keep the person providing access informed of the latest arrangement.

Any cancellation or unsuccessful-access terms should be confirmed with the provider. The page does not invent a standard fee or notice period. A concise, current record helps both sides understand which version of the booking is in effect.

What to keep once the appointment is agreed

Save the confirmed date or window, service locality, machine details, assessment scope and applicable terms. Keep the provider contact and identify who will be at home. This is the practical record of the appointment.

After assessment, retain the finding and repair quotation separately from the original booking message. If work proceeds, connect the invoice, actual component and written coverage to the same job. Booking and repair approval are related stages but not identical decisions.

If the original issue returns, use the job details and describe the current behaviour. A clear online record makes follow-up easier without assuming that every future concern is automatically included in the first visit.

Visit charges and the price of the actual work

The visiting charge is ₹200 if no repair is carried out. Inspection charges depend on location. Confirm the applicable booking and inspection amounts, and how they are treated if you approve work, before scheduling. The ₹200 visiting amount is not the price of a complete repair or a promise of a free inspection.

The repair total depends on the brand, full model, diagnosed fault, required part, labour and access. Ask for the complete payable amount before approval, including any separately agreed transport, additional visit or tax. A symptom described on the phone can guide the enquiry but may not support a fixed final price.

Keep the diagnosis and quotation together. If the scope changes after assessment, ask for the revised explanation and total before additional work begins. Any property plumbing or electrical work must be identified and priced separately where required.

New parts, compatibility and your one-year warranty

New parts supplied by FixMyWasher carry a one-year warranty. The invoice should identify the covered component and written terms. This is not automatically a warranty on the entire washing machine, every future fault or the property installation.

Ask whether a proposed component is manufacturer-original, a compatible alternative or repair of the existing assembly. These are different offers. Confirm the full model match, condition, sourcing and installed price before approving it. A “universal” description alone does not establish suitability.

Labour, workmanship, repairs to existing components and revisit terms need separate confirmation. Keep the invoice and contact the provider with the original job details if a concern returns. Where manufacturer warranty or a service plan may still apply, check that route before authorising independent work.

Prepare access without opening or moving the machine

Keep the model from your invoice or an existing safe label photograph, the complete error message and any previous repair record ready. Clear ordinary access only where it is safe. Tell us about stairs, a stacked installation, a tight enclosure or a locked utility area before the appointment.

Do not dismantle panels, force a locked door, tilt a heavy washer or recreate a dangerous symptom for a video. If there is a shock, smoke, burning, severe movement or water near electrics, stop use and keep away; arrange the appropriate urgent help before a routine appliance visit. Use an isolation control only if it is safely accessible without approaching the hazard.

Safe inspection may require a qualified electrician, plumber or building team first. A washing-machine booking does not automatically include property wiring, earthing or plumbing correction.
