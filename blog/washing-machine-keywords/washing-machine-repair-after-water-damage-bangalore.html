Put the unsafe condition ahead of the repair enquiry

If water is near electrical equipment, keep people away and seek appropriate urgent assistance. Do not enter an affected area or touch the machine, plug or nearby fittings to find out whether power is present. Isolation must be handled through a safe route by an appropriate person.

An ordinary service message is not an emergency response. Do not wait for a routine booking reply while an immediate hazard continues. Explain the condition clearly to the relevant assistance provider and follow the appropriate local response.

Once access and the property condition are addressed, the appliance enquiry can focus on the exposure and feasible next step. Making the surrounding area accessible does not itself establish that the washer can be powered or repaired.

Describe the actual exposure instead of using one broad label

A hose leak outside the appliance, water entering an enclosure and a machine standing in floodwater are different situations. State what was observed and avoid assuming that all water damage has the same repair scope.

If contamination is known or suspected, mention it. Do not call unknown floodwater clean simply because it looks clear afterward. The assessor needs an honest account of the environment and any affected property equipment.

You do not need to determine the internal damage yourself. The first enquiry should establish the exact machine, exposure history and safe access conditions, then confirm whether the requested assessment is supported or a specialist route is required.

Dry appearance is not proof that the equipment is safe

Electrical Safety Foundation guidance warns that water-damaged electrical equipment can remain hazardous after drying and calls for qualified evaluation before power is restored. Residue and contamination are part of the assessment; appearance alone is not enough.

Do not use a hair dryer, heat gun, direct heating or repeated power attempts as a household recovery method. Waiting a chosen number of hours does not provide a universal clearance for an unidentified appliance and exposure.

The appropriate professional should establish the next step with the actual equipment and manufacturer guidance. The external reference below supports this general safety principle; it does not provide Indian installation rules or a promise that any particular washer can be reconditioned.

Coordinate the property assessment and appliance assessment

Water exposure may affect sockets, wiring, protection or other equipment as well as the washer. An appropriate qualified electrical professional should address relevant property safety questions. An appliance repair booking does not automatically include that work.

Ask which conditions must be resolved before the machine can be approached or assessed. Keep any actual property findings available to the appliance provider, along with limitations. Do not translate a statement about one inspected item into clearance for every device in the area.

Likewise, a later washing-machine repair does not certify the building installation. Clear records help each provider understand its task and prevent the household from assuming that somebody else has already assessed an unassigned risk.

Confirm support before arranging a normal repair appointment

Tell the provider that the enquiry involves water exposure, not only a washer that will not turn on. Share the full model from existing records and the known circumstances. This information can affect whether the job is within scope and what specialist assistance is appropriate.

Do not book under a routine symptom while withholding flooding or prior energisation. Accurate history helps the provider plan a meaningful assessment and avoid an appointment based on an incomplete description.

FixMyWasher does not promise recovery of every water-damaged appliance through this page. Confirm the accepted assessment, safe access requirement and charges. If the necessary work is outside scope, use the relevant manufacturer or appropriately capable specialist route.

Ask for findings and limits, not a quick visual assurance

The assessment should explain what was examined, what is affected and what remains unknown. Ask whether the machine’s current condition supports a defined repair proposal or whether additional evaluation is needed before feasibility can be discussed.

A statement that an exterior is dry or a display lights up is not a complete result. Do not ask the provider to prove recovery by energising equipment before the appropriate evaluation. The safe assessment method belongs with the qualified professional.

Keep uncertainty in the written record. If access, contamination or other conditions prevent a complete assessment, that limitation should not be replaced with a blanket assurance that the appliance is ready for normal household use.

Compare a supported repair with the possibility of replacement

Ask which actual components or tasks are included in the proposed repair and why that scope is considered appropriate. Water exposure can create a broader decision than replacing the first part associated with the visible symptom; the finding must support the proposed method.

If several affected items or unresolved conditions are involved, request the combined scope and uncertainty before approving isolated repairs. Consider the appliance’s prior condition, suitable parts, complete cost and any remaining limitation when deciding whether to proceed.

This page does not impose a universal repair-or-replace rule for every exposure. It also does not promise that a repair restores every aspect of the machine to its previous condition. The appropriate decision should follow qualified findings for the actual equipment.

Agree staged work and charges before the scope expands

An initial assessment may lead to a further agreed stage before a reliable repair quotation can be prepared. Ask what that stage is expected to establish, what it costs and what outcome remains uncertain.

The stated visiting charge is ₹200 if no repair is carried out, and inspection charges depend on location. Confirm the applicable terms for the accepted exposure-related enquiry; do not treat that visiting amount as a fixed decontamination or restoration price.

For any proposed repair, ask for the complete amount, identified components, separate property tasks and sourcing dependencies. A revised finding needs a revised explanation and approval before additional work proceeds.

Do not move or send the appliance away without an agreed plan

Water-exposed equipment may need a specifically confirmed assessment or receiving arrangement. This page does not promise collection or workshop acceptance. Tell the provider about the condition before planning transport.

Do not tilt, carry or reconnect the machine yourself to make it easier to collect. Appropriate handling and disconnection need to account for the actual hazard and installation. Clarify who is responsible for each accepted task.

If an appliance or component leaves the property, record identity, relevant condition, authorised work, custody and return or other agreed outcome. An assessment handover should not silently become permission for indefinite repair, disposal or an unapproved replacement.

If a formal report is needed for another party, state the requirement before booking and confirm whether that scope is offered. The provider’s normal work note should remain factual and should not be stretched into an unsupported cause or recovery claim.

New parts, compatibility and your one-year warranty

New parts supplied by FixMyWasher carry a one-year warranty. The invoice should identify the covered component and written terms. This is not automatically a warranty on the entire washing machine, every future fault or the property installation.

Ask whether a proposed component is manufacturer-original, a compatible alternative or repair of the existing assembly. These are different offers. Confirm the full model match, condition, sourcing and installed price before approving it. A “universal” description alone does not establish suitability.

Labour, workmanship, repairs to existing components and revisit terms need separate confirmation. Keep the invoice and contact the provider with the original job details if a concern returns. Where manufacturer warranty or a service plan may still apply, check that route before authorising independent work.

Prepare access without opening or moving the machine

Keep the model from your invoice or an existing safe label photograph, the complete error message and any previous repair record ready. Clear ordinary access only where it is safe. Tell us about stairs, a stacked installation, a tight enclosure or a locked utility area before the appointment.

Do not dismantle panels, force a locked door, tilt a heavy washer or recreate a dangerous symptom for a video. If there is a shock, smoke, burning, severe movement or water near electrics, stop use and keep away; arrange the appropriate urgent help before a routine appliance visit. Use an isolation control only if it is safely accessible without approaching the hazard.

Safe inspection may require a qualified electrician, plumber or building team first. A washing-machine booking does not automatically include property wiring, earthing or plumbing correction.

Questions customers ask

Can I switch on the washer once it looks dry?

No. Dry appearance is not clearance for water-exposed electrical equipment; appropriate evaluation is needed before power is restored.

Should I unplug it while standing near floodwater?

Do not approach wet electrical equipment or enter an unsafe area. Seek appropriate assistance through a safe route.

Does every water-damaged washer need the same repair?

No. Exposure, contamination, equipment and findings differ.

What should I tell the provider first?

The full model, known source and extent of exposure, timing, power history, prior actions and safe access status.

Can I use heat or repeated restarting to dry it out?

Do not use improvised drying or power attempts as a recovery test.

Is the home’s electrical installation included in appliance repair?

Not automatically. Relevant property equipment needs an appropriate separate assessment.

Does FixMyWasher guarantee flooded-appliance restoration?

No. Supported assessment and repair feasibility must be confirmed for the actual job.

Can a photograph establish that repair is safe and worthwhile?

No. It can help describe the enquiry but cannot replace the appropriate evaluation.

What if the repair proposal grows after assessment?

Ask for the new findings, complete revised scope, amount and remaining uncertainty before approval.

Is collection or workshop intake included?

No automatic arrangement is promised. Confirm support, handling and custody before movement.

Does a repair invoice certify all hidden conditions?

No. Keep the actual professional findings and limitations distinct from the billing record.

What warranty is confirmed for new supplied parts?

One year on new parts supplied by FixMyWasher under written terms, with other work and revisit terms clarified separately.
