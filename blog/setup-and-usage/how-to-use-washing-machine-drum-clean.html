How to Use Washing Machine Drum Clean or Tub Clean

Run the machine’s dedicated Drum Clean, Tub Clean or equivalent maintenance programme with the drum empty and follow its exact product instructions. Some programmes require no detergent or cleaner; other appliances specify a particular cleaning method. Do not transfer chemical instructions between brands or models.

This page explains the operating routine. It does not treat a cleaning cycle as a repair for leaks, drainage failure, damaged parts or every source of an unpleasant smell. Start by identifying the actual programme on your washer.

A six-step routine you can follow

  1. Identify the correct programme

    Use the exact model manual to find Drum Clean, Tub Clean or the equivalent, including any model-specific preparation.

  2. Empty and inspect the drum

    Remove all laundry and loose items. Only perform accessible preparation permitted by the manual.

  3. Follow the product rule

    Add nothing if the programme requires no cleaner. If a product is specified or permitted, use its correct amount and placement.

  4. Select and start normally

    Use the documented controls, close the opening normally and confirm the maintenance cycle has started.

  5. Allow the complete process

    Follow operating instructions and avoid interrupting merely because the cycle takes longer than expected.

  6. Finish the aftercare

    Wait for safe release and follow the specified wiping, ventilation or follow-up steps. Record completion and any unresolved concern.

What the cleaning programme is for

A maintenance programme is designed for the appliance’s interior under particular operating conditions. Its purpose differs from washing a basket of clothing. A programme name alone does not establish a universal temperature, duration or chemical recipe. Do not add towels to make an empty cleaning cycle feel more productive. Laundry can interfere with the intended procedure and may be exposed to treatment that is unsuitable for the fabric. The exact manual should tell you how to select the programme and what preparation is required. If your panel has a secondary function rather than a dedicated dial position, use the documented method instead of guessing a hold combination.

Do not mix bleach, acids, descalers or household cleaning products. A generic online recipe should not override the appliance or product instructions. “Natural” does not establish compatibility with seals, finishes or internal materials.

Prepare an actually empty machine

Check inside the drum for small socks and loose objects, rather than assuming the last load was fully removed. Look only in accessible areas; preparation does not require removing panels or dismantling the tub.

Review any instruction concerning residual product in the dispenser. Do not empty or alter an automatic-dosing system unless its guidance calls for that action. Reservoir handling and a manual main-wash compartment are different tasks.

Keep children and pets away during preparation and check the opening before closing it. After-use ventilation must also be managed with household access in mind.

A reminder is a prompt to follow the maintenance routine

Read the symbol or message in the model manual. A maintenance reminder is not automatically a fault code, and the number of washes between reminders is not universal.

Do not copy another model’s interval, reset sequence or reminder count. Use the applicable schedule and any condition-based instructions supplied by the manufacturer.

A reminder disappearing does not by itself establish that the cycle completed successfully. Check the actual completion state, particularly if the programme was interrupted or power was lost.

Select the maintenance function accurately

Similar labels can be confusing. Eco Drum Clean refers to appliance care on some machines, while Eco wash programmes are intended for suitable laundry. Read the full name before starting.

Check that the display shows the intended maintenance programme and that an unwanted delay has not been retained. If Child Lock prevents selection, use its documented full deactivation procedure.

Do not substitute the hottest ordinary laundry programme unless the manufacturer specifically describes it as the appropriate cleaning method. The water level, sequence and permitted additives can differ.

Let the programme complete as designed

The displayed time may be an estimate, and the cleaning process can contain stages that look inactive. Compare the behaviour with the model instructions instead of opening the machine or cancelling because you do not see constant movement.

Follow the manufacturer’s operating and attendance guidance. Plan a suitable window so that you can respond to an actual fault and complete any required aftercare.

If a leak, burning smell or other unsafe condition appears, stop use and obtain appropriate help. Completing a maintenance schedule is not a reason to continue an unsafe cycle.

Finish the accessible aftercare

Wait for the normal release and safe conditions before opening. Follow the model’s instructions for wiping accessible areas, removing loosened residue or running an additional programme if specifically required.

Do not assume a cleaning cycle replaces separate drawer, seal or filter care. Those components may have their own instructions and access precautions. Only undertake owner-accessible work described in the manual.

Avoid spraying water over controls or electrical areas. Use the permitted cleaning materials and after-use ventilation routine. Leave the appliance in a condition consistent with both the manual and safe household access.

Cleaning and descaling are separate decisions

Do not assume every cleaner is a descaler or every cleaning programme requires a descaling product. Check what the manufacturer says about your model and the relevant water conditions.

If a product is permitted, follow its actual label and the appliance restrictions. Do not combine products to cover several suspected problems at once or increase the dose because maintenance was delayed.

Where scale or another internal condition is suspected but not established, ask for appropriate assessment. A generic maintenance article should not become evidence that a particular internal repair is required.

What if the washer has no Drum Clean button?

Look for an equivalent programme or a maintenance section in the matching manual. The function may have another name, or the machine may use a different approved routine.

If no instructions are available, seek the manufacturer’s guidance using the full model reference. Do not invent a cycle by copying temperature, time and chemical settings from another washer.

For a semi-automatic machine, use its separate tub-care instructions. An automatic washer’s heated maintenance cycle should not be transferred to a twin-tub appliance that has different controls and construction.

When guidance is enough and when a service visit helps

You do not need a repair visit simply to choose a suitable programme or understand a load limit. Start with the model manual and the checks relevant to your question. If the machine repeatedly fails to complete a suitable cycle, records an unexplained fault or has a physical problem, request a defined assessment rather than approving parts from a guess.

For a recurring cleaning concern, provide the maintenance programme, permitted product used, completion status and the location or timing of the symptom. A maintenance reminder alone does not require a repair booking.

Visiting and inspection charges

The visiting charge is ₹200 if no repair is carried out. Inspection charges depend on location. Share your Bangalore locality or PIN code and confirm all applicable charges together before booking. ₹200 is not an advertised complete repair or parts-replacement price.

Ask how the visiting and inspection charges are treated if a repair proceeds. No automatic waiver, deduction or additional charge should be assumed. Your approved quote should make clear whether these amounts are included, credited or payable separately, and state any applicable tax and the final payable total.

For a usage demonstration or installation-only enquiry, confirm the actual offered service and booking terms separately. The no-repair visiting rule does not define every other service price.

Supplied-part warranty and other service terms

New parts supplied by FixMyWasher carry a one-year warranty. Ask for the supplied part to be identified on your job record or invoice. This applies to that new part; it does not turn the job into a one-year warranty on the entire washing machine.

Repairing an existing component is different from supplying a new one. Confirm the terms for repaired boards, retained parts, workmanship, labour, revisits and customer-supplied items separately. Do not infer those terms from the new-parts warranty.

Keep the quote, approved changes and completion record together. If the symptom returns, report what happened and when rather than assuming that the same part has failed. Stop using the washer if it leaks significantly, trips the circuit or develops a burning smell.

Advice, a programme change or an adjustment without a new supplied part does not create a one-year whole-machine warranty. Confirm labour, workmanship and revisit terms separately. Keep the appliance manufacturer’s coverage distinct; FixMyWasher does not claim manufacturer-authorised status.

Frequently asked questions

Can I leave clothes inside?

No. Use the empty-drum procedure specified for the maintenance programme.

Should I add detergent?

Only follow the exact model instruction; many dedicated programmes require none.

Can I use a purchased cleaner?

Only if the appliance permits it and its label matches the intended method.

Can I mix vinegar and bleach?

No. Never mix cleaning chemicals.

How often should I run it?

Follow your model’s interval, reminder and condition-based instructions.

Does it replace drawer cleaning?

Not necessarily. Accessible components can require separate care.

Is Eco wash the same as Eco Drum Clean?

No. Read the full programme description and intended purpose.

Can I use the hottest wash instead?

Only if the manufacturer specifies that as its approved cleaning method.

Will it fix every smell or leak?

No. Persistent symptoms may have a separate cause requiring assessment.

What should I record if it stops?

Model, programme, exact stage or message, product used if permitted and whether draining completed.
