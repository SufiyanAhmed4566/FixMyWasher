Washing machine motor replacement cost in Bangalore

Washing machine main-motor replacement is estimated at ₹4,000–₹12,000 including a compatible motor and normal fitting, with some models or assemblies costing more. The exact motor, brand, model and related work determine the quote. A washer that does not spin is not automatically a washer with a failed motor.

This FixMyWasher guide explains what a motor replacement price should cover, how drive designs change the scope and when a smaller repair may be relevant. It also distinguishes a new motor from an existing-motor repair or rewind, so you can compare the actual work and warranty before approval.

Why a spin complaint is not enough to price a motor

A machine can fail to complete spin because a required operating condition has not been met, because water has not drained properly or because of a drive-related fault. The assessment needs to distinguish these paths before an expensive motor is approved. Tell us which functions still work if you already know.

LG’s guidance for an LE motor-related message includes load or obstruction conditions, which illustrates why the wording of an error is not proof that a new motor must be supplied. Follow the guidance for the exact model and seek assessment when the problem remains; do not assume that one brand’s code has the same meaning on another machine.

Ask what finding supports replacement and which checks identified a defect in the motor itself. A quotation should connect that finding to the proposed component rather than list a motor because “not spinning” appeared in the booking message.

What a motor replacement quote should include

The quote should identify the motor or motor assembly, establish compatibility and state its condition. For the new-motor estimate on this page, normal fitting is included. Ask whether removal, refitting, relevant connections and the agreed functional checks are covered by the stated amount.

If additional access, transport or a model-specific procedure is needed, it should be explained before approval. A stacked or built-in installation can require planning that differs from a normally accessible appliance. Describe the site when booking rather than moving a heavy machine yourself.

Necessary related items should be named. A quotation that says only “motor complete” leaves uncertainty about whether a belt, pulley, coupling, mounting component or another assembly is included. Request the actual scope, not every possible component on a generic checklist.

When a smaller component repair may be relevant

Some designs have serviceable components that can be assessed separately from the main motor. Examples can include brushes on an appropriate brushed motor, a capacitor where used or a belt on a belt-driven system. None should be prescribed from a symptom alone.

Ask whether the proposed smaller repair is suitable for the confirmed condition and what limitations remain. Replacing a wearable component is not necessarily enough if a larger motor defect has also been established. Conversely, replacing the entire motor needs explanation when assessment supports a narrower correction.

A sensor or drive-control problem may involve another component or assembly altogether. The purpose of diagnosis is to identify the right scope, not to choose the smallest price regardless of whether it addresses the fault. Ask which part is defective and how the completed repair will be checked.

New motor, used motor and rewinding are different options

The main guide range refers to a compatible new motor with normal fitting. A used motor, a refurbished assembly or rewinding of the existing motor has a different history and scope. If an alternative is proposed, the quotation should describe it accurately and state its own terms.

Rewinding is not a universal option for every washing-machine motor or defect. Ask whether it is technically suitable for the actual design, what work is included and how the result will be assessed. This page does not assign a blanket rewind price or guarantee that every motor can be restored.

A new part supplied by FixMyWasher carries the stated one-year warranty. Do not copy that term onto an existing-motor repair, a customer-supplied motor or a different-condition item without separate confirmation. Compare like with like when assessing two prices.

Why motor prices vary by brand and exact part

The full model determines which motor or assembly is suitable. Capacity, design, version and the way the replacement is supplied can change the part cost and fitting scope. Two washers from the same brand may require different components, so a brand name alone is not enough to set a firm price.

Availability also affects the practical options. Ask whether the identified component is actually available and what arrangements apply if it must be ordered. A website estimate is not a promise of stock, same-day fitting or an exact completion date.

If a proposed price is above the range, request the component description and reason. A larger assembly or additional confirmed work can change the total, but the explanation should identify those differences. Do not accept an unexplained amount simply because the motor is described as “inverter” or “premium”.

Separate motor work from related mechanical or control faults

The motor operates within a system. If assessment identifies another condition that would prevent correct operation after replacement, that work should be described in the quote. Ask whether it is essential to the repair and what evidence supports it.

A new motor is not automatically the solution to a gearbox, bearing, obstruction, wiring or control problem. Equally, a supported additional correction should not be omitted merely to keep the headline motor price low. The complete quotation needs to explain the intended outcome.

When several components share access, ask how labour is calculated and whether any item is already included in a supplied assembly. A motor, board and mechanical assembly should not be charged as a standard bundle without separate findings and your approval.

Illustrative motor-cost decisions

Illustrative situations: These examples explain repair decisions. They are not records of actual FixMyWasher customer jobs.

A belt problem is confirmed instead of a failed motor

Suppose the model uses a belt and assessment supports belt replacement at ₹1,200 including normal fitting. That is the identified repair scope. Adding a new motor solely because the initial complaint was no drum movement would need a separate supported finding.

A new automatic-machine motor is quoted

Suppose a compatible new motor and normal fitting are quoted at ₹7,500. Confirm the exact assembly and relevant completion checks. Do not add another motor-fitting amount if that labour is already included; clarify inspection, unusual access and tax treatment in the final total.

A single semi-automatic motor is the required item

Suppose support is confirmed for the model and a new individual spin motor is quoted at ₹3,200 with normal fitting. This is a different component scope from the automatic main-motor example. It does not cover a second wash motor or refurbishment of the whole machine.

Deciding whether to replace the motor

Consider the complete quote, the condition of the rest of the washer, other confirmed faults and whether the appliance still suits your needs. A new motor can address a defined defect, but it does not remove wear or damage elsewhere. Ask what would remain after the proposed work.

Compare replacement with any technically suitable repair option using the actual scope, warranty and practical arrangements. Do not apply a universal age limit or a fixed percentage rule in place of the machine-specific decision. A low quote for an incomplete job can be misleading.

For a costly assembly, part availability and the planned verification matter as much as a small price difference. Ask whether there are known uncertainties that could require more work before normal use is restored. Make the decision on the complete expected job, not only the first component line.

What should be checked after fitting a motor

The verification should follow the affected operation and the model. Appropriate checks may include wash rotation, progression to the relevant spin stage and the absence of new abnormal sound or movement. Relevant connections and any disturbed areas need attention as part of the agreed procedure.

A brief movement of an empty drum is not necessarily enough for a complaint that occurs later in a programme or under normal operating conditions. Ask what was tested and what limits remain. The explanation should be honest about an intermittent fault rather than guarantee that no future problem can occur.

Keep the supplied motor description, quote and job record. If the original symptom returns, report when it happened and any exact message. Stop use for a significant leak, burning smell, repeated circuit trip or severe mechanical contact while arranging further advice.

Avoid unsafe motor tests and improvised workarounds

Do not connect a loose motor directly to power, bridge terminals or bypass a lid or door safety device to watch it operate. Do not push the drum to help a humming motor start. Internal electrical and moving components require appropriate professional procedures.

If water is near plugs or switches, avoid touching them and arrange safe isolation from a dry location by someone competent where needed. Repeatedly resetting a tripping circuit is not a pricing or diagnostic shortcut. Describe the warning signs during booking.

Do not tip a full machine or lift a stacked appliance to expose the motor label. A model number from a receipt or accessible appliance label and a clear symptom description are enough to begin the enquiry.

Visiting charge, inspection and the motor quote

The visiting charge is ₹200 if no repair is carried out. Inspection charges depend on location. Share your Bangalore locality or PIN code and confirm all applicable charges together before booking. ₹200 is not an advertised complete repair or parts-replacement price.

Ask how the visiting and inspection charges are treated if a repair proceeds. No automatic waiver, deduction or additional charge should be assumed. Your approved quote should make clear whether these amounts are included, credited or payable separately, and state any applicable tax and the final payable total.

If a motor needs ordering or the appliance needs off-site work, confirm transport, return-fitting and payment arrangements in advance. No free revisit or automatic inspection credit is implied by the indicative replacement range.

One-year warranty on the new supplied motor

New parts supplied by FixMyWasher carry a one-year warranty. Ask for the supplied part to be identified on your job record or invoice. This applies to that new part; it does not turn the job into a one-year warranty on the entire washing machine.

Repairing an existing component is different from supplying a new one. Confirm the terms for repaired boards, retained parts, workmanship, labour, revisits and customer-supplied items separately. Do not infer those terms from the new-parts warranty.

Keep the quote, approved changes and completion record together. If the symptom returns, report what happened and when rather than assuming that the same part has failed. Stop using the washer if it leaks significantly, trips the circuit or develops a burning smell.

The job record should identify whether a complete motor or a particular new motor component was supplied. That distinction defines the part being covered. A new motor does not create a one-year warranty on the whole drive system or every retained appliance component.

Request a motor-related repair assessment

Send your Bangalore locality or PIN code, full model and what the machine does during washing and spinning, where already known. Include an existing error photo, unusual sound description and earlier drive repairs if available. Do not run an unsafe washer for the enquiry.

Confirm the initial charges and let assessment establish whether the motor, another component or a non-drive condition is responsible. Review the identified part, fitting scope, new-parts warranty and full payable total before approval.

Frequently asked questions

How much does washing machine motor replacement cost?

For an automatic-machine main motor, the indicative range is ₹4,000–₹12,000 including a compatible new motor and normal fitting, with some models costing more. The exact component and separately applicable charges determine the full quote.

Is a semi-automatic motor covered by the same range?

An individual conventional wash or spin motor has a separate indicative range of ₹1,800–₹4,500 with normal fitting. Confirm exact model support and component scope; larger or specialised assemblies can differ.

Does not spinning mean the motor has failed?

No. Drainage, load, safety or control conditions and other drive components can affect spinning. Diagnosis should identify the cause before replacement is approved.

Can brushes or a capacitor be replaced instead?

Only where the design uses that serviceable component and assessment supports the repair. Not every motor has the same components, and a symptom alone does not establish the right replacement.

Does the motor price include a control board?

Not automatically. A drive-control module or another PCB is a different component and needs a separately identified scope if it is required.

Is motor rewinding the same as a new replacement?

No. Rewinding repairs an existing motor where suitable. A new supplied motor is a different item with different terms. Ask for the actual scope and warranty of each option.

Can a motor replacement cost above ₹12,000?

Yes. Some model-specific motors, larger assemblies or additional confirmed work exceed the guide range. Ask for the reason and approve the complete quote before work.

Is fitting already included in the guide range?

Normal fitting is included for the named replacement scope. Confirm unusual access, transport, related repairs, visit and inspection charges, and tax treatment separately.

What warranty applies to a new motor supplied by FixMyWasher?

The identified new part carries a one-year warranty. It does not cover the whole machine automatically; confirm labour, workmanship, revisit and existing-component repair terms separately.

What do I pay if I do not proceed with repair?

The visiting charge is ₹200 if no repair is carried out. Inspection charges depend on location, so confirm the complete no-repair amount before booking.
