What does a ready site mean?

A ready site has the appropriate accessible supply, drainage and electrical arrangements for the exact machine, along with a suitable position and access for the agreed standard work. It should be possible to connect and assess the appliance without first creating new building services or altering cabinetry.

Site suitability is model-specific. Hose reach, required clearance, drainage arrangement and electrical requirements should follow the manufacturer’s installation instructions. A nearby tap and socket alone do not establish that every requirement is met.

Describe the space and existing points when booking, preferably with safely taken photos. If you are unsure whether something is suitable, say so rather than modifying the site yourself. The booking conversation can identify which conditions need assessment before the standard estimate applies.

For a newly purchased washing machine

Check what installation arrangement was included with the purchase and the manufacturer’s instructions before arranging another paid visit. Retailer or manufacturer provisions can differ, so verify them for the actual purchase rather than assuming either free installation or a mandatory third-party service.

Have the model and purchase information available. Mention whether the machine is packaged, whether the delivery team has already moved it into place and whether any installation work was attempted. Do not operate a machine before the required transit preparation and connections are completed.

The installation record should identify any condition that prevents commissioning. A new machine with damage or an operating problem needs the appropriate next step; connecting it does not make that issue part of an ordinary installation package.

For reinstallation after moving house

Tell us whether the appliance was working before the move and whether any damage, leakage or unusual sound appeared afterward. Reinstallation can involve reconnecting a sound machine, but transport-related or pre-existing faults need their own assessment.

Ask whether disconnection at the old address, transport and reconnection at the new address are included or separate. The ₹400–₹800 ready-site estimate does not automatically cover a two-address relocation service. Specify the work you actually need.

Follow the model’s transport instructions and retain appropriate transit components where relevant. Do not tip a full washer, pull it by hoses or lift it without suitable help. Report trapped water or difficult access so the work can be planned.

Additional hoses and fittings should be itemised

The quotation should distinguish reusable suitable components from new materials needed for the installation. Ask what is being supplied, why it is needed and whether it is compatible with the model. A generic extension or adapter is not suitable simply because it reaches the nearest point.

If the existing hose is damaged or does not meet the required arrangement, the technician should explain the replacement or site correction. Avoid improvised stretching, unsupported drain extensions or connections that leave the hose under strain.

A supplied fitting should not be charged twice within both a materials package and an individual line. Conversely, a labour-only installation offer may not include any new hose. Compare the actual included items before deciding which quote is cheaper.

When the property needs separate work

An unsuitable electrical point, new water connection, blocked building drain or required cabinet change can prevent standard commissioning. Ask what has been identified and which qualified specialist should carry out the correction. These conditions need a clear scope beyond connecting the washer.

Do not use an improvised electrical extension or bypass protection to make an incomplete site appear ready. Appliance installation should follow the relevant manufacturer requirements and appropriate electrical practice. Household electrical repair may need separate professional attention.

If the site is not ready, agree what work can be completed, what remains and any return-visit charge before another booking. No universal failed-access fee, free return visit or fixed site-work price is invented here.

What commissioning should establish

The technician should explain whether the relevant connections, leakage, drainage and operation could be assessed after installation. The machine should be checked in its intended position where practical, since pushing it back can affect hoses and access.

Ask whether the required model-specific preparation and adjustments were completed. Do not accept unusual movement as something that must always disappear after a few washes. If an unresolved condition prevents normal operation, it should be investigated or clearly referred for the next step.

A commissioning check is not a full refurbishment or proof that every future fault is impossible. If an internal fault appears, it needs a diagnosis and quote distinct from the standard installation scope. Keep any limitation in the job record.

Illustrative installation quotations

Illustrative situations: These examples explain repair decisions. They are not records of actual FixMyWasher customer jobs.

Straightforward installation at a prepared site

Suppose agreed standard positioning, existing suitable connections and commissioning checks are quoted at ₹600. This falls within the ready-site range. Confirm any separately applicable booking amount and tax treatment before treating it as the final payable total.

An additional compatible item is required

Suppose standard installation is ₹600 and an identified necessary fitting is separately quoted at ₹300. The subtotal is ₹900 before any other expressly agreed amount or applicable tax. The ₹300 is only an illustrative material price, not a universal tariff for every hose or fitting.

The site needs new electrical or plumbing work

The technician finds that a required connection is not suitable. The next step is to explain the site work and who should perform it. The ready-site estimate should not be presented as a complete price for building a new utility connection.

Compare the whole installation offer

Ask whether an advertised installation charge includes ordinary positioning, connections, model-specific preparation and appropriate checks. Check whether any required material, transport or return visit is outside it. A low headline amount can describe a narrower service.

For a move, compare all stages you need rather than only the final connection charge. If one offer includes disconnection and transport while another covers only reinstallation, their prices describe different work.

Once scope is comparable, assess the price together with the practical arrangements and the clarity of completion checks. Do not pay for optional accessories solely because they are bundled with installation; ask why each is appropriate for the machine and site.

What to keep and ask at handover

Keep the job record, any supplied-part description and the approved additional work. Ask which checks were completed and whether anything remains to be corrected before ordinary use. Note any site issue that prevented commissioning.

Ask for model-appropriate guidance on the first use, care of connections and the relevant manual instructions. The technician should not need you to remove covers or perform electrical measurements to accept the installation.

If leakage, severe vibration or electrical warning signs occur afterward, stop use where unsafe and report the problem. Do not keep operating an appliance in the hope that an unresolved installation issue will settle on its own.

How booking charges apply to installation

The visiting charge is ₹200 if no repair is carried out. Inspection charges depend on location. Share your Bangalore locality or PIN code and confirm all applicable charges together before booking. ₹200 is not an advertised complete repair or parts-replacement price.

Ask how the visiting and inspection charges are treated if a repair proceeds. No automatic waiver, deduction or additional charge should be assumed. Your approved quote should make clear whether these amounts are included, credited or payable separately, and state any applicable tax and the final payable total.

Installation is a charged service even when no fault repair is needed. Confirm how booking charges apply to an installation-only visit and the price of the completed service. Do not interpret the ₹200 no-repair visiting term as replacing an agreed installation charge.

If site conditions prevent completion, clarify what work was possible, the applicable booking terms and the cost of any further visit. There is no universal cancellation or incomplete-installation tariff on this page.

Warranty on parts supplied during installation

New parts supplied by FixMyWasher carry a one-year warranty. Ask for the supplied part to be identified on your job record or invoice. This applies to that new part; it does not turn the job into a one-year warranty on the entire washing machine.

Repairing an existing component is different from supplying a new one. Confirm the terms for repaired boards, retained parts, workmanship, labour, revisits and customer-supplied items separately. Do not infer those terms from the new-parts warranty.

Keep the quote, approved changes and completion record together. If the symptom returns, report what happened and when rather than assuming that the same part has failed. Stop using the washer if it leaks significantly, trips the circuit or develops a burning smell.

A service that supplies no new part does not automatically create a one-year whole-machine or installation-work warranty. Confirm the terms for the actual installation work separately. If a new hose or other part is supplied, identify it on the job record.

Frequently asked questions

What are washing machine installation charges in Bangalore?

Standard installation at a ready site is estimated at ₹400–₹800. Confirm the agreed tasks, separately applicable booking amounts, extra materials, site work and final total.

What does a ready site mean?

It means suitable accessible supply, drainage, electrical arrangements and space for the exact machine, without first creating new building services or making unagreed alterations.

Are new hoses included in the estimate?

Not automatically. Ask which existing components are suitable and what new materials are required and included in the actual quote.

Does installation include transport from my old home?

Not by default. Disconnection, transport and reinstallation are separate stages to identify in the quotation.

Are transit preparations the same on every model?

No. Follow the installation and transport instructions for the exact appliance. Do not assume all machines use identical restraints or preparation steps.

Does the standard charge cover stacked machines?

Do not assume it. Describe the arrangement and ask about suitable support components, access and safe handling before booking.

What if the site is not ready?

Ask what is unsuitable, which specialist correction is needed, what work can be completed and what any return visit would cost. No universal incomplete-site fee is stated.

Does ₹200 cover a completed installation?

Do not interpret it that way. ₹200 is the visiting charge if no repair is carried out; confirm its treatment for an installation-only booking and the agreed price of the service performed.

Is installation covered for one year?

The confirmed one-year warranty applies to new parts supplied by FixMyWasher. Installation workmanship and service terms need separate confirmation.

What should I send to get a useful installation quote?

Send the model, locality, new-installation or relocation requirement, site photos taken safely and access details. State whether you need disconnection, transport or additional materials.
