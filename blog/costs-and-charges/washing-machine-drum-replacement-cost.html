Washing machine drum replacement cost in Bangalore

Specified washing machine drum or tub assembly work is estimated at ₹7,000–₹18,000 including normal labour, with some designs costing more. The exact assembly must be identified before that range becomes useful. “Drum replacement” can refer to a different supplied item from “outer tub replacement”, and neither automatically means every mechanical component is new.

This FixMyWasher guide explains the terminology, what changes a major assembly quote and how to compare it with suitable smaller repairs. The final price depends on the brand, full model, confirmed damage, assembly contents, access and location. Approve a clear scope after assessment rather than treating noise or lack of rotation as proof that a complete drum is needed.

The names can be used loosely during an enquiry. Before approval, ask the technician to identify the actual item and any available part reference. A clear description prevents comparing a small component price with a much larger supplied assembly.

When an assembly replacement recommendation needs evidence

Severe noise, a drum that will not turn normally or a leak can involve several conditions. The assessment should establish what is damaged and why the proposed assembly addresses it. A symptom alone does not prove that the most expensive mechanical option is required.

Ask whether the finding concerns the rotating component, water-containing structure, bearings, support or another part. If the recommendation relies on damage that is not yet fully visible, clarify what further investigation is necessary and which stage you are being asked to approve.

Relevant load, installation, suspension and drive conditions should not be confused with structural drum damage. You do not need to perform internal mechanical tests yourself. Describe what you observed and let the inspection determine the appropriate route.

Brand, full model and assembly version affect the price

The full model determines the appropriate replacement and connections. Capacity or a similar cabinet appearance is not sufficient to establish that an assembly will fit. Ask how the supplied item has been identified for your machine.

Part versions can matter even within a product family. BSH documentation for a particular washer family describes replacement tub versions whose halves are not interchangeable. That is a model-specific example of why a cheaper lookalike part should not be assumed compatible with every machine.

The example does not imply that every brand has the same arrangement or that a particular old part is available in Bangalore. Confirm the actual assembly, availability and fitting requirements for your washer before ordering.

Ask which components are inside the supplied assembly

A major assembly quote should describe its contents: what new items are supplied and what original items will be transferred or retained. Do not assume that bearings, support components, seals or every mounting item are included merely because the phrase “complete drum” appears.

If a larger assembly includes the required smaller components, separately charging those same supplied items needs explanation. If essential fittings or seals are not included in the purchased assembly, the quote should identify them before the repair starts rather than leave completion items undefined.

Ask about the supplied condition as well. A new assembly, a used assembly and a repair to the original structure are different options. Descriptions such as genuine or compatible should match the actual item and should not be used to obscure its condition.

What assembly-removal and fitting labour should cover

Normal labour in the guide range refers to the approved named assembly job. Ask whether removal, fitting, refitting, relevant connections and appropriate checks are included. A component-only listing from a parts seller does not represent the complete installed repair.

Access and handling can be substantial. If the work requires transport, off-site assessment or additional help with a stacked or built-in installation, those arrangements should be discussed before approval. Do not move, lift or tip a heavy machine yourself to reduce an assumed charge.

When another component is replaced during the same access, ask how the combined labour is calculated. The quote should explain overlap instead of adding unrelated standalone job totals without considering what work is actually shared.

Agree how further findings will be handled

A preliminary estimate may change after agreed dismantling reveals the full condition. Before that stage, ask the assessment charge, what can be established, the reassembly arrangements if you stop and what additional work would need new approval.

If the technician finds further damage, ask for a revised scope and complete total. The explanation should identify the new finding and why it changes the proposed repair. A major assembly order should not proceed merely because an initial inspection was approved.

There is no standard unsuccessful-repair fee, free dismantling promise or automatic inspection credit on this page. Clarify the actual terms for each stage so the amount payable does not depend on an unstated assumption.

Illustrative drum and tub quotations

Illustrative situations: These examples explain repair decisions. They are not records of actual FixMyWasher customer jobs.

A named assembly supplied and fitted

Suppose an identified new tub assembly with normal labour is quoted at ₹11,000. Ask which components it contains and which original parts remain. Confirm booking charges, transport if needed and tax treatment before treating it as the complete payable total.

An assembly price plus labour shown separately

Suppose the quote lists the named new assembly at ₹8,500 and its normal fitting/refitting work at ₹2,500. The repair subtotal is ₹11,000. Do not add another full fitting package for the same assembly; compare the actual contents and exclusions with the first example.

Two alternative mechanical routes

Suppose a separately serviceable bearing job is quoted at ₹5,500 and a specified assembly option at ₹11,000. If they are alternatives, choose the suitable defined scope rather than adding them to make ₹16,500. Ask why each option is proposed and what limitations or retained damage would remain.

How to judge whether the assembly repair is worthwhile

For a major quote, review the machine as a whole. Consider its usefulness, condition, other confirmed faults, part availability and the coverage on the supplied new assembly. Ask whether the proposed job would restore the required operation or leave another known problem unresolved.

Replacing a drum or tub does not automatically replace the control board, motor, door lock or suspension. Those retained components should not be represented as new or covered under the assembly’s parts warranty without a specific agreement.

No fixed age threshold or repair-cost percentage is imposed here. Compare the complete repair with the realistic costs and practical requirements of your alternative. Include site changes only if they are actually needed, and avoid counting hypothetical future breakdowns as certain costs.

Questions before a large assembly order

Confirm that the correct assembly has been identified and that availability is known or still under review. An indicative guide range is not a promise of local stock or immediate installation. Ask for an expected plan and discuss any timing needs before approval.

If a deposit is requested, clarify what it covers and the relevant order, cancellation or return terms. No universal deposit percentage or refund policy is created in this guide. Keep the assembly description and payment agreement with the quotation.

Clarify transport, handling, return fitting and the condition in which the machine will be left if work is staged. Those arrangements matter more on a major repair than a vague claim of “complete service”.

What to ask about after assembly fitting

The relevant water containment, rotation and operating behaviour should be checked after fitting where safe and practical. The checks should relate to the original complaint and any disturbed connections. A demonstration that the drum moves is only one part of assessing a structural leak or spin-related problem.

Ask whether the appliance was assessed in its final installed position and whether any limitation remains. If another fault or property-side condition prevents the relevant test, that should be explained. A limited check should not be described as proof that all future mechanical problems are impossible.

Keep the assembly identification, approved scope and job record. Report unusual noise, movement or leakage if it returns. Stop use for severe contact, significant water escape or an electrical warning rather than running repeated cycles to settle an unexplained fault.

Leave heavy assembly handling to the repair team

Do not try to extract the tub, turn a water-filled machine on its side or lift a stacked installation yourself. These actions are not required to obtain a quote and can create avoidable injury or water-release risks. Describe the access conditions when booking.

If the machine contains trapped water or laundry, report that before attendance. Avoid forcing a front-loader door or opening a full or hot filter arrangement. Use only the permitted model-specific procedure when its precautions can be met safely.

For water near electrical connections, smoke, sparks, burning smells or repeated circuit trips, stop use and arrange safe isolation from a dry location by someone competent where necessary. Do not recreate the problem for a recording.

Visiting, inspection and major-repair charges

The visiting charge is ₹200 if no repair is carried out. Inspection charges depend on location. Share your Bangalore locality or PIN code and confirm all applicable charges together before booking. ₹200 is not an advertised complete repair or parts-replacement price.

Ask how the visiting and inspection charges are treated if a repair proceeds. No automatic waiver, deduction or additional charge should be assumed. Your approved quote should make clear whether these amounts are included, credited or payable separately, and state any applicable tax and the final payable total.

Clarify each later stage: further investigation, transport, assembly order and return fitting. The ₹200 no-repair visiting term is not a fixed charge for all dismantling or off-site mechanical work.

Warranty on the actual new assembly supplied

New parts supplied by FixMyWasher carry a one-year warranty. Ask for the supplied part to be identified on your job record or invoice. This applies to that new part; it does not turn the job into a one-year warranty on the entire washing machine.

Repairing an existing component is different from supplying a new one. Confirm the terms for repaired boards, retained parts, workmanship, labour, revisits and customer-supplied items separately. Do not infer those terms from the new-parts warranty.

Keep the quote, approved changes and completion record together. If the symptom returns, report what happened and when rather than assuming that the same part has failed. Stop using the washer if it leaks significantly, trips the circuit or develops a burning smell.

Record whether the new item is an inner drum, outer tub or a specified combined assembly. The warranty follows the identified new part. Retained controls, motor and other original components do not become new merely because the assembly job required extensive access.

Frequently asked questions

How much does washing machine drum replacement cost?

Specified drum or tub assembly work is estimated at ₹7,000–₹18,000 including normal labour, with some designs higher. The exact supplied assembly, model and separately applicable charges determine the quote.

Is the inner drum the same as the outer tub?

No. They describe different parts of the relevant design. Ask what component or combination the quote actually includes.

Does a complete drum quote include the motor?

Not automatically. Assembly contents must be identified. Motor, controls and suspension may remain original or need separate supported work.

Should I add bearing cost to the assembly price?

Do not add it by default. Ask whether the required bearings are already included and whether a bearing-only quote is an alternative repair route.

Why might a similar-looking assembly not fit?

Model and version differences can affect compatibility and connections. The exact replacement must be identified for the actual appliance.

Does the guide include fitting labour?

Normal labour is included for the named assembly scope. Transport, special access, unrelated work and other separately applicable charges need confirmation.

Can a drum or tub repair exceed ₹18,000?

Yes. Some model-specific assemblies or additional confirmed work cost more. Ask for the reason and complete revised quote before approval.

What if extra damage is found after opening the machine?

Request the finding, revised scope and total before additional work. Agree the investigation stage and reassembly arrangements before dismantling begins.

Does the new assembly give the whole machine a one-year warranty?

No. New parts supplied by FixMyWasher carry a one-year warranty on the identified new items. Retained components and service terms require separate confirmation.

When should I compare repair with another machine?

For a substantial quote, compare the full repair scope with the appliance’s condition, other confirmed faults, part availability and your realistic alternatives. No universal age or percentage rule is used.
