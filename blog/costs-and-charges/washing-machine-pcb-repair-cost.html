Washing machine PCB repair cost in Bangalore

Repair of an existing washing machine PCB is estimated at ₹1,800–₹4,500 for suitable cases, including the agreed board repair and normal refitting. The actual price depends on the brand, exact model, board involved, damage and work required. A display problem or stopped cycle does not by itself establish that the PCB is defective.

This guide covers repairing the existing electronic board. Buying a new replacement board is a different option with a different price range. FixMyWasher assessment should establish whether repair is appropriate, explain any remaining uncertainty and give you a defined quotation before work begins.

Unless a row says otherwise, repair estimates include the named part or repair work and normal fitting labour. They exclude separately confirmed visiting or location-based inspection charges, unrelated repairs, special access or transport, and any applicable tax not expressly included in your quote. Do not add a separate fitting charge to a price already described as parts plus fitting.

The existing-PCB repair range is not a guarantee that a board can be repaired for its lowest figure. If assessment finds the board unsuitable for repair, ask for the reason, the available alternatives and the charges already agreed for the assessment.

Which board is being assessed?

A washing machine can have a main control board, a user-interface or display board and, on some designs, a separate drive-control module. These names describe different functions and should not be used interchangeably in a quotation. Ask which board is implicated and how it relates to your complaint.

The full appliance model and the identified board matter because electronics can vary between similar-looking machines. A repair estimate for one board does not automatically cover every electronic module inside the washer. If two boards are proposed for work, request the evidence and scope for each.

A button problem could involve an interface component, connection or another operating condition. Likewise, a machine with no display may need supply or connection assessment before board work is justified. The enquiry starts with the symptom; the quotation should follow the findings.

What repairing the existing PCB actually means

The agreed work addresses a confirmed defect on the existing board rather than replacing the whole module with a new one. Depending on the board and damage, the proposed correction may differ substantially between jobs. A responsible quote defines the intended repair without promising that every board can be restored.

The price should make clear whether removal, any agreed board work, normal refitting and relevant machine checks are included. If the board must be assessed away from the appliance, ask how it will be identified, what the next approval point is and how the return and refitting are arranged.

A repaired original board remains a repaired component. It should not be described as newly supplied simply because an electronic component on it was replaced. Ask for the warranty terms that specifically apply to the board-repair service rather than assuming the new-parts policy covers it in full.

Why one PCB repair is more expensive than another

The cost can vary with the board involved, the nature and extent of damage, suitable component availability, access and the assessment needed to reach a defensible repair decision. Brand is useful context, but the exact model and board are more specific than a general “premium brand” label.

A localised fault and damage affecting several areas are different scopes. Protective construction or limited service information can also affect whether a repair is practical. Those factors should lead to an explanation of suitability and work required, not an unsupported promise of a guaranteed low-cost fix.

If a quotation exceeds the guide range, ask why and compare the complete option with a compatible replacement. Do not approve an escalating repair merely because money has already been spent on an assessment; the next decision should reflect the expected work, remaining uncertainty and total cost.

When board repair may or may not be a sensible option

Repair may be worth considering when the defect can be identified, a suitable correction is feasible and the repaired board can be assessed in the machine. The technician should also consider whether a related fault needs correction to avoid leaving the cause of the complaint unresolved.

Replacement or another option may be more appropriate when the damage, condition or inability to verify a reliable correction makes repair unsuitable. No fixed visual rule is used here: a photograph or a burnt-looking area alone does not establish every affected circuit or the complete cause.

If repairability cannot be established without further work, ask for a staged agreement. Understand the assessment charge, the maximum work you are approving at that stage and what happens if the board is found unsuitable. A preliminary discussion should not become open-ended authorisation.

Clarify off-site assessment and unsuccessful-repair terms

Ask whether the quoted amount is for assessment, an attempted repair or a completed repair with defined checks. Those descriptions have different implications. This guide does not invent a “no fix, no fee” policy, free transport or a universal charge for an unsuccessful board repair.

Before the board or appliance leaves the site, agree what is being taken, how it is identified, the intended work and the next contact point. Ask about the machine’s condition while the board is removed and whether any refitting cost would apply if you decline the next stage.

If the repair cannot be completed, request an explanation of what was established and the available next options. Confirm the actual payment terms in advance so the outcome does not depend on an assumption about the word “repair”. Keep the written scope with the booking details.

A board repair can require a separate related correction

A fault elsewhere in the appliance may need attention alongside the board. If the technician identifies such a condition, the quotation should explain it and state whether correction is essential to completing the proposed repair. Do not accept an unexplained list of additional parts.

For example, a confirmed damaged connection or leak affecting the relevant area is a separate finding with its own scope. The quote should distinguish correcting that condition from repairing the board itself. Avoid stating an exact cause, such as a supply surge, without supporting evidence.

If household electrical work is required, it may need an appropriately qualified electrician rather than being bundled into an appliance-repair estimate. Ask who is responsible for the work and what must be completed before safe testing of the washer.

PCB repair cost examples

Illustrative situations: These examples explain repair decisions. They are not records of actual FixMyWasher customer jobs.

A defined repair to the original board

Suppose assessment supports an existing-board repair at ₹2,800 including normal refitting and stated checks. Ask what other amounts remain payable and what warranty applies to that repair. The figure does not mean a new board has been supplied.

Repair and replacement offered as alternatives

Suppose the repair option is ₹2,800 and a new compatible board with normal fitting is ₹6,500. You are comparing two options, not authorising a ₹9,300 package. Confirm whether any assessment amount is separate or credited in the option you choose; no automatic credit is implied.

Assessment does not support a repair

A board is found unsuitable for the proposed repair after an agreed assessment stage. Ask for the finding, the charges already agreed for that stage and the cost of any replacement option. This illustration does not promise free diagnosis or impose an invented unsuccessful-repair fee.

What completion checks should demonstrate

After normal refitting, the affected function needs checking in the machine where safe and practical. The relevant check depends on the original complaint: starting, filling, rotation, drainage or another function may be involved. A display lighting up is not sufficient evidence for every type of fault.

If the complaint occurs intermittently or only after a longer period, discuss the limits of the checks completed. A short successful run can be useful without proving that all possible intermittent conditions have been ruled out. Ask what to record if the symptom returns.

The job record should state the repaired board, the work performed at a useful level and any unresolved recommendation. Keep the machine stopped if a safety concern remains. Do not open the control panel to inspect the repair or recreate an electrical fault for confirmation.

Do not attempt live board checks to obtain a quote

Internal electronics are not a customer-side pricing test. Do not remove covers, bridge terminals, connect a loose board to power or bypass safety controls. Unplugging alone does not make every internal component suitable for untrained handling.

If the washer trips repeatedly, smells burnt, sparks or has water near electrical areas, stop using it and avoid wet plugs or switches. Arrange safe isolation from a dry location by someone competent where necessary. Tell the booking team about the warning rather than trying repeated resets.

For the enquiry, provide only the model, symptom and existing photo or error message. The technician can decide the appropriate assessment process without asking you to expose the electronics.

PCB inspection, visiting charge and repair approval

The visiting charge is ₹200 if no repair is carried out. Inspection charges depend on location. Share your Bangalore locality or PIN code and confirm all applicable charges together before booking. ₹200 is not an advertised complete repair or parts-replacement price.

Ask how the visiting and inspection charges are treated if a repair proceeds. No automatic waiver, deduction or additional charge should be assumed. Your approved quote should make clear whether these amounts are included, credited or payable separately, and state any applicable tax and the final payable total.

For board work, also clarify any bench assessment, transport or return-fitting charge before authorising it. The ₹200 no-repair visiting amount is not a blanket price for off-site electronic diagnosis.

Warranty for a repaired board needs separate confirmation

New parts supplied by FixMyWasher carry a one-year warranty. Ask for the supplied part to be identified on your job record or invoice. This applies to that new part; it does not turn the job into a one-year warranty on the entire washing machine.

Repairing an existing component is different from supplying a new one. Confirm the terms for repaired boards, retained parts, workmanship, labour, revisits and customer-supplied items separately. Do not infer those terms from the new-parts warranty.

Keep the quote, approved changes and completion record together. If the symptom returns, report what happened and when rather than assuming that the same part has failed. Stop using the washer if it leaks significantly, trips the circuit or develops a burning smell.

The headline on this page is existing-PCB repair. Do not apply the one-year new-parts warranty to the entire repaired original board unless a separate agreement expressly provides that coverage. Ask about the repair’s own duration, covered work and claim arrangements before approval.

Request a PCB-related assessment in Bangalore

Share your locality, full model, exact symptoms and any existing display message. Mention earlier board work, water leakage, electrical warning signs or whether the problem occurs only during a particular stage. Do not remove the board before booking.

Confirm the initial inspection charge and how any later bench work would be approved. After diagnosis, compare the defined repair and replacement options with their complete totals and separate warranty terms.

Frequently asked questions

How much does washing machine PCB repair cost?

Suitable repair of an existing PCB is estimated at ₹1,800–₹4,500 including the agreed repair and normal refitting. The exact board, damage, model and separately applicable charges determine the quote.

Does that price include a new PCB?

No. It covers repair of the existing board. A new compatible PCB is a different option, estimated at ₹3,500–₹12,000 with some models costing more.

Does an error code prove that the board is damaged?

No. A code or symptom guides assessment but does not identify every failed component. Relevant operating, connection and other conditions need consideration.

Can every washing machine PCB be repaired?

No. Suitability depends on the board, damage, available correction and ability to assess the result. Ask for the reason if repair is not recommended.

Are main-control and display-board repairs the same?

No. They may involve different modules. The quote should identify the actual board and exact appliance model.

Is bench assessment always included?

Do not assume it. Ask whether off-site assessment, transport, refitting and further investigation are included or separately charged before approving that stage.

Is there a no-fix, no-fee policy?

No universal policy is stated here. Confirm what is payable for assessment, an attempted repair or an unsuccessful outcome before work begins.

Does a repaired original PCB have the one-year new-parts warranty?

Not automatically. The one-year warranty applies to new parts supplied by FixMyWasher. Existing-board repair terms need separate confirmation.

Can I add the PCB repair and replacement ranges to calculate the bill?

They are usually alternative options, not automatic cumulative charges. Ask which option you are approving and how prior assessment amounts are treated.

What information helps with a PCB repair enquiry?

Provide your locality, full model, symptoms, existing error message and previous repair history. Do not open the control panel or perform live electrical checks.
