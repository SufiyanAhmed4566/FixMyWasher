What deep cleaning should mean in your quotation

A useful quotation lists the areas to be cleaned and the access involved. Depending on the model and agreed job, this may involve appropriate accessible detergent areas, door-area surfaces, permitted filter arrangements or other identified parts. Do not assume every machine has the same service access.

Ask what will be removed, what will remain in place and which internal surfaces cannot be reached by the chosen method. An accessible-area service and a major assembly-removal service are different scopes. Both should be described accurately.

The result should be specific: addressing identified residue or deposits in the agreed areas and checking any disturbed connections where relevant. The package should not promise a factory-new machine, removal of every permanent mark or protection against all future odours.

When an operating problem should be assessed first

Tell us if the washer also fails to drain, leaks, trips a circuit, stops mid-cycle or displays an unexplained fault. Those conditions can need diagnosis before a cleaning service is appropriate. Booking cleaning alone because it costs less may leave the actual cause unresolved.

Residue and a mechanical or electrical fault can coexist. Ask which task addresses the operating failure and which is maintenance you can choose separately. A cleaning package should not be presented as a guaranteed cure for a defective pump, heater, seal or board.

If the initial inspection changes the recommendation, agree the revised scope and amount before work begins. The original cleaning enquiry is not open-ended approval for replacement parts or a more expensive dismantling job.

Odour, deposits and stains are different expectations

An odour complaint should be considered alongside usage, residue, retained water and relevant installation conditions. Cleaning the agreed areas may help with identified residue, but the quote should not guarantee that every possible source of smell is covered.

Visible mineral deposits, detergent residue and a permanently discoloured surface may need different treatment or have different limits. Ask what is being addressed and what result is realistic for the material and condition. A stain alone does not prove a component needs replacement.

If a door seal is physically damaged, cleaning is a different scope from fitting a new one. The seal replacement estimate is ₹2,000–₹5,000 including the compatible seal and normal fitting. It should be proposed only when assessment supports that repair.

Is complete drum removal included?

Not automatically. The ₹900–₹1,800 range is for a defined cleaning service and normal labour within that scope. Full removal of a drum or tub assembly can involve substantially different access, handling and refitting requirements. Ask whether it is necessary and appropriate for the model.

If extensive dismantling is proposed, request the reason, what will be accessed, what items may need replacement and the complete quote. Any uncertainty that can only be resolved after opening the assembly should have an agreed investigation stage and stopping point.

Do not compare a simple cleaning-cycle offer with a full assembly-removal quote as if they are equivalent. Equally, an impressive term such as “complete deep clean” should not substitute for a clear description of what is actually done.

A built-in drum-clean cycle is a separate maintenance function

Some machines provide a drum or tub-clean programme with model-specific instructions. Using that programme is not necessarily the same as manually cleaning the accessible areas or carrying out a technician’s defined package. Ask how the proposed service differs from the permitted routine you can perform yourself.

Follow the actual manual for programme choice and permitted products. Do not add laundry or chemicals based on generic advice from another model. Some cleaning functions have specific restrictions, so a universal chemical recipe is inappropriate.

If the machine has an unresolved operating fault or electrical warning, do not run repeated cleaning cycles to see whether it disappears. Arrange the appropriate assessment first.

Deep cleaning and descaling should not be merged into one vague claim

Descaling addresses suitable mineral deposits using an appropriate method, while a broader cleaning package may address other residue and accessible components. Ask whether the identified condition calls for descaling and whether it is already included.

A package should not automatically charge both labels when they describe the same agreed operation. If they are separate tasks, the quote should explain the purpose, products and cost of each. Do not assume every machine in Bangalore needs the same treatment merely because of its location.

Cleaning products must suit the appliance and be used according to their instructions. Do not mix cleaners or assume household acids are safe for every surface. The service should not promise that chemical treatment restores an electrically defective heater or repairs damaged seals.

How brand, design and condition affect the price

Different designs provide different access to drawers, filters, seals and internal components. Brand and full model help establish the appropriate method, but condition and the agreed area list also affect the work. A front-loader and a top-loader should not simply receive an identical procedure.

The quantity and location of residue can change the realistic scope. If additional work is proposed after assessment, ask what changed and approve the revised total first. The indicative range is not a promise that every neglected or specialised machine can be treated within the same amount.

Access also matters. A built-in or stacked arrangement may require planning beyond a normally accessible washer. Send safe photos and explain restrictions before booking; do not lift or dismantle the installation yourself.

Illustrative deep-cleaning prices

Illustrative situations: These examples explain repair decisions. They are not records of actual FixMyWasher customer jobs.

A defined package with consumables included

Suppose the agreed accessible-area cleaning tasks, suitable consumables and normal labour are quoted together at ₹1,200. Ask which areas are covered and what checks follow. Clarify booking charges and tax before treating it as the final payable total.

An extra task changes the quote

Suppose a ₹1,200 package is extended by a separately explained ₹300 task that you approve. The subtotal is ₹1,500. The ₹300 is an illustration, not a fixed extra-service tariff; ask whether the new task duplicates anything already included.

A fault changes the appropriate service

A customer requests cleaning for an odour, but assessment also identifies poor drainage. The next decision is which corrective work is required and whether cleaning remains useful. Do not automatically add the pump replacement range without a diagnosis supporting a defective pump.

What to review at completion

Check the performed work against the agreed area list and ask what could not be accessed. The result should reflect the actual scope, rather than a claim that every unseen surface has been restored. Where possible, the technician can explain the condition found and the tasks completed.

Ask whether relevant connections and operation were checked after components were disturbed. If another fault prevents the appropriate checks, have that limitation explained. Cleaning should not leave an operating issue hidden behind a freshly wiped exterior.

Keep the job details and any supplied-part information. If leakage or abnormal behaviour appears afterward, report when it occurs and keep an unsafe machine stopped. Do not repeat strong treatments or dismantle parts yourself to resolve an unexplained result.

Choose a maintenance routine for the actual machine

Use the manufacturer’s model-specific care instructions and adjust the discussion to usage and observed conditions. There is no universal paid deep-cleaning interval on this page. A reminder for a built-in cycle does not automatically mean a technician visit is required at the same frequency.

Routine care may include suitable attention to the areas the manual permits you to access. The technician should explain which tasks you can safely perform and when a persistent issue needs inspection. Avoid turning every maintenance reminder into an unnecessary parts-replacement recommendation.

If the same residue or odour returns quickly, ask what conditions may remain unresolved. More frequent paid cleaning is not the only possible answer; the relevant usage, drainage or other confirmed issue may need attention.

Prepare the booking without unsafe cleaning attempts

Provide the full model, locality and the reason for cleaning. Mention any previous chemical treatment, fault message, leak, trapped water or recent repair. Do not run an unsafe washer or open internal areas to take a photo.

Do not mix bleach, acids or different cleaners. Keep products according to their labels and mention what was already used. The appropriate method depends on the appliance and condition rather than the strength of an improvised solution.

Clear ordinary access safely, but leave heavy lifting, stacked-appliance handling and internal work to the appropriate team. Describe any access problem before the visit so its effect on scope can be discussed.

Booking charges for a cleaning-only visit

The visiting charge is ₹200 if no repair is carried out. Inspection charges depend on location. Share your Bangalore locality or PIN code and confirm all applicable charges together before booking. ₹200 is not an advertised complete repair or parts-replacement price.

Ask how the visiting and inspection charges are treated if a repair proceeds. No automatic waiver, deduction or additional charge should be assumed. Your approved quote should make clear whether these amounts are included, credited or payable separately, and state any applicable tax and the final payable total.

Cleaning is a paid service with its own agreed scope even when no fault repair is needed. Confirm how booking charges apply to the cleaning-only visit; the ₹200 no-repair visiting term should not be interpreted as replacing the price of a cleaning service actually completed.

If the machine’s condition prevents cleaning or requires a changed service, clarify the applicable charge and next step before continuing. No universal unsuccessful-cleaning or cancellation fee is invented here.

How warranty applies to a cleaning service

New parts supplied by FixMyWasher carry a one-year warranty. Ask for the supplied part to be identified on your job record or invoice. This applies to that new part; it does not turn the job into a one-year warranty on the entire washing machine.

Repairing an existing component is different from supplying a new one. Confirm the terms for repaired boards, retained parts, workmanship, labour, revisits and customer-supplied items separately. Do not infer those terms from the new-parts warranty.

Keep the quote, approved changes and completion record together. If the symptom returns, report what happened and when rather than assuming that the same part has failed. Stop using the washer if it leaks significantly, trips the circuit or develops a burning smell.

A cleaning service that supplies no new part does not automatically receive a one-year whole-machine or odour-free guarantee. Confirm the actual service terms separately. If a new part is independently required and approved, its identification and coverage should be recorded.

Frequently asked questions

How much does washing machine deep cleaning cost?

A defined service is estimated at ₹900–₹1,800 including the agreed cleaning work and normal labour. Model, condition, scope and separately applicable charges determine the actual quote.

Does deep cleaning always include complete drum removal?

No. Ask exactly what will be removed, cleaned and refitted. Extensive assembly work needs a separate suitable scope and quote.

Are cleaning products included?

Confirm whether all suitable required consumables are included in the package. Do not assume a headline price covers unspecified materials.

Is a drum-clean programme the same as professional deep cleaning?

Not necessarily. A built-in programme and a technician’s defined manual cleaning tasks can cover different work. Compare the actual scope.

Will cleaning fix a failed pump or heating element?

It should not be promised to repair a component assessed as defective. Operating faults require diagnosis and an appropriate separate repair decision.

Is descaling automatically included?

No. Ask whether it is suitable for the identified condition, what method is proposed and whether it is part of the stated package or separate.

Is deep cleaning required at a fixed interval for every machine?

No universal paid-service interval is stated. Follow the exact model’s care instructions and discuss actual usage and condition.

Does ₹200 cover a completed cleaning visit?

Do not interpret it that way. Confirm how booking charges apply and pay the agreed price for the cleaning service actually performed.

Does cleaning carry a one-year whole-machine warranty?

No. The confirmed one-year term applies to new parts supplied by FixMyWasher. Cleaning and other service terms require separate confirmation.

What should I ask before booking?

Ask for the included areas, dismantling limits, products, completion checks, known exclusions, booking charges and full payable amount. Mention operating faults or earlier chemical treatments.
