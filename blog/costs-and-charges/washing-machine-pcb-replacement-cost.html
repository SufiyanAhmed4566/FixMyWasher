Washing machine PCB replacement cost in Bangalore

A new washing machine PCB replacement is estimated at ₹3,500–₹12,000 including normal fitting, with some model-specific boards costing more. The exact brand, model, board version and included work determine the quote. This is a planning range for a new compatible board, not a fixed price for every electronic module.

Before approving replacement, establish which board is faulty, whether repair is a suitable alternative and what the installed price includes. This FixMyWasher guide focuses on selecting and quoting a replacement board accurately, including compatibility, setup where required, related faults and the one-year warranty on new parts we supply.

Unless a row says otherwise, repair estimates include the named part or repair work and normal fitting labour. They exclude separately confirmed visiting or location-based inspection charges, unrelated repairs, special access or transport, and any applicable tax not expressly included in your quote. Normal fitting does not silently guarantee every specialised setup service. If the board needs a model-specific procedure, ask for its inclusion to be stated and confirm that the proposed replacement can be made suitable for the appliance before ordering.

Identify the actual board before comparing prices

The term PCB can refer to a main control board, display or user-interface board, or a separate drive-control module where fitted. The quotation should name the component. A cheaper display board is not a like-for-like alternative to a main board if the diagnosis points to a different module.

Provide the full appliance model and let the technician identify the relevant board details safely. Similar product names or cabinet designs can conceal different electronics. A photo that looks right or connectors that appear to match do not establish full compatibility.

If a quote proposes two modules, ask why both are needed and whether they are supplied together or separately. The answer should describe the confirmed scope, not treat every electronic problem as a reason to renew all control components.

New, repaired and refurbished boards are different offers

The headline range on this page is for a new compatible board with normal fitting. A repaired original board, a used board taken from another machine and a refurbished board have different histories and should be described accordingly. Do not accept “replacement” as the only condition description.

If a seller describes a board as genuine, ask which manufacturer or source the term refers to and whether the supplied item supports that description. A compatible third-party component should not be represented as an original manufacturer part without basis. This website does not claim manufacturer-authorised status.

Compare the offered condition, compatibility, installation scope and warranty alongside the amount. A lower part-only price may be a different proposition from an identified new board supplied, fitted and checked as part of a defined repair.

Replacement should follow a supported diagnosis

No display, repeated restarting or an interrupted cycle can have more than one cause. The assessment should explain why the identified board needs replacement and what relevant conditions were checked. A message on the display is not a complete diagnosis of every electronic component.

Ask whether repair of the existing board is technically suitable and, if so, what its separate scope and terms would be. Replacement can be a reasonable recommendation, but the reasoning should be clearer than “electronics are expensive” or “this brand always needs a board”.

If assessment remains uncertain, agree the next step rather than authorising a speculative list of parts. A provisional estimate can help budgeting, but a final replacement approval should identify the board and what the proposed work is intended to resolve.

What setup and refitting can add to the scope

Some board replacements require model-specific configuration or another prescribed setup step. Whether this applies depends on the actual board and machine. Ask if the replacement is supplied ready for the model and what, if any, additional procedure is necessary.

The quote should state who completes the required setup, whether it is included and whether any limitation could prevent commissioning. A board should not be ordered on the assumption that every compatible-looking unit will work immediately after being plugged in.

Normal refitting should also include relevant connections and securing the component properly. Ask about the checks after installation and any disturbed areas that need attention. The job is not adequately described by a part purchase if required fitting or setup remains undefined.

Check the reason for any additional quoted component

A new board does not automatically correct every other fault in the appliance. If a related load, damaged connection, leak or another confirmed condition also needs correction, it should be separately identified. Ask why it is necessary and whether the machine can be safely tested without addressing it.

Do not assume that a new board, motor and sensor must be fitted together. Each item needs a supported reason. Where components are part of a larger supplied assembly, ask what is included so smaller items are not counted twice.

If an external electrical condition is suspected, ask what has actually been established and what specialist help is appropriate. Avoid attributing the failure to a specific event without evidence. The repair record should distinguish confirmed findings from possibilities.

Questions before ordering a model-specific board

Ask whether the required board has been identified and whether availability is confirmed or still being checked. A listed price range is not a stock statement. If timing matters, discuss it before authorising an order; no universal same-day supply or completion guarantee is made here.

If a deposit is requested, ask what it covers and what order, cancellation or return terms apply to that particular item. Do not assume a standard deposit percentage or universal return right from this guide. Keep the agreed terms and part description together.

Clarify whether a return-fitting visit is included and what happens if compatibility or setup remains unresolved. The proposed work should be reviewable before a part is ordered, rather than leaving essential questions until the technician arrives to fit it.

Illustrative PCB replacement quotations

Illustrative situations: These examples explain repair decisions. They are not records of actual FixMyWasher customer jobs.

A supplied-and-fitted new board

Suppose a new compatible main board with normal fitting is quoted at ₹6,500. That is the repair subtotal for the named package, not automatically ₹6,500 plus fitting again. Confirm any required setup, inspection treatment and tax before treating it as the complete payable amount.

A part-only quote compared with an installed quote

Suppose one offer lists a board alone at ₹5,200 and another lists the equivalent new board with normal fitting and stated checks at ₹6,500. The difference cannot be judged fairly until the first offer’s fitting, setup, booking charges and warranty are known. The example does not establish the price of those missing items.

A model-specific quote above the guide range

Suppose an identified specialised board is quoted at ₹13,500 including its stated fitting scope. Ask for the board description, availability and the reason for the higher amount. The guide range is not a hard ceiling, but exceeding it should be explained before approval.

Compare replacement with repairing the original board

If repair is suitable, compare its total, uncertainty, checks and warranty with the new-board option. The existing-board repair estimate is ₹1,800–₹4,500, but a cheaper repair is not automatically the best choice for every board condition. Ask what makes each option reasonable for this machine.

Do not add both estimates as a default package. If repair assessment has already been authorised, ask how that cost is treated when choosing replacement. No automatic assessment credit or free unsuccessful-repair policy is stated.

For a substantial quote, also consider the condition of the rest of the washer and any other confirmed fault. Replacing the board does not replace the motor, seals or mechanical assemblies. The decision should be based on the complete proposed repair, not an assumption that new electronics make the appliance new.

What to check in the completion explanation

Ask which functions relevant to the original complaint were checked after fitting and any required setup. A lit display shows only part of the operating picture. Depending on the symptom, the machine may need assessment of starting, filling, rotation, draining or another affected stage.

If a full relevant check cannot be completed, ask why and what remains to be done. Intermittent faults may require a clear description of test limits. Do not treat a limited demonstration as proof that all future electronic faults are impossible.

Keep the supplied board description, quote, approved changes and job record. If the symptom returns, report the sequence and any exact message. Avoid opening the panel or disconnecting the board yourself to investigate a warranty concern.

Visiting and inspection charges for PCB replacement

The visiting charge is ₹200 if no repair is carried out. Inspection charges depend on location. Share your Bangalore locality or PIN code and confirm all applicable charges together before booking. ₹200 is not an advertised complete repair or parts-replacement price.

Ask how the visiting and inspection charges are treated if a repair proceeds. No automatic waiver, deduction or additional charge should be assumed. Your approved quote should make clear whether these amounts are included, credited or payable separately, and state any applicable tax and the final payable total.

For a board requiring sourcing or off-site assessment, clarify any transport, deposit and return-fitting arrangements before approval. These are not automatically covered by the ₹200 no-repair visiting term.

One-year warranty on the new board supplied

New parts supplied by FixMyWasher carry a one-year warranty. Ask for the supplied part to be identified on your job record or invoice. This applies to that new part; it does not turn the job into a one-year warranty on the entire washing machine.

Repairing an existing component is different from supplying a new one. Confirm the terms for repaired boards, retained parts, workmanship, labour, revisits and customer-supplied items separately. Do not infer those terms from the new-parts warranty.

Keep the quote, approved changes and completion record together. If the symptom returns, report what happened and when rather than assuming that the same part has failed. Stop using the washer if it leaks significantly, trips the circuit or develops a burning smell.

Record the new PCB’s identification with the job details. If the quote involves a refurbished board or repair of the existing board instead, it is not the new-board scope described by this page; confirm the appropriate terms separately.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a new washing machine PCB replacement cost?

The indicative range is ₹3,500–₹12,000 including a new compatible board and normal fitting, with some model-specific boards costing more. Confirm required setup, separately applicable charges and the final total.

Is this the price for repairing the old board?

No. Suitable repair of the existing PCB is a different scope, estimated at ₹1,800–₹4,500. Repair and new-board replacement are usually alternatives.

Does the same brand always use the same PCB?

No. Exact models and versions can use different boards. The component must be identified for the actual appliance.

Can I use a board because its connectors look the same?

Appearance alone does not establish compatibility. Ask how model suitability and any configuration requirements have been confirmed.

Is programming included in normal fitting?

Do not assume it. Ask whether the actual board requires setup or programming and whether that work is available and included in the quote.

Is a refurbished board the same as a new board?

No. New, used, refurbished and repaired-original boards are different conditions. The quotation and warranty discussion should describe the supplied item accurately.

Can the replacement cost exceed ₹12,000?

Yes. Some model-specific boards or additional confirmed work cost more. Ask for the component description, reason and complete quote before approving it.

Do I need to replace the motor at the same time?

Not automatically. A separate motor or other component requires its own supported finding and approved scope.

What warranty comes with a new FixMyWasher PCB?

New parts supplied by FixMyWasher carry a one-year warranty. It applies to the identified board; labour, revisits, workmanship and retained machine components need separate confirmation.

Will the inspection charge be deducted from the replacement bill?

No automatic deduction is stated. Confirm whether visiting and location-based inspection amounts are included, credited or separate in your quotation.
